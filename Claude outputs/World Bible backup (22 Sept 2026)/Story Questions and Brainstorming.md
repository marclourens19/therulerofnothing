# Story Questions and Brainstorming

## Status Key

- **Established:** a foundational decision that should not be changed casually.
- **Working concept:** the current direction, but its mechanics or execution may still change.
- **Open question:** deliberately unresolved and available for later development.
- **Discarded:** an idea retained only so that it is not accidentally reintroduced.

---

## Story North Star

> Alaric matters because, despite being surrounded by ancient powers, predetermined souls and world-ending magic, he continues choosing what kind of person he will be.

Alaric must remain emotionally and practically human. His significance should not come from secret tactical mastery, effortless superiority or an inherited right to rule. Although impossible powers lie dormant within him, the person he becomes is determined by his choices.

Fate may bring Alaric and Seralune together, but fate cannot make them love one another. Their relationship matters because they continue choosing each other even after learning that the universe intended them to meet.

---

## Core Premise

The story follows an ordinary human boy who awakens one thousand years after his existence was erased from history. He has no memories of who he was or how he came to lie in the middle of an impossible battlefield.

Unknown to him, his soul was once meant to be one with the soul of Seralune, an elven princess born with infinite mana but no elemental『Affinity』. Alaric possesses almost no usable mana, but all four metaphysical affinities—Light, Dark, Time and Spirit—lie dormant and locked within him. Seralune has the mana required to wield those powers; Alaric carries the powers she cannot naturally possess.

The intended series ending is their freely chosen sacrifice to remove magic and『Affinity』from the living world. Their love for each other and their friends leads them to surrender the lives they wanted, so those friends and future children can choose lives no longer assigned a worth by magical strength. Removing magic does not automatically abolish prejudice, slavery or cruelty; the surviving world must still confront those institutions and beliefs.

After their deaths, Alaric and Seralune reunite in the Soul World as two distinct people, remembering those they left behind and finally able to love without endangering anyone.

**Status:** agreed emotional destination for the entire series, not Volume One. Both sacrifices and the reunion are intended; why the sacrifice is necessary, how magic is removed and how their identities survive remain open mechanics. See **Series Endgame — A World Without Magic** below.

## Creative Direction

### Genre and Tone

- Epic fantasy with mystery, horror, political conflict and intimate character drama.
- The story may contain blood, death, cruelty, slavery, sexuality and other mature subjects. These elements should serve character, atmosphere or consequence rather than exist solely for shock.
- The world should feel old, inhabited and independent of the protagonist.
- History should survive through incomplete records, propaganda, religious interpretations, contradictory witnesses and myths that contain fragments of truth.
- Alaric begins as a nobody in a world inhabited by ancient, immensely powerful people. His ability to change fate must come from persistence and moral choice rather than conventional dominance.

### Influences

- **Final Fantasy XIV:** interconnected history, sympathetic antagonists and conflicts whose roots extend far beyond the protagonist's lifetime.
- **Final Fantasy XV:** reference for the desired closeness of Alaric's travelling friends, their ordinary shared moments and the emotional weight of that companionship—not a plot or ending to reproduce.
- **Re:Zero:** sustained character pressure, painful consequences, mystery-led revelations and arc structures that repeatedly test the protagonist's values.
- **The Lord of the Rings:** grounded characters, a world that feels larger than the immediate plot and history that carries emotional weight.

These works are guides to emotional and structural qualities, not templates to reproduce.

### Villain Design

Seralune's eventual antagonism should remain emotionally understandable even when her actions become indefensible. The useful lesson from Emet-Selch is not to reproduce his goal, but to give the antagonist a loss, love or conviction powerful enough that the reader understands why she refuses to surrender it.

Research link: [What characteristics define a truly great villain?](https://www.reddit.com/r/fantasywriters/comments/1fdfa6a/what_characteristics_define_a_truly_great_villain/)

## Magic and『Affinity』

### The Eight Affinities

Every soul is born tethered to an『Affinity』. The strength of that connection varies enormously. Some people possess an affinity so faint that they cannot produce usable magic and instead live as farmers, craftsmen, soldiers, archers or other non-magical members of society.

Mana and『Affinity』are separate. Mana is the capacity or fuel to use magic;『Affinity』determines what form that magic can take. Possessing a powerful affinity without sufficient mana is as limiting as possessing vast mana without an affinity through which to express it.

The complete magical order consists of **Eight Affinities**, divided into two groups:

| Group | Affinities | Function | Distribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Common Four** | Fire, Water, Wind and Earth | Govern the physical forces from which ordinary civilisation is built. | Widespread, although strength varies. |
| **The Veiled Four** | Light, Dark, Time and Spirit | Govern metaphysical forces that act upon life, death, causality and the soul. | Normally only one living bearer of each affinity exists at a time. |

When a bearer of one of the Veiled Four dies, that affinity passes to a newly born soul. Under the natural order, there are never more than four recognised metaphysical bearers alive at once—one for Light, one for Dark, one for Time and one for Spirit.

Possible traditional formulation:

> The Four shaped what could be touched. The Four beyond them governed what could not. Together, they were the Eight.

**Working terminology:** “Common Four” and “Veiled Four.” Different cultures may use different names for the two groups.

### Known Roles of the Veiled Four

- **Light:** traditionally becomes a holy priest or priestess.
- **Spirit:** traditionally becomes the Hero, protector or bearer of the legendary spirit blade.
- **Dark:** role and cultural interpretation unresolved.
- **Time:** role and cultural interpretation unresolved.

These roles may be religious or political traditions rather than universal laws.

### Alaric's Exception

Alaric contains all four Veiled Affinities at once, dormant and locked within his soul. This should be impossible under the world's accepted natural order.

His affinities are undetectable, and the four recognised metaphysical bearers can apparently continue to exist while he carries them. Neither Alaric nor the world initially knows what is inside him.

**Open questions:**

- Why does the natural order permit—or fail to prevent—Alaric's existence?
- Are his affinities originals, duplicates, fragments or something more fundamental than those held by the recognised bearers?
- Why does he possess almost no usable mana?
- What unlocks each affinity, and what does using one cost him?
- Is the belief that only one bearer of each Veiled Affinity can exist completely true?

### Elemental Hierarchy

Affinity has shaped status, labour, warfare and racial prejudice. Some societies enslave people with particular affinities because their magic is economically or militarily useful. The system should affect ordinary life, not exist only for combat.

**Open questions:**

- Which kingdoms privilege or persecute particular affinities?
- How is affinity identified at birth?
- Can tests be falsified, misunderstood or manipulated?
- What work is associated with each affinity?
- How do effectively non-magical people survive within affinity-based hierarchies?

## Souls and the Soul World

### Established Metaphysics

- A soul is the person without the body.
- After death, a soul passes into the Soul World.
- Souls are eventually used to bring new people into the living world.
- Souls can be separated under exceptional circumstances, particularly in matters involving the Veiled Four.
- Souls can be damaged, erased or destroyed.
- Ordinary reincarnation does not necessarily preserve conscious identity or accessible memories.

### Open Questions

- Why must every ordinary soul be tethered to an affinity?
- Who or what governs the movement of souls?
- Is the Soul World a place, a state of existence or both?
- How long does a soul remain there?
- What determines the soul's next life?
- What is the difference between destroying a soul and erasing a person's existence from history?
- Can a damaged soul heal?
- What happens to an affinity when its soul is destroyed rather than naturally returned?

## Mydean Belief — The Eight, the Turning and the Last Dark

These are widespread Mydean beliefs, not necessarily an accurate account of the universe. Different kingdoms and peoples should possess their own explanations of magic, death and the soul.

### The Eight

Mydean religion recognises all Eight Affinities as the complete order of existence. The Common Four are familiar and invoked in everyday life; the Veiled Four are treated with greater solemnity, fear or reverence because their bearers are unique and rarely encountered.

Ordinary people commonly speak of **the Four** when they mean Fire, Water, Wind and Earth. Farmers, soldiers and craftsmen appeal to the powers that shape the tangible world around them. References to **the Eight** belong more naturally to temples, royal vows, funerals, sacred law and moments of profound awe.

This distinction allows both expressions to coexist:

- **“By the Four”** feels immediate, human and commonplace.
- **“By the Eight”** feels formal, sacred and unusually serious.

### The Turning

The Turning is the common belief that the dead pass through an unseen realm before their souls return to the world in another life. Ordinary Mydeans do not know the exact nature of the Soul World. Religious teachings about the Turning may therefore contain both truth and error.

### The Last Dark

The Last Dark is the feared state of a soul cast outside the Turning, unable ever to return. Whether it truly exists is unknown to ordinary Mydeans. The belief unknowingly echoes the real possibility that a soul can be destroyed or erased.

### Common Mydean Expressions

- **“By the Four”** — a mild oath expressing surprise, disbelief or alarm.
- **“Four preserve us”** — a frightened appeal for protection.
- **“By the Eight”** — a solemn invocation, uncommon in casual speech.
- **“Before the Eight”** — language suitable for a sacred, royal or funerary vow.
- **“The Last Dark take you”** — a severe curse.
- **“What in the Last Dark…”** — an expression of anger or bewilderment.

These expressions should appear naturally and sparingly. Natharul and other cultures should have distinct beliefs, rituals and oaths rather than borrowing Mydean language.

## Alaric and Seralune

### The Connection

Alaric and Seralune were meant to possess a single soul. Through an error in creation, an act of God or some other cosmic fracture, that soul entered the world as two people.

Their connection cannot be completely severed. Fate repeatedly draws them towards each other, but it does not create their love or absolve them of choice. Love is not a spell, resource or automatic consequence of their souls. It is the repeated act of choosing each other when separation, fear and the world itself give them reasons not to.

When their souls willingly align, Seralune's infinite mana can flow through Alaric's Veiled Affinities. Separately, they initially appear incomplete: she has limitless power with no element through which to express it, while he carries impossible elements without the mana needed to wield them.

**Working power concept:** through their union, Seralune may wield Light, Dark, Time and Spirit upon herself and the world. Time may prevent or reverse damage to her body, making her almost impossible to overcome. The limitations, costs and exact mechanics still require development.

### Open Questions About the Connection

- What caused one soul to become two?
- Was the division truly an error, or was it intended by something beyond the known gods?
- Does fate cause their meetings, merely predict them or actively resist alternatives?
- If the connection cannot be broken, what can still be chosen?
- What happens when one of them dies?
- Why might their memories survive death when ordinary memories do not?
- Does the surviving half remain incomplete, unstable or changed?
- What does willing union require beyond romantic love?
- Can either person refuse the connection permanently?

### Seralune

- Born an elven princess to the king and queen of Natharul.
- Born with infinite, inexhaustible mana but no natural elemental affinity.
- Raised under the duties, restrictions and expectations of high-elven society.
- Initially appears magically incomplete despite possessing the greatest mana reserve in history.
- Eventually becomes an antagonist or villain, but remains a co-protagonist whose perspective and choices matter.

**Open questions:**

- Why is her mana infinite?
- Why did her half of the divided soul receive mana while Alaric's received the Veiled Affinities?
- What was she like before grief and history transformed her?
- What does Natharul expect her to become?
- What does she want before Alaric enters her life?
- At what point does her desire become morally indefensible?
- What does she believe she is saving or restoring?

### Alaric

- Born an ordinary human boy to an ordinary, loving family.
- Possesses almost no usable mana.
- Carries all four dormant Veiled Affinities without knowing it.
- Awakens one thousand years later without his name, history or memories.
- Matters because of his decisions, not because hidden power makes him inherently better than other people.

**Open questions:**

- Why was he born with so little mana?
- Why can no test detect what lies within his soul?
- What was daily life like with his commoner family and later in the queen's household? His mother's fate and the terms of his wardship remain open.
- Why did Seralune love him as a person rather than as the missing half of her power?
- What did he choose during the ancient crisis?
- Why was his existence dangerous enough for the ancient world to erase?
- What remains of the first Alaric within the boy who awakens?

## The Ancient Sin

### Current Direction

One thousand years ago, ancient powers discovered the connection between Alaric and Seralune. Unable to overcome Seralune while their souls were united, they targeted the person she loved most.

They cast the most powerful forbidden spell in existence upon Alaric. Its intended purpose may have been to erase his soul, remove him from history or sever him from Seralune so completely that their union could never recur. Instead, Alaric was displaced one thousand years into the future without his memories. The wider world forgot or misunderstood that he had ever existed.

This is the central historical mystery. Its first apparent explanation should not be the complete truth.

### Questions That Must Be Answered

- What exactly did the ancient people discover about the two souls?
- How did they discover it?
- How many people knew the truth?
- Why could they not simply separate Alaric and Seralune?
- Why could they not kill Seralune?
- Why would killing Alaric normally fail?
- Who created the forbidden spell?
- What did its creators intend it to do?
- What did the spell actually do?
- Did they know it might destroy Alaric's soul?
- Why did it send him precisely one thousand years forward?
- Why did he lose his memories?
- What happened to Seralune immediately afterward?
- Did losing Alaric weaken her, enrage her or alter her memories?
- What did the world believe happened?
- Who benefited from the accepted version of history?
- What evidence survived?
- **What part of the ancient story is completely wrong?**

That final question is essential. A thousand years of distance creates room for myth, propaganda, destroyed records, mistranslation and sincere but contradictory beliefs. The world must have a history independent of Alaric, and the truth should be messier than any surviving account.

### Logical Pressure to Resolve

Removing Seralune's lover might make her more dangerous rather than weaker. The ancient conspirators therefore need a convincing reason to believe targeting Alaric would neutralise her—or a motive that made them accept the risk.

Possible directions:

- Their union, not grief, was the source of her invulnerability.
- The spell was intended to erase both Alaric and Seralune through their connection, but only partly succeeded.
- The conspirators misunderstood how their bond worked.
- Someone involved wanted Seralune to survive for a hidden purpose.
- The public explanation of the event was deliberately fabricated.

These are possibilities, not established answers.

## Series Endgame — A World Without Magic

**Author-only direction, agreed 31 August 2026. This is the intended conclusion of the entire series, not the ending of Volume One or the current arc.** The emotional destination is agreed; the intervening plot, magical mechanism and exact final scene remain to be designed.

### Friendship as the Foundation

Alaric, Wena, Redd and Silas develop into an exceptionally close chosen family. Their bond must be earned through shared work, care, arguments, private jokes, ordinary pleasures and repeated decisions to remain beside one another. Readers should want Alaric to return home to these companions, not merely admire their usefulness in an adventure.

Seralune's path carries greater tragedy, with Nereth as her core companion and emotional support. Their relationship also needs genuine joy, disagreement and mutual care. Nereth is a person with her own future, not a device for increasing Seralune's suffering. Destruction arising from Seralune's condition and her connection with Alaric must not make every act of compassion or happiness a punishment.

### Love, Isolation and Responsibility

Alaric and Seralune eventually travel together, rediscover their shared past and fall in love through their present choices. Their memories and unique connection can draw them away from everyone else. That isolation is a dangerous narrowing of their lives, not proof that their friends matter less once the romance begins. Their companions must retain agency and help shape the final choice; their exact contributions are not yet fixed.

The world has told Alaric and Seralune that they cannot be together. They choose each other despite it, while learning to take responsibility for the harm their uncontrolled power and decisions can cause. Their final act grows from love extending beyond the couple, not from deciding that only the two of them deserve happiness.

### Intended Sacrifice and Final Emotional Image

They love their friends too deeply to accept a world that assigns those friends—or anyone else—a right to freedom according to magical strength. They recognise the same claim of ownership beneath the restrictions upon their own love: someone else believes birth grants the authority to decide what another person's life may be.

Together, they sacrifice their lives to remove magic and『Affinity』from the living world. They still desperately want to live together among their friends. Losing that possible future is the cost, not something made irrelevant by the prospect of an afterlife.

Their purpose is to let their friends choose their own lives and prevent future children from being assigned a worth through magical evaluation. The ending should pay off both the preliminary indication at birth and the binding assessment at ten. People were always equally valuable; magical strength never made anyone more deserving of personhood.

The surviving companions have futures beyond mourning. The intended final emotional image joins their hard-won freedom with Alaric and Seralune's quiet reunion in the Soul World. The two remain distinct individuals, remember those they loved and can finally be close without anyone being endangered. The exact scene and ordering of these images remain open.

### Guardrails

- Their deaths do not prove that forbidding their love was right. They reject imposed ownership while accepting responsibility for dangerous power and harmful choices.
- Sacrifice does not erase past victims or require universal forgiveness. Seralune's existing antagonist direction can lead into this ending, but her change of course must be earned.
- Removing magical advantage does not automatically eliminate racism, wealth inequality, slavery or cruelty. Reform and rebuilding remain real work for survivors.
- Friends are participants in the story's resolution, not merely an audience for the couple's deaths. Do not replace their relationships with the romance or fix their individual fates prematurely.
- The afterlife reunion must follow established metaphysics, not undo the loss of the lives and shared future surrendered in the living world.

### Mechanics and Decisions Still Open

- Why can only their combined power accomplish this, and why must both die rather than simply give up their abilities?
- How can magic and inherited Affinity be removed without destroying ordinary life, physical time, souls or the Soul World? Severing the binding between souls and magical power is a possible mechanism, not established canon.
- How do their two identities and memories persist through the Soul World's existing reincarnation process, and what do they know about that possibility before choosing the sacrifice?
- How are people, creatures and societies dependent upon magic protected during the transition, including Nereth's unresolved corruption?
- What decisive roles do their friends play, and how are affected people's choices represented in a change imposed upon the whole world?

## Discarded or Superseded Ideas

- **A world-ending event that simply repeats every one thousand years:** discarded. The millennium gap remains important, but it should not become a repetitive apocalypse cycle.
- **Alaric as secretly tactically gifted from the beginning:** discarded. He may learn and grow, but Chapter 1 promises an ordinary, frightened and morally decent person—not a hidden combat prodigy.
- **Love as an automatic magical command:** superseded. Fate and the divided soul create connection and potential; love remains a choice.
