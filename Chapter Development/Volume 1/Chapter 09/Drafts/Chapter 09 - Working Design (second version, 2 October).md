## Current author decisions

He can move around Kelmend and gather information, subject to his continuing injury. No separate Silas viewpoint is selected; Alaric learns about his work through later contact or things he can observe.
- Chapter 9 ends on the arrival night with Alaric's first sustained double encounter. The two recovery days belong in the next Alaric chapter, after Seralune's Chapter 10. This chapter break is now approved.

## Recommendations for the arrival night

### The reopened door and Gerolt's death

The familiar words, sword and dog together make Marta hear Alaric. The words alone do not prove that Gerolt is dead or function as an infallible password.

Compress the full escape account, but keep the questions and answers that change Marta's understanding. She may care where her uncle is, what he said, how badly he was hurt and when Silas arrived. Choose the questions that belong to her; do not require a checklist of death details. The reader should experience the death anew through his family's response.

Alaric can explain the help he witnessed and disagree with Marta's interpretation of Silas. He cannot certify Silas's motives, explain an old marriage or absolve the gorge. Marta can retain her anger without being incapable of hearing facts.

### The inn as a household

Introduce the resident women through individual work, wants and judgments. One can handle a guest's immediate needs while another wants quiet near her rooms. Who gathers information, what she knows and what she chooses to pass on should differ.

Place the lighter teasing after the first grief and the immediate guard visit, or during the following day. A small awkward exchange can show Alaric is still capable of reacting to ordinary attention. It should not make the death announcement feel like a prelude to universal admiration.

The proximity to Chapter 8's auction makes the women's approved freedom significant. Show it through choices and disagreements rather than a speech explaining the difference. Payment for sex and payment for intelligence are separate agreements. Marta should hear their objections to sheltering Alaric instead of the scene treating loyalty as the absence of judgment.

### The guard visit

Marta's familiarity and offer of drinks can influence how the men conduct a completed interview. They need something plausible to report, and no direct sighting of Alaric, Wena or Gerolt's sword. Alaric should recognise the two voices from Chapter 8.

Do not have the guards abandon an explicit room-search order or an obvious clue solely because she is beautiful. The current order is to question her; the extent of their authority and instructions needs a choice. There is no established need for a new legal warrant system or magical charm ability.

Her information network can make maintaining a relationship with her useful to the guards. It also creates dependencies and vulnerabilities; it is not immunity from the elves or a later determined search. A bounded success tonight is enough.

### Rest and the double

The accepted working plan places Alaric's first sustained double encounter after genuine help from Marta. He first needs an ordinary reflection in which he can learn his face, naturally while washing. The author's latest direction makes this first encounter a private false-awakening vision: the inn and city seem destroyed before he truly wakes in bed. It exploits guilt about Gerolt and challenges the safety he has been offered. It does not deliver Darcy's intelligence or take over his choices. Its accusation is not evidence of Alaric's genuine past or a reliable prediction of the future.

The author approved the first-night ending, with the recovery interval shown when Alaric's viewpoint next returns after Seralune's Chapter 10. Do not squeeze two recovery days or the Darcy lead into Chapter 9's closing summary. The exact final line remains to be selected; "I'm fine" is still a proposal.

## Recovery and the next lead

Arrival is late on Day 2. Two full recovery days are Days 3 and 4. A new action can begin at the end of Day 4 or on Day 5; the exact departure remains unset. The interleaved Seralune chapters need clear temporal transitions.

Choose a few consequential recovery scenes rather than narrating every meal and hour. Let Alaric experience ordinary welcome and begin to settle, while the city's inquiry and both injuries persist. Any unusually rapid recovery needs to follow the established developing connection rather than become a convenient reset.

The Darcy opportunity should arise from fresh information, newly completed verification or a newly available contact. Explain what makes her useful regarding the battlefield and what makes the opportunity temporary when designing that chapter. Marta should not hold a known urgent lead for two days and then demand haste. The author has deferred these details; do not ask for them again to complete Chapter 9. A report that Darcy is being moved remains a proposal, not a selected event.

Silas pays for and arranges his own lodging. Marta's instruction to stay outside does not become permission to enter later. The search is not for him, so he can roam and collect information. The eventual return to Alaric needs a simple means of contact or an agreed meeting; its exact arrangement can arise naturally. Marta's later recommendation of his help need not mean forgiveness.

Alaric asking Silas for help is a small step towards trusting others. It does not complete the Volume 1 arc in which he later begins carrying responsibility alone. If the double has already urged withdrawal, asking for help can also be an early act of resistance.

## Latest author ending direction and craft recommendations

The author prefers the following ending movement:

1. An apparent awakening in the middle of the night: Alaric feels uneasy.
2. Blood runs under his door.
3. He opens it and finds a resident woman dead. He recoils and vomits.
4. He goes downstairs and finds Marta dead, with Wena beside her and blood around them.
5. He goes outside and sees the town apparently burning, bodies in the streets and people screaming or crying.
6. His own-faced double stands there and accuses him: wording along the lines of "This is who you are, Alaric."
7. Alaric truly wakes in the inn, badly sweating. The slaughter was a vision experienced as a dream.

This changes the earlier manifestation rule that the first encounter happens while the surrounding waking scene continues. The author may choose dream encounters and waking encounters separately; do not imply that every later appearance is now an ordinary dream. The world-created image remains the existing intended underlying mechanism, while Alaric and the reader lack proof of that mechanism at this point.

Recommendations awaiting selection:

- Give his movement a goal: after the first body he seeks Marta; after finding her he seeks help outside. Do not make the passage a tour of progressively larger horrors.
- Identify the dead resident through a genuine interaction earlier this chapter, not an anonymous corpse added for impact. Use one familiar detail rather than a catalogue of wounds.
- Keep Wena alive in the vision, trying to rouse Marta, as a possible deliberate echo of her response to Gerolt. This is a recommendation; her exact behaviour and condition remain unselected.
- Render the burning town through the streets and landmarks Alaric actually encounters, not an omniscient account of every district. Keep this expansion shorter than the intimate discovery inside the inn.
- The double's accusation frames help as danger and presses him towards finding his past. It does not objectively certify guilt, possession or foreknowledge.
- Let the real awakening leave one concrete consequence: concealment, checking the household, distrust of his own face or intensified pursuit of answers. Choose one immediate action rather than all of them. Sweat alone should not carry the whole aftermath.
- Do not transfer actual blood, moved objects or physical deaths from the vision into the waking room. Such a transfer would be a new power and contradict the proposed unreal slaughter.
- Keep the genuine reaching-hand and name memories distinct from this manipulated scene; the change does not retroactively make those memories false.
