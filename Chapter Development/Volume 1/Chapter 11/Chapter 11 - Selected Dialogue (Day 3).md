# Chapter 11 — Selected Dialogue: Day 3

**10 October 2026. Revised from the author's selections.** These are selected exchanges and proposed refinements, not the continuous chapter.

The author selected **1A, 2A, 3A, 4A, 5B, 6A and 8A** as the directions to keep. The new wording below addresses the requested corrections; selection of a direction does not automatically approve every new line.

**Section 7 is removed. There is no sword training in Chapter 11.** Alaric asks here; actual training begins in the Great Expanse. This supersedes the earlier proposed lesson, practice prop and lesson location.

Use **Gerolt's farm**. Alaric does not learn Brand's name until much later. Brand remains an author-facing name in headings; the chapter's prose and dialogue keep him unnamed. Silas identifies him only as one of the Rulers.

The carter remains a proposed source. The twins are sold separately, and the second sale is still in progress when they leave. Brand notices neither Alaric nor Silas. Silas remains outside Marta's house on Day 3. The guards' promised drinks and the Day 4 events remain part of the full design.

## 1A. Breakfast — kept as selected

“Marta—do you know anyone who might recognise those machines? The ones on Gerolt's farm?”

She set a bowl in front of him. A man at the far table called her name, and she raised a hand to him.

“Tonight, lad. Give me till tonight.”

“I could go and ask them myself. I don't need you to come with me. If you could just tell me who—”

“And if I knew who, I'd tell ye.” She turned towards the table. “I'm coming! Ye won't starve in the next bloody minute.”

Alaric drew back from the counter. Marta looked at him again.

“That wasn't meant for you. Sit down and eat. I'll have a think when this lot give me a moment.”

“All right.” He looked towards the stairs. “What about Wena? She's still shut in the room.”

“I'll ask Rhose to take her out the back. She stays off the street, though. I'm not having her seen by those guards.”

“Thank you. I didn't mean to make more work for you.”

“Aye, well, ye can spare me reheating that bowl. Get it into ye.”

## 2A. Outside — a concrete reason to accompany Silas

“She let you stay?” Silas asked, taking the folded cloak.

“Yes. She said we'd talk tonight.”

Silas shook it out with his good hand. Alaric watched him draw it over his shoulders.

“Why didn't you tell me she was your wife?”

“Because getting you through that door was enough trouble.”

“I had to hear it from Marta.”

“Aye. And now you've heard it, leave it alone.”

Alaric looked away. Silas pulled the cloak clear of his bandage and started towards the street.

“Have you heard anything about Gerolt's farm?” Alaric asked, following him.

“More guards heading out there this morning. And there are still elves in town.”

“Are they bringing anything back? The machines, or—”

“That's what I'm trying to find out. Everybody's got a fucking story. I'd like to hear one from someone who's actually been there.”

“Who are you going to ask?”

“A man who hauls for the fort. He might know what they've been bringing in.”

“Could I come with you? Marta hasn't time to talk until tonight. If they've taken one of those machines into town, I want to know where it is.”

Silas looked down at his ankle.

“You able to walk?”

“Much better than last night.”

“Then come on. If that foot starts giving out, you tell me. Don't wait till we're halfway across Kelmend.”

**Why this changes:** Alaric wants the location of something he can investigate. He does not claim that witnessing the aftermath qualifies him to check current hauling reports. The ogre reprimand is removed.

## 3A. The source — clear speakers and a useful warning

Silas stopped before they entered the yard.

“I'm going to speak to the man by that cart. Stay where you can see the entrance. If guards turn in here, come and tell me.”

“What does he know?” Alaric asked.

“He hauls for the fort,” Silas said. “If they've brought anything back from Gerolt's farm, he may have handled it.”

Alaric looked towards the man.

“Are they looking for him, too?”

“Not that I know of. And I'd rather not leave him with guards asking what he was telling us.” Silas nodded towards the entrance. “Quietly, Alaric. Don't shout across the yard.”

Alaric moved to where he could see the street. Silas went to the carter.

Later, two guards crossed from the other side and turned towards the yard. Alaric walked over to Silas and spoke close beside him.

“Silas. There are two coming in.”

Silas looked past him, then turned back to the carter.

“We'll get out of your way. Thanks.”

They left before the guards reached the cart. Alaric waited until they were in the next street before speaking.

“I wasn't sure whether to tell you sooner. They stopped on the other side first.”

“You told me when they turned in,” Silas said. “That's what I asked.” He glanced back towards the yard. “Good. Another minute and they'd have been standing beside us.”

**Continuous-scene requirement:** the carter's conversation goes between the instruction and the warning. Design his information from what he actually handles or witnesses; do not give him the ancient battle's explanation.

## 4A. The auction — Alaric asks for instruction, not a lesson today

“They were holding on to each other.” Alaric watched the buyer push through the crowd. “He saw that, Silas.”

“He saw.”

“Then how can he just—” Alaric turned back towards the platform. “How can everyone stand here and let him?”

“He's paid,” Silas said. “As far as those guards are concerned, he's done nothing wrong.”

“But they're children.”

Silas's jaw tightened.

“I can fucking see that.”

The auctioneer called the second child's price again. Alaric watched the child standing alone on the platform.

“Silas, please. Teach me how to fight.”

Alaric caught his sleeve.

“I can't stand here and listen to them calling a price for that child. They've already taken one away, and now—” His voice caught. “I need to be able to stop this. Please. Show me how.”

Silas looked at him, then towards the guards beside the platform.

“Not in Kelmend. I've enough shit to deal with already.”

“Then when?”

“Aye, well, ask me again when we're clear of this place. And there's time.”

Alaric looked back towards the child.

“I will.”

**Timing:** this is a request and a reluctant future possibility. No demonstration, grip instruction, practice session or promise to start today follows. Actual training begins in the Great Expanse.

## 5B. Brand passes — delight and an urgent withdrawal

“I'd rather whatever did it was miles away by now,” one of the guards said.

The young man beside him laughed.

“Don't say that. I've only just arrived.”

“There are thousands dead out there, sir.”

“Oh, I know. Thousands, and no one heard a thing.” A broad grin spread across his face, showing his teeth. “I'd have liked to see that. I hope whatever did it is still here.”

Silas's good hand closed hard round Alaric's arm.

“Move, Alaric. *Now.*”

Alaric turned towards him.

“Silas, what—”

“Don't look back. Just fucking move.”

His voice was low, close to Alaric's ear. Alaric had seen him face the ogre, but he had never seen his face like this. He went with him, hurried along by the hand on his arm.

The auctioneer's voice followed them down the street, still calling the second child's price.

**Staging:** Silas reacts as soon as he recognises the man. He is frightened enough to pull Alaric away immediately. His commands stay low so the withdrawal does not contradict Brand noticing neither of them. The young man's pleasure is in the reported deaths and the possibility of encountering what caused them. The cause of the battle remains unconfirmed.

## 6A. Out of earshot — the warning, without the closing reply

“Who was that?” Alaric asked.

“One of the Rulers.” Silas looked back towards the street. “Keep well clear of him and his lot, unless you want an early death, boy.”

“But he looks so young. He can't be much older than me.” Alaric hesitated. “He didn't look that dangerous.”

Silas stopped and turned to him.

“Listen, Alaric. You haven't the faintest idea what people like him can do. It's best he never even sees us. If he decides we're his enemies, we might as well cut our own fucking throats and get it over with.”

Alaric fell quiet.

**Removed:** “All right. Let's go.” Silas is already taking him back to the inn. The continuous prose carries their movement without making Alaric announce it again. Brand's name and domain remain undisclosed.

## 7. Removed — no sword training in this chapter

Do not replace the rejected lesson with stick practice, advice on his grip, a demonstration or training disguised as a quieter scene.

The useful warning and Alaric's prompt departure already give Silas evidence of his ability to cooperate. The fort discussion can later establish a limited operational responsibility without granting combat competence. The evening with Marta follows their return and the day's remaining business.

## 8A. Evening — Wena and a memory they can share

Wena pushed her nose towards the edge of the table. Marta drew her bowl away.

“Oh, don't start. Ye've had yours.”

Alaric smiled a little.

“She did that at Gerolt's. She was under the bed when I woke up. I didn't even know she was there until she made a noise.”

“Under the bed?” Marta looked down at her. “Oh, she'd come out soon enough once he brought dinner.”

“Only her nose at first. He called her brave then.”

Marta gave a small laugh.

“He was always on at me for feeding her. Then he'd sit there slipping her bits off his own plate, like I couldn't bloody see him.”

Alaric looked towards her bowl.

“He held mine for me. Until I had it properly. I think he thought I was going to drop it.”

“Ye probably were.” She rubbed at her eyes. “He'd have wanted to be sure ye'd eaten.”

Wena came forward when Marta lowered her hand. Marta set the spoon down and scratched beneath her jaw.

“Come here, love.”

Alaric left his spoon beside his bowl. For a while they sat listening to Wena breathing beneath the table.

**Boundaries:** Gerolt holding the bowl, Wena under the bed and his “brave” remark come from Chapter 1. Marta's dog-feeding recollection is a proposed ordinary memory, not an approved new history. The full evening also needs Alaric's report of his day and a return to his investigation; this is one part of that conversation.
