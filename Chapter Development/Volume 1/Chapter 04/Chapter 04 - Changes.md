#### 2. Alive, and freezing

*Draft line 23 → revised line 23*

**Before**

> *So I'm alive, and lying on a stone floor I don't know, somewhere so black I can't see my own hand. Very well. Before I do anything else, I need to find out where I am.*

**After**

#### 3. Her voice comes back

*Draft line 31 → revised line 31*

**Before**

**After**

**Why.** Claude's reading: plainer. "The room was big, then, bigger than any of hers" becomes "The chamber was larger than any room she knew", and "there was nobody in it to answer" becomes "no one answered".
