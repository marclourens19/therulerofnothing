### 11. The creature; "Leave it"; the bite

#### 93. The one oath, in Natharul's words

*changed* · your answer 6 (5 October), corrected against the world bible

**Before**

> "Make them stop." Nereth's face was a hand's width from hers, lit from below by the flowers, white and wet. "Make them stop. Make them *stop*—"

**After**

> "Make them stop." Nereth's face was a hand's width from hers, lit from below by the flowers, white and wet. "Make them stop, you rootless thing. Make them *stop*—"

**Why:** You took the recommendation of one oath during the strangling, "The Last Dark take you". But the Last Dark belongs to Mydea's religion. The world bible keeps Natharul's own oaths, and Chapter 7's draft comparison took the same phrase out of a Natharul laundry worker's mouth. Natharul's worst insult in the world bible is "Rootless": a traitor, an exile, someone with no house. Seralune has just run from hers, and the roots are all round them.

#### 94. The bite spreads towards her temple

*changed* · your answer 8 (5 October)

**Before**

> The bite was a small thing, a ragged crescent below her eye. But lines were spreading out from it under the skin, dark and branching, the way the roots had branched over the stones, up into the corner of her eye and down her cheek towards her mouth, and Seralune could see them grow.

**After**

> The bite was a small thing, a ragged crescent below her eye. But lines were spreading out from it under the skin, dark and branching, the way the roots had branched over the stones, up past the corner of her eye towards her temple and into her hair, and Seralune could see them grow.

**Why:** The marks spread towards her temple and into her hair, not towards her mouth. By the light of the flowers they still look only dark; their colour comes in daylight (change 95).
