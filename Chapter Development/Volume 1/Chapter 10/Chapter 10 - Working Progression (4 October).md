## The experience the chapter should create

Seralune begins with someone who answers her and helps her walk. Nereth grows irritated, then increasingly difficult to reach. After the choice of the right tunnel, she stops answering altogether. Seralune must guide a woman who still follows her but seems less and less aware of where they are. The eventual attack breaks that long silence.

After the discharge, their roles have reversed completely. Nereth cannot lead or even walk; Seralune must drag her towards daylight, see what the spell has done, and find a way towards the port below.

## Continuity to preserve

- Close third person through Seralune. Her mind remains clear; only Nereth is affected by the contamination.
- Nereth knows the initial stretch to the first turning, not the entire aqueduct network.
- The circles result from Nereth's impaired judgement and repeated wrong choices. Moving tunnels are not established by this chapter.
- Nereth begins with the lamp in her left hand and Seralune's good left hand in her right.
- Seralune's right palm remains cut and bound. She wears borrowed grey clothes, a shawl and borrowed shoes.
- Neither woman knows Cyrandor's fate. Seralune's hopes about Thaer are not proof that Cyrandor is safe.
- The author knows the contamination comes from Seralune's mana. Neither woman understands this during the chapter.
- Contact blocks formed spells, but does not protect Nereth from contaminated ambient mana, poison or ordinary physical harm.
- The copied pleas from rooted corpses are real sounds Seralune can hear, distinct from Nereth's altered judgement.
- There is one strangling attempt, at the creature encounter.
- The creature was made from Seralune's mana and defends her as author knowledge. Seralune sees it intervene without discovering its origin or acquiring command over fauna.
- Its bite lands beneath Nereth's left eye. The wound and dark marks remain after her mind clears.
- The vast Fire discharge empties Nereth's mana and opens the mountain towards the coast. It does not give her a permanent increase in rank.
- Seralune holds Nereth before the returning formed fire reaches them. Ordinary collapsing stone remains dangerous.
- The port is visible below the mountain and becomes their immediate destination. Its name and detailed geography are not invented here.
- Preserve the prior decision that this discharge does not kill living people aboveground. Recommended staging: the breach and scar run across an uninhabited slope, with the port visibly off to one side. Seralune cannot certify the safety of every distant person.
- The prior timing is entry late morning on Day 2 and escape towards dawn on Day 3. Walking, pauses and a failed rest need to supply that duration. Exact hours are not known to Seralune.

## 1. Enter the aqueducts with Nereth still recognisable

Continue beyond the arch from Chapter 7. Establish the wall, the ledge, the dark water and the small area the lamp reveals. Seralune notices she cannot hear the falls.

Nereth gives a useful warning about Seralune's cut palm or unstable footing. She remains precise and capable. This matters: the reader needs to feel what is being lost later.

**Change:** the palace is behind them, and Seralune depends on Nereth's help in an unfamiliar place.

## 2. Seralune tries to keep a conversation going

Seralune asks about Cyrandor while they walk. Her intention is to make the silence easier, reassure herself or find a familiar person they can discuss. It need not mean she regards his disappearance as a cheerful subject.

Start with a small question about the relationship she has noticed, or whether he will be all right. Nereth answers briefly, pauses or asks not to discuss it. Her initial discomfort is understandable grief.

Seralune should recognise that she has hurt her and change approach. Later irritation can occur when she offers help or asks about the way; she need not keep reopening Cyrandor after being asked to stop.

**Change:** the attempt at closeness does not relieve the darkness, and Seralune becomes uncertain how to speak to her.

## 3. Choose the initial route for a reason

At the first fork, Nereth recalls the left-hand side from the limited maintenance work she knows. They try it. The right-hand tunnel remains visibly available but untried.

Neither promises an exit. Show them making an ordinary decision with incomplete information.

Give the walking room to exist. Nereth still finds safe footing or helps Seralune over a bad section. Do not turn the first few pages into a list of symptoms.

**Change:** they move beyond Nereth's reliable knowledge.

## 4. Irritation appears between useful actions

An ordinary question receives an unexpectedly sharp answer. Nereth takes Seralune's offer to carry the lamp as criticism, or becomes impatient when asked to slow down.

Keep some familiar competence alongside this. She can still notice the injured hand immediately after speaking harshly. Seralune initially explains the change through fatigue, grief and the difficulties of the route.

Use fewer, increasingly uncomfortable exchanges. Nereth should not become a source of constant sarcastic speeches.

**Change:** speaking to Nereth begins to require care.

## 5. Verify that they are walking in circles

The first return has an innocent explanation: a branch brought them round. They mark a junction and try a correction.

The second return reaches that same deliberate mark. Give the reader something physical to recognise; do not rely on an announcement that they are lost.

Nereth is less able to recount the turns and more aggravated by Seralune's correction. They have evidence that the chosen route keeps failing, but not knowledge of the mana's cause.

**Change:** Seralune can no longer simply follow the person holding the lamp.

## 6. Seralune suggests the right tunnel and takes the lead

Return them to the established fork. Seralune proposes the route they have not tried: the right-hand tunnel.

A possible spoken direction is: "We keep coming back here. Let me take the lamp, Nereth. I want to try the other way." The exact wording remains for review.

Nereth may make one tired objection or hand over the lamp without answering. Seralune takes it in her good left hand.

This is a reasonable choice from the available evidence. She cannot know it takes them into stronger contamination. The story should not imply that she knowingly endangers Nereth.

**Change:** Seralune leads, and the worsening exposure now makes Nereth progressively harder to reach.

## 7. The right tunnel: movement becomes heavy and speech disappears

Let the decline acquire duration. Nereth's feet drag. Her steps land heavily. She breathes harder, loses pace and no longer responds to Seralune's questions.

Choose observable failures in sequence rather than announcing that she is "not here". Seralune pauses; Nereth takes another step before stopping. Seralune addresses her; she does not look up. A hand remains against the wall after there is no longer any need for support.

Seralune offers a rest, lowers the light to help her see and waits for answers. When none come, she stops asking so often. She walks more slowly and checks over her shoulder instead.

Do not give Nereth new articulate speeches about Cyrandor or the dead in this stretch. Her silence is the danger. The reader should hear the breathing and dragging feet where conversation used to be.

**Change:** Nereth still follows, but Seralune cannot tell whether she is being heard.

## 8. Roots, fauna and the dead

The roots thicken and the pale growth becomes visible beyond the lamp. Establish this light before the lamp eventually breaks.

Reveal a corpse through a human detail, then the root damage. Servant's grey connects the dead to what the two women wear. Seralune can suspect a history of people coming down here without knowing their individual stories.

Nereth does not engage in a long exchange about the bodies. Seralune may try to make her look and receive no response. She has to decide where to put her own feet, where to lead Nereth and whether it is safe to stop.

Unidentified movement and sounds can remain outside the light. Do not supply a bestiary.

**Change:** the surroundings become overtly horrifying while her companion becomes less responsive.

## 9. A plea carries through the dead mouths

"Help me" interrupts the walking and breathing, rather than a new prolonged argument.

Seralune sees a mouth move in a body that does not breathe. Another mouth carries the same words further along. She answers, but receives no name or proper reply.

She follows because there may be something living at the source. Nereth follows silently; she does not now become an eloquent guide or explain what the sound means.

Seralune still checks where Nereth is. The plea does not erase her responsibility for the woman behind her.

**Change:** her attempt to reach someone in need leads them to the creature.

## 10. Helping the creature breaks Nereth's silence

At a hollow beside a wider ledge under an old arch, Seralune finds the small lizard threaded with roots and flowers. Its mouth opens with the cries from the bodies.

Seralune sets down the lamp and uses a loose stone to lift a root pinning its hind leg. Nereth sits nearby, exhausted and unresponsive.

Nereth's first words after the long silence can be a warning or abrupt command: "Leave it." Seralune briefly feels relieved that she is speaking. The answer then fails to become an ordinary conversation: Nereth repeats the command, raises her voice or demands that Seralune get up.

Keep the outburst short and escalating. The earlier irritation and prolonged silence prepare it; a sudden polished speech would weaken it.

The leg comes free. The creature scrambles onto Seralune's sleeve and climbs towards her shoulder.

Nereth rises, tackles Seralune sideways onto the wide stone and attempts to strangle her. The lamp breaks. Pale growth supplies the remaining light.

Seralune claws, kicks, pushes and tries to pull away the wrists. Her first cry breaks as the pressure grows. The cut palm hurts, her sight darkens and her strength fails. She approaches unconsciousness without fully losing the viewpoint.

The creature reaches Nereth's face and bites beneath her left eye. One hand leaves Seralune's throat. She pulls away the other and rolls free, coughing and gasping.

Nereth tears the creature off and staggers near the channel. Seralune catches her despite her fear. Blood and dark marks spread from the bite, and Nereth's pain and confusion intensify.

**Change:** Seralune survives the attack, but must now help an injured companion she cannot feel safe beside.

## 11. The explosion and the exhausted body left behind

Seralune supports Nereth beneath the surviving arch before the spell. Nereth's casting hand points down the wider channel. Retain the copied plea emerging from her mouth after the bite if using the present fire passages; it remains an observation, not an explanation of possession or a deity.

The light grows, the ledge jolts, and sound overwhelms speech. The fire tears through the aqueduct, breaks the mountain's side and returns through the ruined passage. Seralune holds Nereth; formed flames pass round them.

Stone can still strike her. Keep a surviving recess and an uneven route towards the breach; do not remove every surface needed for their escape.

Nereth's arm falls. No further fire comes. She is beyond walking and may not yet answer coherently.

Seralune checks for breath before trying to move her. She has a painful throat, an injured hand and little strength herself. The rescue cannot become effortless because the spell has ended.

**Change:** confinement has been violently opened, and Nereth can no longer help them leave it.

## 12. Dragging towards the breach; the port below

Seralune cannot sustain a clean carry across the broken passage. Proposed staging: she uses the shawl or gathered clothing beneath Nereth's shoulders, keeps her head clear of sharp stone and pulls her short distances. She stops, adjusts her hold and goes again. Do not drag Nereth by the neck or wounded face.

The final movement towards the breach takes effort. The first cold air reaches them before Seralune can see the full view.

Leaving the contamination lets Nereth's awareness return gradually, but does not refill her mana or heal the bite. A small response or attempt to help is enough. No immediate full explanation or forgiveness is required.

At the opening, let Seralune stop and look. The broken mountain and the long scar down the uninhabited slope make the spell's scale visible. She can compare what she sees with what she has heard of Thaer's power; she has not witnessed a comparable Dark spell herself.

Below and off to one side lies the port town: roofs, harbour lights and the shapes of vessels becoming visible as the sky lightens. It is the first inhabited destination she can actually point towards.

Its distance matters. She can see where they need to go, but Nereth still cannot walk and the slope is broken. Seralune must find usable ground and move slowly. Short carries, supporting Nereth if she can later bear some weight, or careful movement between resting places remain staging choices to establish on the page; do not pretend she can drag someone down an entire steep mountainside without difficulty.

End as they start making their way towards the port. They do not reach a bed, obtain aid or settle what happened between them in the last few paragraphs.

**Change:** Seralune finally has a visible destination, and must get them both there.

## What makes this shape work

The deterioration is also a loss of ordinary conversation. Nereth begins by answering, then answers sharply, then no longer answers. Her violent return to speech should therefore feel like a frightening event in itself.

Seralune's attempts to keep them together continue changing: conversation, reassurance, asking to hold the lamp, choosing the right route, checking over her shoulder, helping the creature, fighting for breath, holding Nereth through fire and dragging her towards daylight.

The right turn is not a moral mistake. Nereth's attack is not proof of her hidden true character. The bite is not justice. Seralune's protection from formed spells does not erase the physical harm she has just suffered.

The port is hope with practical distance still between them and help. The mountain's destruction stays visible behind their first steps towards it.
