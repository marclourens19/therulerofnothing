# Chapter 10 – Final Corrections

6 October 2026. Applied after the combined draft, five revision passes and the pacing review. The current manuscript is `Chapter 10 - Beneath Natharul.md`.

This pass makes six approved corrections:

1. The opening preserves Seralune's deliberate choice at the end of Chapter 7: “The arch was behind them before Seralune stopped looking back at it.”
2. The reason she initially follows Nereth remains Nereth's experience of the channels. The additional claim that being led was easier than thinking is removed.
3. The creature's bite is placed in Nereth's cheek below her left eye, consistent with the wound described afterwards.
4. While Seralune's eyes are closed as the returning fire passes them, the account stays with what she feels and hears. She sees the divided fire after opening her eyes.
5. During the fall while carrying Nereth, Seralune releases the wrists to brace herself, keeps her injured forearm beneath Nereth's knee, and uses her good hand to protect Nereth's head.
6. Seeing the sea prompts the involuntary turn towards Nereth: “She looked round as though she might find her standing there to see it.” It pays off Nereth's wish to see the sea before Seralune looks back at her unconscious companion.

The chapter retains Nereth's gradual deterioration, the choking attack, the destructive spell, Seralune's fear during the carry and the descent towards the port town. The revision does not add another conversation or resolve Nereth's injuries.

Chapters 1–9 remain at their existing versions on `main`; earlier local review copies were not used to replace the newer repository text.
