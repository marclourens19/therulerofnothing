# Chapter 4: The First Draft, and What It Took from the Old Chapters

Written on 27 September 2026. This compares the new `Chapter 4 - Before Evening.md` with the old material it replaces: the Seralune coda at the end of old Chapter 3, `Old - Before Re-plan/Volume 1 - Rewrites/Chapter 3 - The Weight of the Living (Rewrite).md`, from "There was no sound." to the end, about 1,450 words.

Almost everything is new, so this shows the key passages and your lines rather than going paragraph by paragraph. The plan behind it is `Chapter 4 - Design.md`.

The first draft is saved unchanged as `Drafts/Chapter 4 - Before Evening (Draft 1).md`. From now on, every round of changes is a change list against it.

## At a glance

- **Length:** 2,651 words, from the old coda's 1,450. It's shorter than Chapter 2 (4,723) and longer than Chapter 3 (1,995). It covers every beat you approved. Tell me if you want more room anywhere: see call 7.
- **Shape:** one room and one day, all in her eyes, with no scene breaks.
  1. The mana leaves her; the white; she falls.
  2. *Am I dead?*
  3. No sound, not even the falls.
  4. The crystal, the cut, the pull.
  5. This morning, and the fight she can't remember.
  6. The door, the calling, the anger.
  7. The panic.
  8. The long hours, and "before evening".
  9. *Was I in there?*
  10. Sleep, or not.
  11. She gets up.
  12. Thaer, and the fear in his eyes.
- **Her voice:** it starts dry and practical ("Very well", "A great deal of crystal", "Well. That was clever."). The dryness drops away as the day goes on, and nobody points that out.
- **"Nothing":** twice. Her mana "done nothing at all", and "On her side, there was nothing at all" (the door).
- **"The dark":** none. She's in total black all day, so it's said as what she can't see: "somewhere so black I can't see my own hand", "not being able to see".
- **Her mana is never used.** It leaves her once, on its own. The crystal draws on it once, and she gets away with her body, not by pulling it back. The rest of the time it only crowds up in her when she's frightened, and is "no help at all".
- **Nobody tells her anything,** and she doesn't learn "sealed" or "a thousand years". She only reaches *Was I in there?*, and turns it down: *People don't sleep inside crystal.*

## Your call

These are mine, not yours. Each one adds something you haven't decided, or is a change you didn't ask for.

1. **The scream in the white.** "Someone was screaming, and it was her, and the whole world went white."
   - In Chapter 1, at the same instant, Alaric hears it: "Somewhere inside it, someone screamed. He couldn't tell if it was him."
   - Nobody points at the link. A reader who remembers may wonder. Keep it, or cut the scream?
2. **She can't hear the falls.**
   - "She had never been anywhere in the palace where she couldn't hear the falls. They were there under everything, day and night, so constant that she hardly ever noticed them."
   - It's new, but it's built on your waterfall. It's the first thing that tells her she's somewhere wrong. It also makes the falls, the first thing she knows in Chapter 5, a sound she's been missing all day.
3. **What her mana feels like when she's frightened.** "Her mana had crowded up under her ribs and behind her eyes the way it always did when she was frightened, so full she could hardly breathe around it, and as always, it was no help at all."
   - This is her own sense, where Alaric has **THOOM**. It's taken from the old version.
   - It fits "it acts on its own" and "it just exists": it moves inside her, and never does anything.
4. **Thaer's two knocks, and the door he cuts.**
   - Her memory plants his habit: "the door opening before she'd said he could come in, because her brother had never once in his life waited to be let in". That's from the old Thaer, and Chapter 5's ending needs it.
   - At the end she steps back and waits for his two knocks. "They didn't come." Instead "A line of light appeared down the middle of the door… and the two halves of the door fell apart along it".
   - That shows his sword without explaining it, and shows how frightened he is: for once, he doesn't knock.
5. **"He smelled of horses."** It's the only sign that he came from far off, following your reading that he felt the seal break and rode back. She can't know more than the smell.
6. **The inside of the crystal pulls, and the outside doesn't.**
   - She walks round the outside with her palms on it, and nothing happens.
   - Only the hollow inside draws on her mana: the part that held her, which she doesn't know yet.
7. **Length.** It's 2,651 words for a whole day.
   - *My recommendation:* keep it. A day of nothing works while every stretch changes something, and longer would start to repeat itself.
   - If you want more, the best place is her morning: more of home, her mother and Thaer before it all stopped. Or the long hours, with more thirst and cold.
8. **The title: "Before Evening".** It works twice. It's Thaer's promise, and he comes after evening, a thousand years late, though the reader only learns that later. And it sets up "You said you'd be back before evening, too" in Chapter 5. The old title was "The Shape of Absence".

## Your lines, as they stand

Both are word for word as you approved them:

> *Am I dead?*
>
> *No. Don't be ridiculous. Surely the dead don't get cold, and I'm freezing.*
>
> *So I'm alive, and lying on a stone floor I don't know, somewhere so black I can't see my own hand. Very well. Before I do anything else, I need to find out where I am.*

> *Mother was in my room this morning and we fought, though I can't even remember what about, and Thaer was there too, promising he'd be back before evening. And then… what? I must have done something after he left. I must have gone somewhere. So why can't I remember going anywhere at all?*

The fight she can't remember stays unexplained. It's the hole where Alaric was (round 5), and nothing on the page points at it. The only thing added next to it is her regret: *Whatever I said to Mother this morning, I'll take it back. I don't even care who was right.*

## What the old chapter gave, and what changed

- **The opening.**
  - *Old:* "There was no sound. / Just for an instant, the dark had been white. / Then it wasn't. / Seralune woke up certain that something had broken."
  - *New:* "Her mana moved." It's something happening to her, and only she could feel it. The white comes after, and the old line's "the dark" is gone.
- **Waking.** The stone against her cheek survives. The old line "She opened her eyes. The dark didn't change at all." became her touching her own lashes to find out whether her eyes are open.
- **Calling "Brother?" and "Mother?".** It survives, later in the chapter, with his name: "Thaer!"
- **What she knows.**
  - *Old:* the south garden, the evening window, her mother's voice, and Thaer's two knocks.
  - *New:* the falls she can't hear, the sun on her floor, and the knocks.
- **The counting in Thaer's voice** ("Count. / Slowly. One breath first.") is gone, as you asked. Her own voice fights the panic instead: *Stop it. Stop it. Panicking won't open that door, and it won't bring anyone.* / *Breathe. You know how to breathe. You've been doing it all your life.*
- **Reaching for her mana to make light.** Gone, under your rule: she never tries to use it and never expects to. Instead her mana leaves her once on its own, and the crystal draws on it once.
- **The carved circle and the metal strips** became your crystal: huge, split open, ringing when she taps it, with a hollow inside that draws on her mana. The old "**Tink.**" in bold became plain prose ("ringing as it went"), since bold is only for **THOOM**.
- **Her memory of this morning.**
  - *Old:* the council bell, the pears, and Thaer's promise, with the order of them wrong.
  - *New:* your line, the fight she can't remember, and his knocks.
- **Walking the walls and the seam.** It survives, with the room bigger, and the door with no handle on her side, as you designed it. The old "Seven careful steps to a corner" is gone, because counting is gone.
- **"After that, time stopped having any shape."** It became "After that, she lost track", followed by the hours: thirst, the cut, the cold, *It must be evening by now*, *Was I in there?*, and sleeping or not.
- **The footsteps and the door.**
  - *Old:* running footsteps, then "**KRRRACK.**", and the door torn out of its frame.
  - *New:* she steps back to wait for his two knocks, which never come. Then a line of light, and the door falls in two.
- **Thaer in the light.** It's almost unchanged: "the light caught the narrow point of one ear through his hair, and the line of his shoulder, and she knew that shoulder". New: "He smelled of horses".
- **The ending.** It's unchanged, as you asked: "And for the first time in her whole life, Seralune saw fear in her brother's eyes." The old "on the broken circle, and the hollow at its centre" became "his eyes were fixed on something behind her. She didn't need to turn round to know what it was." That saves her first sight of the crystal for Chapter 5.
