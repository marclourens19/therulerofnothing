# Chapter 2: What the Redesign Changed

Written on 27 September 2026. This compares the redesigned `Chapter 2 - The Price of a Voice.md` with the version merged on 26 September, which is now kept as `Drafts/Chapter 2 - The Price of a Voice (Version 1, before the redesign).md`. Most of the second half is new, so this goes scene by scene and shows the key passages as before and after. Every decision it carries is in `Chapter 2 - Design.md`, rounds 5–7.

The new chapter is saved unchanged as `Drafts/Chapter 2 - The Price of a Voice (Draft 2, the redesign).md`. From now on, every round of changes goes in a change list against it, as before.

## At a glance

- **Length:** 2,809 words before, 4,684 now.
- **Shape:** still one night in Alaric's eyes, with no scene breaks. It now has six movements: the door, the fight, the fire, the ride, the forest, and the last stand.
- **Unchanged:** the door, word for word, up to the stone travelling under the floor.
- **Kept, moved or adapted:**
  - Liluth's wounds and escape;
  - "Fucking elves";
  - "I've got you";
  - "She heard me" and the horns;
  - Wena and "Not this time";
  - the stool and the mug;
  - "I'm not leaving you" / "Wasn't asking" / "You already did" / "She'll stay if you do" / "You're a stubborn little bastard";
  - the name vision, "There you are", "Tell her the old fool sent you" and "Take Wena and run, Alaric";
  - the hand of fire.
- **Alaric's thoughts:** none before. Now at the shout, on the ride, when Gerolt goes down, and at the last stand, built from your rough versions.
- **"Nothing":** used twice, and both are Alaric's: the thought before the shout ("Then I'm nothing again") and the last shout ("Don't you dare leave me with *nothing* again!").
- **Comparisons:** the same three as before (the question, the candle, how Gerolt sounds), plus one he has lived through: the burning riders smell like the field he woke up in.

## Your call

These are new, mine rather than yours, and each needs a yes or no. Since this was written, your notes have become round 1 of `Chapter 2 - Changes.md`, and the quotes below show the draft before them.

1. **The neighbours' window.** As they ride out: "Two fields over, a single window was lit." Gerolt looks at it, then turns the other way: "Make for the trees, lad." It's the only place the reader learns why he runs that way, and nobody says it. **Answered:** yes, and Gerolt says "The trees…" first (`Chapter 2 - Changes.md`, change 6).
2. **How he lights the house.** He puts his palm flat on the doorpost, and the fire runs up it into the eaves. Afterwards "His palm had gone an angry red. He didn't look at it." That's the same cost as the candle in Chapter 1.
3. **Alaric and the horse.** "Up close, it was taller than any horse he had pictured when he heard the word." He gets on badly, half hanging off it. It's the memory rule lived: he knows what a horse is, but has never been on one.
4. **Gerolt's small lines, all new:**
   - "Good," Gerolt said. He didn't sound as though anything was good.
   - "Don't haul on them." (the reins)
   - "Gerolt." No answer. "*Gerolt!*" / "Still here."
   - "Gerolt swore, softly and at length." (after the fall)
5. **The horse is shot** at the edge of the trees and throws them into the bracken. That's why they go on foot.
6. **Alaric's thought when Gerolt goes down.** You didn't give one, so this is mine: *"Get up. Get up, please, you have to get up—"* / *"I can't. I can't even lift him. What use am I if I can't even—"*
7. **Alaric laughs too** at "worst horse": "Alaric laughed too. He couldn't have said why." It's the one ordinary moment the two of them share.
8. **The riders come from the river,** ahead of them, not from behind. That's the shock: they've got round the wood.
9. **A rider laughs** at Gerolt standing alone with empty hands, and "The laughing stopped" when his hand catches fire.
10. **Silas's look,** taken from the old World Bible: "He was big, in a dark cloak, and the heavy blade in his hand was black to the hilt. When he turned, the firelight caught his face: dark stubble, and an old pale scar running from his cheekbone down towards his jaw." He kills the three riders who are left.
11. **Silas and the sword.** "His eyes went to the sword across Alaric's lap and stayed there, and something crossed his face that Alaric couldn't read." This is where he knows his master's blade.
12. **Wena at the end.** She runs to Gerolt, pushes her nose under his hand, then follows them. **Answered:** reworked to show it as Alaric sees it (change 23).
13. **The last line:** "Alaric went backwards into the dark with Gerolt's sword still in his hand, and watched Gerolt kneeling in the firelight until he couldn't see him any more." It keeps the shape of the line you called "perfect".
14. **Two approved lines had no place:** "Careful, lad. I run hot." (it answered the hot hand in the cabin, which is gone) and "On that field… Found one lad still breathing." *Recommended:* leave both out, to keep the forest short.

## 1. The door

Unchanged, word for word, from "The fourth knock faded into the wood" to "Gerolt was still dragging at the sword with both hands on the hilt, the dead elf's weight hanging from the blade."

## 2. The fight

**The shout.** Your thought for him, then the dodge (rounds 5 and 7).

> **Before:** "GEROLT!"
>
> Gerolt twisted towards the shout as the floor burst.
>
> He almost cleared it.
>
> A jagged spear of stone came up through the boards and took him under the ribs. … The stone snapped with a dull crack and left a broken length of itself inside him.
>
> Gerolt screamed.
>
> **After:** *If that reaches him—*
>
> Everything the boy had was in this cabin, and most of it was one old man with his back turned.
>
> *Then I'm on my own. Then I'm nothing again.*
>
> "GEROLT!"
>
> Gerolt wrenched the sword free and threw himself sideways, and the floor burst where he had been standing.
>
> A jagged spear of stone came up through the boards. It caught him as he fell and tore along his side, through his shirt and the skin beneath it, and went on upward until it split the beam above the door.

Your rough version was: "He thought that was it, that was all done, everything he has known, even if just in this moment. He would be alone and be nothing again."

**Liluth.** Her eye, her face, her arm and her escape are unchanged. The slab still throws him back, and now it breaks something:

> **After:** He hit the floor hard enough to shake the whole cabin, and the boy heard something inside him crack.

**The stone in him has gone,** so these went with it: "Don't. Leave it where it is.", "Gerolt screamed. It was a raw, animal sound, nothing like the voice…", and "Can't walk."

## 4. The ride (new)

> **After:** Two fields over, a single window was lit.
>
> Gerolt raised his head and looked at it, for as long as it took the horse to shift its feet twice. Then he turned his head the other way, towards the black line of trees at the far side of the field.
>
> "Make for the trees, lad."

Your thoughts for him on the ride ("Why are they attacking us, what just happened, why did Gerolt kill that man, why, what is happening"). Each turn says something new: the killing, the door, the fire, then everything.

> **After:** *What just happened?*
>
> The elf had laughed in the doorway. He'd been laughing, and then the sword was in his jaw and he was choking on his own blood, and Gerolt hadn't said a word to him first. Not one.
>
> *He killed him. He just killed him—why? Why did he—*
>
> *Why did they come at us like that? We didn't open the door. That's all we did. We didn't open a door, and now—*
>
> … *Why did he burn it? They can see us now. Everyone can see us, why would he* do *that—*
>
> … *What's happening? What is* happening*—*

He doesn't ask why they came, because he worked that out in Chapter 1. He asks why they came *like that*.

## 6. The last stand

**The handover** is now a handful of words, with Marta in one line:

> **Before:** "West," he said. "Across the river. Kelmend." … "There's an inn by the south gate. Marta. Show her that. She'll know it." … "Tell her the old fool sent you."
>
> **After:** "Kelmend. Over the river." His breath snagged, and he waited until he had it back. "Marta, at the inn by the south gate. Show her that. Tell her the old fool sent you."
>
> It ended in a small cough, and blood began to run from the corner of his mouth.
>
> "Take Wena and run, Alaric."

**The hand of fire** is kept word for word. Now it's used on the riders:

> **After:** He swept his arm out, and the fire left it. It fell across the first two riders and their horses in a sheet, and all four of them were burning at once. …
>
> The smell reached Alaric a moment later. It was the smell of the field he had woken up in.

**Alaric's thoughts,** from your rough version ("What do I do, I can't do anything, I want to help, I can't, I'm scared to help, I will die, I don't want to die"):

## 7. Silas (new)

> **After:** Gerolt lifted his head. The point of one arrow stood out through the back of his shirt. He coughed, and turned his face a little towards the light, and his beard was dark with blood. He looked at the man for a long time.
>
> "Heh."
>
> The fire on his hand went out.
>
> He stayed on his knees.

He's never called Silas, because Alaric doesn't know his name. His line is yours, with "boy" as his word:

> **After:** "Get up, boy, or we're both dead!"

**The ending:**

> **Before:** Alaric lay in the wheat with Gerolt's sword beneath him and watched the house burn.
>
> **After:** Alaric went backwards into the dark with Gerolt's sword still in his hand, and watched Gerolt kneeling in the firelight until he couldn't see him any more.

## Knock-on effects

- **Old Chapter 3** (Alaric alone through the wheat to the forest, and Wena running back towards the fire) no longer happens. The next chapter starts with Silas, Alaric and Wena running from the horns along the river.
- **Silas knowing the sword** now happens here. In old Chapter 6 it was the token at the river.
- **Liluth heard him,** and the riders now know Gerolt had company, a burnt house, five dead riders and a man with a blade. Natharul's side of that is for later chapters.
- **The neighbours** saw the fire. What happens to them is still open.
- **"The name Gerolt swallowed"** in Chapter 1 is still unsaid, and he dies without saying it, as agreed.
