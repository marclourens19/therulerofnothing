# Chapter 6: Design

Started 29 September 2026. **Status:** round 1 is waiting on the author. Nothing is written yet. The order stays the same as for every chapter: the design is agreed, then a dialogue round, then Claude says the chapter is ready and waits for the go-ahead.

**The target** (29 September): 4,000–5,000 words, and longer if the story needs it. Chapter 5 taught that length comes from what happens, so this design plans enough happening from the start: six scenes, each with something that changes.

## Where the story stands

- **Chapter 3** ends in Silas's cave on the Kelmend side of the river, at first light on Day 2. Gerolt died last night. Alaric believes it was his fault, and nobody has told him otherwise. He cried into Wena's fur and whispered "I'm sorry".
- **Silas** has given his name and nothing else. He answered "How did you know him?" with a different answer. He said "Marta." to his blade when Alaric named her. He says Kelmend's guards "would sell their own mothers to Natharul", and "We'll need another way in."
- **Last night at the bridge,** a Natharul rider was talking with Kelmend's guards round their fire.
- **Liluth** survived, cut apart by Gerolt, and she heard Alaric. She comes back later in Volume 1, not here.
- **Chapter 5** ends at the same first light. Seralune watched the royal warship leave for Mydea the night before, and wondered whether her father was on it.

## Fixed for this chapter

- **Alaric's eyes only.** The narration follows his attention.
- **It's Day 2,** from dawn at the cave to night. Marta's door is Chapter 8 (outline round 1).
- **Alaric distrusts Silas, and Silas doesn't answer his questions** (the author, outline round 1).
- **Alaric is himself:** kind, smart, caring, with his humour still in him. When he's frightened he has Subaru's mouth, but inside his head, and he keeps his feelings to himself.
- **Silas:** Guts, not Gerolt: brutal, cunning, "acts instead of thinking", "reckless in the big choices". He still loves Marta. **He doesn't win cleanly this time** (the note from the outside review of Chapter 3).
- **"Empty" isn't talked about yet.** Kelmend's verdict is Chapter 8.
- **Nothing he couldn't know.** He doesn't know what the ship is, who Liluth is, or what the scouts want.

## A proposed shape (for round 1 to change)

About 4,300 words in six scenes. Everything here is a proposal, and the numbers point to the questions below.

1. **Dawn in the cave** (about 500). Silas comes back in: "Up." Alaric asks why he's helping him, and gets an order instead of an answer. The food sack: this time Silas makes him eat (question 7).
2. **The smoke** (about 600). From the first rise, smoke still stands over the far bank where the farm was. Alaric wants to go back and bury Gerolt. Silas refuses. It's their first real clash, and Alaric gives in, for now (question 2).
3. **The road and the questions** (about 900). Hours off the roads. Alaric asks one question after another: who are you, how did you know Gerolt, who's Marta, why did you say her name like that. Silas turns each one aside in a different way (question 3). Alaric's feet, his ribs and Wena. Silas pushes again to leave the dog, because they're looking for a boy with a dog (question 4).
4. **The turn** (about 1,200). Alaric acts on his distrust. While Silas is off scouting, he sets out for Kelmend alone, because Gerolt said Marta, not Silas. It goes wrong (question 5), Silas comes after him and gets him out, and it costs (question 6).
5. **After** (about 600). Silas is furious, and hurt or shaken. Alaric has to decide whether to stay with a man he doesn't trust. He stays, knowing what Silas is, and that's his choice in this chapter.
6. **Kelmend at night** (about 500). The wall and the town's lights, and "Tomorrow." Then something passes overhead going east, towards the farm, so big Alaric has no word for it. Silas goes very still (question 8).

## Round 1 (asked 29 September)

1. **Why is Alaric with Silas this morning?**
   - *Recommended:* nobody says. Silas packs at dawn and says "Up", and Alaric goes because Silas is going to Kelmend and Gerolt told him Kelmend. *Why is he helping me?* is the question under the whole chapter, and Silas never answers it. That's the distrust.
   - *Alternatives:* Silas says it outright ("I'm going anyway"), or Alaric decides it himself ("I owe it to Gerolt to get there").
2. **Does Alaric want to go back and bury Gerolt?** *New, your call.*
   - *Recommended:* yes. It's the one thing he wants for himself today, it's what Gerolt deserves, and Silas refusing it is the first time they really clash. It also makes the ship at the end land harder, because it's going where Gerolt is lying.
3. **How does Silas avoid his questions?**
   - *Recommended:* a different way each time, so it doesn't turn into a pattern: an answer to a different question (his move from Chapter 3), an order, silence, and once a jab at Alaric.
   - Is there one question you want him to answer truthfully today? Or none until Marta's door?
4. **Does Silas push again to leave Wena?** In Chapter 3 he said to leave the dog, and Alaric didn't.
   - *Recommended:* yes. The riders described a boy with a dog, so she's the thing that will get them caught, and Alaric refuses a second time. It keeps Gerolt's gift a cost as well as a comfort.
5. **The turn: what does Alaric walk into when he goes alone?**
   - a. **Kelmend guards on the road with a Natharul rider,** asking about a boy with a big dog, like the bridge last night. *Recommended:* it follows from Chapter 3.
   - b. Farm folk who'd sell him for a reward (Mydea: "people talk, and aren't afraid to give each other up").
   - c. Elves.
6. **How does Silas not win cleanly?**
   - a. He's wounded getting Alaric out.
   - b. He kills a Kelmend guard, and a second one gets away, so now the town itself is looking for them.
   - c. Both.
   - *Recommended:* c. It's also the first sign of the mistake the handoff sets for him in Volume 1: "he chooses brutality, and leaves an easy trail." Is that where you want it to start, or is his big mistake saved for later?
7. **Alaric's state.** Grief, guilt and distrust, with his humour still in him.
   - *Recommended:* the humour shows once, at Silas's expense or to Wena, and he hates himself for it straight away.
   - And does he eat this morning? In Chapter 3 he didn't touch the sack.
8. **The ship.** This was the outline's call, left for later: the royal warship passes overhead at dusk, going east towards Gerolt's farm.
   - *Recommended:* yes, as the last image. The reader knows from Chapter 5 whose ship it is and why it's going there; Alaric doesn't. It's cause and effect, not a near-miss: the king going to see the field Alaric woke on.
   - *Alternative:* the chapter ends on Kelmend's wall and "Tomorrow", and the ship waits for later.

**Answers (30 September).** The author gave their own shape instead of answering question by question, plus two reference pictures: "Ask enough questions, challenge my answers, let's get this chapter to the target length and beyond."
1. **Dawn.** Silas wakes Alaric by nudging his foot: "Up, boy, time to get moving" (the author's rough line, "don't use my exact words but similar"). Silas is hinting that he's coming with Alaric, and Alaric has to wonder why.
2. **Leaving the cave.** Now Alaric can see clearly: the forest ahead, and behind, smoke rising beyond the trees. He stands there a while thinking of Gerolt, sees Silas walking on, pulls himself together, and follows at a long distance behind him.
3. **The road.** Agreed. Alaric is like *A Realm Reborn*'s Alphinaud here, pestering Silas with questions about last night and who Marta is.
4. **The turn.** Alaric gets annoyed with Silas's evasions and wonders if he can be trusted, so he slips away to reach Kelmend on his own with Wena. Pushing through the forest towards Kelmend, he naively comes upon an **ogre** (the author's picture: huge, pale, pot-bellied, with a tusked underbite, clawed hands, rag bindings and a spiked wooden club). Wena barks, it runs after them, catches Alaric by the foot and hauls him up to its face: "Can eat… food…" (the author's rough line). Silas, alerted by the barking, fights it and kills it, then says something snarky, like "Would it kill you just to listen for once."
5. **Kelmend and the ship.** Alaric follows Silas until they see Kelmend's lights. Then the Natharul king's royal airship passes overhead. It's massive: the author's picture is FFXIV's *Agrius*, a huge red hull, spined and organic-looking, with dark spires hanging beneath it and small craft below. Alaric's reaction is "what is that?", and Silas is very shocked too.

Not taken up from round 1: burying Gerolt (question 2), Silas pushing to leave Wena (4), and who Alaric walks into (5), which is now the ogre. These come back in round 2.
