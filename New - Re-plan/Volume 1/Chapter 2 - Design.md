# Chapter 2: Design

Started 26 September 2026. This is the plan for Chapter 2, agreed with the author one question at a time before any writing. It follows the `chapter-rewrite` skill: most of the old chapter will change, so the plan comes first.

## Already fixed by decisions

- **It continues from the fourth knock** at the end of Chapter 1.
- **The riders are Natharul scouts.** They came because they saw dead elves, and they're shocked that they neither saw nor heard the battle. They want answers from the farmer whose land it is.
- **Natharul punishes a whole place for one death.** Chapter 1 set this up with "They don't ask who did it. They ask where." The nearest house with children is two fields over.
- **Liluth is Eminent Earth,** the same level as Gerolt.
- **Gerolt keeps his fire back until the end.** He won't use it near the boy or in his house, because magic costs its user and he doesn't want to hurt the boy or burn his home.
- **Gerolt dies in this chapter,** "with a hand of fire, ready to use his Affinity in a last stand".
- **Gerolt gives the boy his sword and Wena.** The sword replaces the old token. Wena is "almost a wish to keep her safe".
- **The sword was planted in Chapter 1:** one glance at the floorboards under the table.
- **The palm in Chapter 1:** Gerolt's palm reddened after the flame, and the candle burned him. The small flame there comes before the whole fire here.

## Questions

### Asked (round 1)

1. Where does the chapter end, and whose eyes see the last stand?
2. Does his name come back in this chapter, and does Gerolt say it?
3. Does the boy still break his silence to warn Gerolt?
4. Is the boy's refusal to leave what holds Gerolt's fire back?

### Later

- **Kelmend and Marta.** Does Gerolt still send him there? Is Marta still Gerolt's niece, and still the innkeeper? How does the sword prove who sent him?
- **The neighbours.** When Gerolt kills a Natharul scout, the punishment falls on the place. Does he know it will, and choose the boy anyway? Does the chapter show that cost?
- **The scouts.** Keep the old pair: the male killed and never named to Alaric, and Liluth maimed and escaping?
- **"Empty" as a name.** Does Gerolt call him Empty in this chapter, before the name returns?
- **The name Gerolt swallowed in Chapter 1.** Is it said here, or saved for later?
- **The title.** Keep "The Price of a Voice"?
