**Marta — character and dialogue handoff**

Marta is Gerolt's niece and runs the busy inn just inside Kelmend's South Gate. She is regarded as the most beautiful woman in Kelmend; the guards gossip that many customers come simply to look at her.

Her voice takes inspiration from **Baderon in FFXIV**: familiar, worldly, blunt and comfortable handling people. Use light dialect—"ye," "me," occasional "lad"—with natural contractions. Her personality should carry the voice more than phonetic spelling.

She can sound welcoming and familiar with someone she knows, as with Wena:

"Wena? Well now, what're ye doing at me door, girl?"

With Silas, her anger is personal. She previously forbade him to return, and resents him approaching as though their relationship is repaired:

"Get the fuck out of Kelmend, Silas. Now. I told ye to keep away from me. Don't come knocking here as if we're all right."

Keep the swearing. Her beauty does not soften her authority or make her welcome Silas.

Her age, detailed appearance, Affinity and private ambitions remain undecided. The proposed connection between Silas's previous visit and her father's death is still under discussion.
