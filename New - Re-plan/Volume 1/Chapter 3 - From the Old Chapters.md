# Chapter 3: The First Draft, and What It Took from the Old Chapters

Written on 27 September 2026. This compares the new `Chapter 3 - The Weight of the Living.md` with the old material it replaces:
- Alaric's half of old Chapter 3, `Old - Before Re-plan/Volume 1 - Rewrites/Chapter 3 - The Weight of the Living (Rewrite).md`;
- the opening of old Chapter 6, `… Chapter 6 - The Road Owed to the Dead (Rewrite).md`.

Almost everything is new, so this shows the key passages and your lines rather than going paragraph by paragraph. The plan behind it is `Chapter 3 - Design.md`.

The first draft is saved unchanged as `Drafts/Chapter 3 - The Weight of the Living (Draft 1).md`. From now on, every round of changes is a change list against it.

## At a glance

- **Length:** 1,896 words. That's short next to Chapter 2 (4,724) and old Chapter 3's Alaric half (about 2,800). It covers everything you asked for without padding. Tell me if you want more room anywhere.
- **Shape:** one night into first light, all in Alaric's eyes, with no scene breaks. It moves through the run, the broken bridge, the river, the last elf, the cave, "Marta", and alone.
- **"Nothing":** three times, all in your lines for Silas: "Then Gerolt died for nothing", "You did nothing" and "A boy like you means nothing to them".
- **"The dark":** none.
- **Seralune:** not in this chapter. Chapter 4 is hers.

## Your call

These are mine, not yours, so each needs a yes or no.

1. **His inner fight, with more turns than we agreed.** Each one is new, so the spiral moves:
   - "Every stride jarred the ache behind Alaric's eyes, where the name had come in."
   - After Silas's line: *"If I stop, then he—"* / *"So don't stop."* He breaks off before your word "nothing".
   - At the torches: *"They've already killed him. Why are they still coming?"* Then, looking at the sword and Gerolt's blood on his sleeves: *"It's me. They're coming for me."*
   - After the kill: *"He could have done that for Gerolt."* This sets up his anger in the cave.
   - After his answer to Silas: *"If he'd left me in the mud, he'd be asleep in his own bed right now."*
   - The cave thought now opens *"Why me? What did I* do*?"*, because Silas's question changed to "what happened back there".
2. **The bridge.** Silas: "They've brought the bridge down." So the elves broke it to trap them.
3. **Silas swearing in the river.** "It swore at the river and at the dog, and at Alaric, and at the dog again." Then: "—stupid, stubborn little—". That echoes Gerolt's "stubborn little bastard", and nobody points it out.
4. **The sword.** Alaric drops it to pick Wena up. Silas has picked it up, and after the river "He pulled Gerolt's sword out of his belt and gave it back to Alaric."
5. **The kill, in detail.**
   - The elf raises an open hand to use his magic, and Silas stands on his wrist.
   - As the blade heats, smoke comes out of the wound, "and then out of the elf's mouth with the scream, until the scream stopped".
   - Afterwards Silas shakes out his hand, burned by the hilt, because magic costs its user.
   - He kills the screaming horse quickly: mercy for the horse, none for the elf.
6. **The cave's things:** a bedroll, a blackened pot, a water skin, a sack, sticks and a ring of stones. Silas lights the fire by putting his hand among the sticks.
7. **Asking the name.**
   > "So…" It was a stupid thing to ask. Alaric asked it anyway. "What's your name?"
   >
   > "Silas."
   >
   > He didn't ask for Alaric's.
8. **His hands over his face:** "It was easier with his hands there, where he didn't have to see Silas's face."
9. **"Marta".** Silas's hands stop, he says the name "to the blade, not to Alaric", and then the rag moves "harder than before".
10. **"We'll need another way in."** Alaric looks up, and "Silas was already looking at the fire."
11. **The ending.**
    - Silas pushes the food sack across, and doesn't push again when Alaric leaves it.
    - Silas: "Get some sleep."
    - Alaric moves his thumb once between Wena's eyes, "the way Gerolt had".
    - He cries "quietly, so that Silas wouldn't hear".
    - The last line is *"I'm sorry."*
12. **The title.** The working title is the old one, "The Weight of the Living". It fits: Alaric lives, Gerolt doesn't, and Alaric carries it.
13. **Length.** It's short, as above. More room anywhere, or is it right for what it is?

## What the old chapters gave, and what changed

- **The river, the bridge and Wena.** Old Chapter 3 had a bridge held by riders. Alaric swam alone, let go of a root to save Wena, and hid under the bank. Now the bridge is broken. Alaric carries Wena in without thinking, and the current takes them both. Silas, who said leave the dog, hauls them out. The hiding under the bank is gone.
- **The stranger's hideout.** Old Chapter 6 had a hollow in the rock, and a scout strangled with a cord. Now it's your cave behind the bush, and the kill is your open, cruel one.
- **The token.** Old Chapter 6 had "Show me what you keep touching." That's gone: Silas knew the sword in Chapter 2, and he picks it up and gives it back without a word.
- **"Where is he?" / "He stayed behind."** Gone, because both of them saw Gerolt die. It became the anger ("You could have *saved* him!" / "You did nothing").
- **"Do you know Marta?" / "I know the inn."** It became Silas saying the name once, to his blade.
- **Mydea handing him over** was a warning about the Mydean guards. Now it's Silas's line about Kelmend.
- **Left for later:** the night road, Kelmend's gate and the inn door (old Chapter 6), "Empty" ("they don't talk about that yet"), and the neighbours.
