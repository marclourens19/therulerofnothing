# Chapters 5–10: Design

Started 29 September 2026. **Status:** round 1 asked.

The author: "We can design chapters 5–10, the design of them / structure, not the actual content yet." So this file settles what each chapter is for: whose eyes, where it starts and ends, what the viewpoint character wants, the choice that turns it, what's different by the end, the small answer it pays, and the hook. Scenes and lines come later, one chapter at a time, as they did for Chapters 2–4.

`Volume 1 Structural Map - Proposal.md` is used here as a source of ideas, not as canon ("just a discussion to understand an idea"). Where an idea comes from it, it's marked *(proposal)*.

## Already fixed

**By the Chapter 4 design (27 September, rounds 1–5):**
- **Chapter 5 is Seralune's.** Its order and lines are agreed in `Chapter 4 - Design.md` ("Chapter 5, the order" and "The lines, version 2"). It runs from the embrace in the broken chamber to first light, and ends on a knock at her door, with Cyrandor unseen behind it.
- **Chapter 6 returns to Alaric.** ("6 can return to Alaric.")
- **Chapter 7 is her escape,** with Cyrandor. He's the first person who answers her: the number of years and her mother come from him.
- Nobody says "sealed" or "a thousand years" to her face in Chapter 5. She overhears "the seal" through the door.
- Nereth is composed on duty in Chapter 5. Her first slip comes in the escape.

**By the handoff (28–29 September), changing that design:**
- Chapter 5 **may be split into Part One and Part Two.**
- **Cyrandor's knock is different from Thaer's,** and she assumes it's Thaer because she's waiting for him. (The agreed ending had the knocks "spaced exactly like Thaer's".)
- **Thaeroval regrets the sealing,** hopes their father may change his mind, and delays resealing her.
- Seralune is confused, alarmed and distrustful, and doesn't meekly accept explanations.

**By Decisions, for the chapters after Chapter 5:**
- Gerolt sent Alaric to **Marta, at the inn by Kelmend's south gate.** The sword is the proof. Marta is Gerolt's niece; everything else about her is undecided.
- **Kelmend's guards would sell him to Natharul,** so "we'll need another way in" (Chapter 3). A Natharul rider was already talking to them at the bridge.
- **Silas still loves Marta.** Her father died in his gorge. "Marta." said to the blade is a tell readers are waiting on.
- Two notes from the outside review of Chapter 3: **pay a small answer on Silas, Gerolt and Marta soon,** most naturally when Marta sees Silas at her door; and **the next time Silas is on the page, he shouldn't win cleanly.**
- **Redd and Freya grew up in Kelmend's Faint quarter.** Redd was tested at ten and served a master who has since died; he's unclaimed. Freya was never tested: Redd hid her from the yearly inspections.
- **Liluth heard him** and comes back later in Volume 1.
- **The king of Natharul is flying to Mydea** on the royal warship, to see the field with the dead elves on it. Seralune watches the ship leave in Chapter 5.
- **Darcy's rescue is Volume 2's mission,** and Gilmot's threat against the Faint quarter is part of why she can't simply be taken.
- **The guard for the whole volume:** until they meet, the two stories affect each other through cause and effect, never through near-misses.

## Each chapter, in a little more detail

### Chapter 5 (Seralune): Natharul, in two parts

- **Fixed:** the agreed ten-beat order and the version 2 lines.
- **Proposed split,** at the natural seam in the agreed order:
  - **Part 1** is beats 1–5: the embrace; the court and "You can just ask me"; the walk up and Cyrandor's smile; the falls, the city, the warship leaving and the tree; her room, Nereth, and "Where's Elowen?" Its question: *what happened to my world?* It ends when nobody has heard Elowen's name.
  - **Part 2** is beats 6–10: through the door and "the seal"; Thaer comes back and she turns on him; the blows under the floor; the snap at Nereth and the sorry; first light and the knock. Its question: *what are they going to do to me?*
- **What the handoff changes:** the knock (question 2), her first choice (question 3) and Thaer's regret (question 4).

### Chapter 6 (Alaric): the road to Kelmend

- **Wants:** to do the one thing Gerolt told him to. **Against him:** Silas thinks the town will sell him to Natharul, and a Natharul rider was already at the bridge.
- **The turn:** Alaric insists on Marta. It's the first time he pushes against Silas rather than following him.
- **Silas doesn't win cleanly** (the review note). Something about getting in goes wrong, or costs him, and it's the first crack in his competence.
- **The world:** Kelmend seen from outside, and what a town looks like when Affinity decides who lives inside the wall and who lives against it.
- **Ends:** Marta opens the door and sees Silas. The small answer on Silas, Gerolt and Marta starts here.
- **Your call, new:** on the road, the royal warship passes overhead, going towards Gerolt's farm. The reader knows it from Chapter 5; Alaric has no word for it. It's cause and effect, not a near-miss: her father's ship, going to his field.

### Chapter 7 (Seralune): the escape

- **Fixed:** Cyrandor behind the door. He's the first to answer her: the number, and her mother. Nereth's first slip comes here.
- **From the handoff:** Nereth must choose to come; Seralune can't order her to flee and have the story call it freedom. Cyrandor succeeds because he gives Seralune information and a choice.
- **The old version** had Cyrandor lose an arm holding a barrier, and Thaeroval unable to strike his sister. What stays is for round 2.

### Chapter 8 (Alaric): Marta's inn

- **The payoff:** Gerolt's instruction leads somewhere, but shelter isn't automatic. What Alaric does decides whether Marta keeps helping *(proposal)*.
- **The small answer:** something about Marta and Silas, and the gorge, as far as Alaric can see it.
- **"Empty" becomes a verdict.** Someone who can read Affinity finds nothing in him, and it's dangerous rather than pitiful. Gerolt's word comes back. *(In the old version Marta sensed it; that part of her is undecided.)*
- **The problem the next chapters turn on:** Darcy, and the price Gilmot has put on the Faint quarter.

### Chapter 9 (Alaric): the Faint quarter

- **Redd and Freya enter through the Kelmend problem.** Their home is the quarter Gilmot has threatened, so Darcy's choice is about their lives before they're ever Alaric's friends.
- **The world:** the Faint quarter shows the hierarchy in housing, food, policing and who can be taken from whom.
- **The turn:** Alaric decides to help, knowing that helping has a cost other people will pay.
- **Freya:** she makes a choice of her own, not as Redd's sister, and Alaric notices something about what she wants *(proposal)*.

### Chapter 10 (Seralune): after the escape

- **The old Chapter 10** ("Beneath Natharul") had the seal's leaked mana poisoning the land under the palace, Nereth's corruption beginning, and Seralune's compassion costing someone. With the rule that her mana acts on its own, that fits: it's the first time her mana hurts someone she cares about.
- Whether Nereth's corruption starts here is round 2.

## Round 1 (asked 29 September)

1. **Chapter 5 in two parts?**
   - *Recommended:* yes, as "Chapter 5 (Part 1)" and "Chapter 5 (Part 2)", split after "Where's Elowen?". Each part gets its own question and its own ending, and the numbering stays as agreed (Alaric in 6, the escape in 7).
   - *Why:* ten beats is a lot for one chapter, and the order already divides cleanly into *what happened to my world* and *what are they going to do to me*.
   - *Alternative:* one long chapter.
2. **The knock.** The handoff says it isn't Thaer's knock, but she takes it for his.
   - *Recommended:* use what Chapter 4 already set up. Thaer knocks twice and walks straight in without waiting. This time there are two knocks, and then whoever it is *waits*. She's been up all night waiting for him, so she says "Thaer?" anyway, and the lock turns.
   - *Why:* it's honest in her eyes: the page shows exactly what she hears, and a reader who remembers Chapter 4 can catch what she's too tired to.
   - *Alternative:* the knocks sound different, and she notices and hopes anyway.
3. **Her first choice in Chapter 5.** The handoff wants her to stop waiting for her family to explain her life and start finding out for herself.
   - *Recommended:* she doesn't just overhear Leorin and Thaer; she goes to the door to listen. Afterwards, when Thaer comes back, she doesn't tell him she heard "the seal". It's the first thing she keeps from him.
   - *Why:* it's a small change to the agreed beats 6 and 7, it's what Alisaie would do, and it makes her choosing to follow Cyrandor in Chapter 7 something she's been building to.
4. **Thaer's regret, in her eyes.** She can't see inside him, so the page can only show what he says and does.
   - *Recommended:* keep "No one touches my sister. Not until I say so." Through the door, give Thaer one more line that shows he's hoping their father will see her differently when he's back. That line is yours to give in your own words first.
   - **And the blows under the floor:** someone ordered the crystal repaired. *Recommended:* Thaer did, "in case". She can't know that in Chapter 5, but it keeps his regret from excusing him, and it matters in Chapter 7.
   - *Alternative:* Leorin ordered it behind Thaer's back.
5. **Chapter 6: where it ends, and the way in.**
   - *Recommended:* it runs through Day 2 and ends at night, when Marta opens her door and sees Silas.
   - **The way in** is yours. One option that fits what's decided: through the Faint quarter against the wall, where Redd and Freya live, so the reader sees it before the story needs it.
6. **Does Alaric go into Fort Kelmend and meet Darcy in Volume 1?** Decisions have her rescue in Volume 2 and Gilmot's threat against the Faint quarter, but nothing says Alaric meets her first.
   - *Recommended:* yes. The fort gives Alaric his first chosen act and his first kill. Darcy refusing to leave, so the quarter isn't burned, is his first real test of respecting someone's choice when it denies him what he wants. And she gives him the next road: the core, and Helmi in Favale.
   - If yes, the plan puts the fort in Chapter 11 and her refusal in 12, so Chapters 8–9 can give Marta, the verdict of "Empty", Redd and Freya room to breathe.
7. **Chapter 10 is Seralune's?**
   - *Recommended:* yes, after the escape. Leaving her from Chapter 7 until 13 would be too long.
   - *Your call, new:* the royal warship in Chapter 6 (above). Yes or no.
