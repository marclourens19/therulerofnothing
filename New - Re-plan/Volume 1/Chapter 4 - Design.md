# Chapter 4: Design

Started 27 September 2026. **Status:** round 1 asked. This is the plan for Chapter 4, Seralune's first chapter, agreed one question at a time before any writing, following the `chapter-rewrite` skill.

## Where the story stands

What the reader knows after Chapter 3:

- **Elves are the killers.**
  - Natharul's scouts came to Gerolt's door, and their riders shot him.
  - Liluth escaped maimed.
  - Every elf the reader has met so far wanted Gerolt or Alaric dead.
- **Natharul is only a name.**
  - Gerolt said it at the window and swallowed a more specific name when the riders came.
  - Silas says Kelmend's guards "would sell their own mothers to Natharul".
- **Alaric woke at midday on the Silent Field,** in a light that "searched him". He reached for the word 『Magic』, and Gerolt said 『Affinity』.
- **A white flower with folded petals** stood untouched at the edge of the field.
- **Chapter 3 ends at first light,** with Alaric crying into Wena's fur.

When Chapter 4 opens, the reader is suddenly inside Natharul, in an elf's eyes. It's the first elf they're asked to care about, straight after elves killed Gerolt. That jolt is worth having, and the chapter has to earn her quickly.

## Already fixed by decisions

- **Chapter 4 is all hers.** Stay in her eyes, as with Alaric. Nothing goes on the page that she couldn't see, hear or know ("How does she know?").
- **Who she is,** in your words: "a stubborn princess who stands up for herself and never backs down from what she wants."
  - She's never cocky, and she says sorry. She cares deeply for those around her.
  - Alisaie in everyday life. Shoko when she's with someone she's extremely comfortable with.
  - **Her flaw:** she wants to help everyone, so she takes control, and it ends badly.
- **Her side of the volume's question.**
  - She's told she was sealed for her own good.
  - She doesn't remember it, or deciding anything, so it scares her and she rejects it.
  - Your line for her belief: "How could I be dangerous? I never had magic."
- **She says "magic",** the old word; people now say 『Affinity』. Alaric reached for 『Magic』 in Chapter 1 too, and nobody points it out.
- **Her mana.**
  - She knows she has a great deal of it, and no one has ever found its end. In truth it has none.
  - She has never been able to shape it into anything.
- **Her Volume 1 want:** to escape a system that's locking her away, and to get answers from her mother, who opposed the sealing.
- **Her mother, the queen.**
  - She opposed the sealing, and ran away when everyone else agreed to it.
  - She always believed her daughter would wake.
  - She set trusted servants to report to her, and the duty passed down by word through the Order.
- **Thaeroval stays exactly as written** in the old version.
  - He wants her safe in his "chains", and believes he must choose for her. That means resealing her.
- **Nereth.**
  - A servant maid, Fire. Ram on duty, Revy when she slips.
  - She's loyal to a fault, follows orders and doesn't like to slip. She has wanted to see the world since she was a child.
- **She doesn't remember Alaric.**
- **The writing rules from Chapters 1–3 carry over.**
  - Plain, common words. No "the dark".
  - Add meaning where she'd feel it, small and concrete.
  - The narration follows her attention, and doesn't tell the reader what they already know.
  - No comparisons she couldn't make.
  - Join sentences that belong together.
  - Important lines are designed together.
- **Her own senses.** She "keeps her own sensory language and doesn't borrow his", so there's no THOOM here. In the old chapters her fear showed through her mana, which fills her and answers before she's decided anything. That fits.
- **"The dark" will be hardest here,** because she wakes in a room with no light at all. It can still be done: she can't see her own hand, and there's the stone, the cold and the black.

## Questions

### Round 1 (asked 27 September)

1. **How much of her story Chapter 4 covers.** The old version spent about 10,900 words on her before going back to Alaric: the coda, then all of Chapters 4 and 5.
   - *Recommended:* Chapter 4 runs from the white to first light. She wakes, Thaer comes, they walk through the palace, she's told, the lock turns, the lamps flare, and it ends on the two knocks.
   - Chapter 5 goes back to Alaric. Her escape (the old servant, Nereth, Thaer at the stair) is Chapter 6.
   - *Why:* three chapters of Alaric, then two of her in a row, is a lot to ask of readers who have only just met her. The old design admitted that cost. Alternating leaves a hook hanging in one thread while the other plays. Both chapters end at the same first light, and nobody has to say so.
   - *The cost:* her escape waits a chapter, so Alaric's Chapter 5 has to be strong enough to hold the reader through the knock.
   - *Alternative:* the old order, with Chapters 4 and 5 both hers.
   - *Length:* about 6,000–7,000 words on this plan. That's longer than Chapter 3, and you've said longer is fine when the story needs it.
2. **The opening, and the hours alone.**
   - *Recommended:* it opens on the white, the same instant as Alaric's light in Chapter 1, and nothing explains the link. She wakes on stone in total black, alone.
   - The reader knows Alaric woke at midday, and Thaer walks her through the palace under the moon. So she's alone for hours. She can't tell how long, but readers will want a reason nobody came.
   - *Recommended reason:* Thaer felt the seal break, and came as fast as he could from somewhere far off.
     - The old canon has him arrive "in response to the broken seal". *Where he was* is new.
     - He arrives alone, ahead of the court, with the road still on him.
     - She can ask how he knew, and he doesn't answer. It hints at his part in the seal without saying it.
   - *Alternatives:* no one felt it, and a guard hears her calling at last. Or leave it unexplained.
   - **When the reader learns she's an elf.** *Recommended:* early, when the light catches the point of Thaer's ear, as in the old version. The jolt of "she's one of them" belongs in the first scene.
3. **"Sealed for your own good": who tells her, and how much.** It's decided that she's told. The old version never said "sealed" to her face, so this is new.
   - *Recommended:* Thaer tells her, in her room. After "a thousand years", she asks why she was in there, and he says she was sealed, for her own good.
   - He says "You were sealed", not "I sealed you". His own part stays hidden, as it was in the old version, but a reader can go back later and catch the way he said it.
   - He still won't say why she was dangerous, what happened, or where their mother is.
   - *Her answer* is decided: she rejects it. "How could I be dangerous? I never had magic." That's a line to design together.
   - *Why here:* it puts her Volume 1 want on the page in her first chapter. From the first night she has something to refuse.
4. **The thousand years: exact or round?** It's an open question in Decisions: Alaric's jump was random, so "exactly a thousand" looks designed. Now Thaer has to say a number.
   - *Recommended:* Thaer gives an exact, uneven number, because he has counted every year. Everyone else rounds it to "a thousand years".
   - It settles the open question on the side of the randomness. It also shows what those years were to him without anyone saying it.
   - The number is yours.
   - *Alternative:* "A thousand years", as in the old version.
5. **The old cast and the old palace.** These came from the old chapters, not from you, so each needs a yes or no. Who each person is gets decided with you when they're on the page.
   1. **Leorin,** her cousin, now head of the royal court, with silver in his hair.
      - He's the institution, where Thaer is family.
      - He calls her "Your Highness", and that's the first thing that tells her time has passed.
   2. **Elowen,** her attendant from before. Nobody in the palace has heard the name.
   3. **The old servant** who drops his linen and weeps when he sees her.
      - In the old version he's Cyrandor, the keeper of the queen's Order, and he frees her in the escape.
      - The Order is decided; he and his name aren't.
   4. **Wardwrights,** the court's seal-makers, and their silver chain.
   5. **The king, her father, is away.** He left for Mydea before midnight to see the field for himself.
      - She overhears it through the door, and it means nothing to her. The reader knows which field.
   6. **The changed palace.** The royal tree has nine stars where there were four. There are caged crystals where there were none, and the city has grown over the mountain.

   *Recommended:* yes to all six.
6. **Nereth, re-voiced.** The old Nereth shakes so hard the pitcher rattles on the cup. That isn't Ram.
   - *Recommended on duty:* composed and exact. She answers what she's asked and nothing more. Whatever she thinks of the princess from the chamber doesn't show.
   - *Recommended first slip,* in this chapter. When the lamps flare and the guards come in with swords, she steps in front of Seralune.
     - She does it because her orders were to attend Her Highness, and nobody ordered her to move, not because she's brave.
     - That's "loyal to a fault" and "follows orders", and it defies them all the same.
     - For one breath something comes out of her mouth that no maid says, and then she's "Your Highness" again. Seralune sees it.
   - *Alternative:* no slip yet; save it for the escape.
7. **Her voice, and how it changes.**
   - *Recommended:* with Thaer she starts as Shoko, because he's the person she's most comfortable with. She's soft, reaching for him, and sorry for frightening him.
   - Each door he shuts on her makes her more Alisaie with him, for the first time in her life. The chapter's change shows in how she talks to him, and the narrator never says it.
   - With Leorin and the guards she's Alisaie from her first line ("I'm standing right here. Ask me.").
   - When her mana flares the lamps, she says sorry to Nereth first, before she says anything to the guards.
   - **The counting.** In the old version, when she panics in the black, she hears Thaer: "Count. One breath first."
     - It's her fear-habit, the way Alaric has Subaru's mouth, and it's Thaer's gift to her.
     - So it hurts more as he becomes the one she's afraid of.
     - *Recommended:* keep it.
