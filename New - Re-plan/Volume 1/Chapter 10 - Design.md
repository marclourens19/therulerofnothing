## The working shape

These are the author's beats, in order and numbered so we can refer to them.

1. **The arch.** Seralune doesn't know the way, so she lets Nereth lead.
2. **Circles.** They're walking in circles. Seralune's mana is taking Nereth's sense and her mind.
3. **Seralune takes the lead** and finds a new path, which leads them deeper into the corruption.
4. **What's down there:** the fauna, dead bodies covered in roots, and creepy sounds coming from everywhere.
5. **Nereth gets worse** as the mana corrupts her, and lashes out at Seralune.
6. **"Help me"** interrupts them. The voice comes out of the corpses' mouths, and Seralune goes after it.
7. **The source:** a small corrupted lizard with roots and flowers growing on it.
8. **The bite.** Nereth pulls Seralune's arm away, and the lizard reacts by biting Nereth's face. She's poisoned and corrupted, in extreme pain, and her mind is lost to the mana.
9. **The fire.** Her corrupted fire goes down the aqueduct and destroys everything in its path. It's massive: the ground shakes, and it blasts through the side of the mountain.
10. **The flames rush back.** Seralune is holding Nereth, so the fire goes round them both: Seralune is immune to magic, through her mana.
11. **Spent.** Nereth has used all her mana in that one spell. Seralune carries her to the hole in the mountain. She's never seen destruction like it, and it rivals her brother's power.
12. **Fresh air.** Out of the mountain, Nereth's mind recovers.
13. **The end:** Seralune carrying Nereth down the mountain.

**The feel (the author's):**
- Horror: dark, close, uncomfortable and unsettling.
- Nereth's fire is pure destruction, almost like an atomic bomb in feeling.
- Nereth's descent into madness is shown slowly and built well.

## Notes before the dialogue round

1. **Deaths, and the tear.** The tear is where thousands die and the world blames Seralune (`Volume 1 Picture.md`). If this blast kills people above ground, Chapter 10 does the tear's job first. In close POV she can't know either way: she's inside the mountain, and the city is behind her. Round 2, question 1.
2. **"Rivals her brother in power."** A Ruler is born carrying the mantle, so Thaer had Dark in her own time, and she may have seen him use it. But what she saw is the author's to give. Without a real memory, the thought can't be written from inside her head. Round 2, question 2.
3. **The atomic-bomb feeling, without the word.** She has no such comparison, and the page can't use one she hasn't lived (principle 5). The feeling comes from what she sees and feels, in order:
   - the light first;
   - a moment of nothing;
   - the sound and the push;
   - the ground going;
   - the heat that doesn't touch her;
   - the mountain opening onto the outside.

   Her one comparison is Thaer.
4. **The parting fire mustn't look like she did it.** Her mana can't be used; it acts on its own. She sees the fire go round them and doesn't know why. It also fits what the handoff has for the tear (§14.2): "Nereth survives while close to Seralune."
5. **The descent: the mana makes more of what's in Nereth; it doesn't invent her.**
   - She's Ram on duty and Revy when she slips, so the madness is Revy with nothing holding her back.
   - Her outbursts come from the mana, so they aren't her first real refusal of Seralune. That stays later, as decided.
   - A decided rule can show here: **Seralune's feelings make Nereth's corruption flare.** *Proposal:* Nereth gets worse whenever Seralune is frightened or angry, and Seralune can't see why.
6. **Each step of the descent should be a new kind of wrong** (principle 8: a spiral changes what it claims each time). *Proposal, in order:*
   - she's sure of the way, and wrong;
   - she hears things Seralune can't;
   - she stops answering to her name;
   - she's angry;
   - she's violent;
   - after the bite, she's gone.
7. **Seralune and the place.** *Proposals:*
   - she doesn't lose her way;
   - the growth turns towards her and closes round Nereth;
   - the creatures leave her alone and follow Nereth.

   The old design had the last two. They're the evidence she adds up without reaching the answer.
8. **How she notices the circles.** *Proposal:* their own footprints in the silt, going the same way. Chapter 7 already has "The dust on the floor took their footprints."
9. **Light.** *Proposal:* the lamp is their only light at first. When it's lost (dropped when Nereth lashes out?), the growth gives off a pale light of its own, and the dark comes in close.
10. **One creature.** The lizard is the only creature seen whole. The rest are heard, glimpsed, or found dead, which is horror from rules and absence (design bible §19.3). The falls, which she can't hear, are the absence that runs through the whole chapter.

## What could make this chapter 100/100?

- **The descent is the chapter.** The blast matters because the reader has watched Nereth lose herself step by step (design bible §19.4: spectacle has to spend something the reader already values). The cost is Nereth, not the mountain.
- **Two choices give the fear consequences:** Seralune taking the lead, and Seralune going after the voice. It's her flaw both times, the same move as "Let her go." in Chapter 7. She's not foolish: she believes someone is alive down there.
- **Close POV all the way.** The blast is seen from inside the channel. Nereth's madness is only what Seralune sees and hears. Her thoughts reason, as hers do: *It's this place. It's doing something to her.*
- **After the blast, silence.** Then daylight through a hole in the mountain, the fresh air, and Nereth coming back. That relief has been earned.
- **What can't go back:** a hole in the mountain under the palace, Nereth's mark, and her mana spent. Natharul now has its own proof that Seralune is dangerous, though she doesn't know that yet.
- **Risks:**
  - the madness turning Nereth into someone else;
  - the immunity reading like a power;
  - the blast doing the tear's job;
  - too many monsters.
- **Length:** thirteen beats, with room for the horror to build. *Estimate:* 5,000–6,000 words.

> Cognitive Distortion & Perception Shift
>
> * Mana-Induced Paranoia: The victim perceives ambient magic as a personal threat. They believe friendly spells, magical items, or even pure mana streams are actively trying to poison, spy on, or manipulate them.
> * Sensory Overload & Hallucinations: Corrupted mana alters the brain's sensory processing. Mages begin "seeing" the world through a twisted magical lens—hearing whispers in elemental sounds, seeing horrific geometric shapes in natural geometry, or tasting ozone and rot when magic is cast nearby.
> * Grandiose Delusions of Power: The corrupted energy feeds the ego, convincing the user that they are the only ones capable of handling its true power. They develop a god complex, believing ordinary mortals or traditional magic systems are weak, outdated, or beneath them.
>
> Behavioral & Emotional Extremes
>
> * Volatile Emotional Amplification: Corrupted mana erodes emotional regulation. Minor annoyances spark explosive rage, small doubts spiral into crushing despair, and casual competition turns into a murderous obsession.
> * Obsessive-Compulsive Channeling: The victim becomes single-mindedly obsessed with gathering, studying, or hoarding the corrupt magic source. They will neglect basic biological needs—like eating, sleeping, or hygiene—just to stay close to the power.
> * Loss of Risk Assessment: The altered mind loses its capacity to fear danger. A corrupted mage will willingly burn out their own lifeforce, sacrifice loved ones, or cross forbidden boundaries because the concept of "consequence" no longer registers.
>
> Identity & Psychological Fragmentation
>
> * The "Whispering" Effect: The mana acts as a conduit for an external, malicious force (like an ancient god, a demon, or the collective agony of a ruined land). This manifests as a parasitic secondary consciousness or an intrusive "voice" that slowly rewrites the victim's core morals and memories.
> * Temporal Disorientation: Because raw or corrupted mana interacts heavily with the fabric of reality, a corrupted mind might live in multiple timelines simultaneously. They might hold conversations with dead ancestors, react to events that haven't happened yet, or forget their own current identity entirely.
> * Erosion of Empathy (Apathy): The user grows completely detached from the living world. They begin to view other living beings not as people, but as mere "fuel cells," vessels for mana, or obstacles in their path.
