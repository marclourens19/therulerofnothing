# Chapter 10 – Design

Started 2 October 2026. **Status:** a direction is proposed and round 1 has been asked. Nothing below is decided unless it's marked as decided.

## Whose chapter

**Seralune's.** This is already decided: the author's Chapter 9 working design puts the two recovery days "in the next Alaric chapter, after Seralune's Chapter 10". She was last on the page at the end of Chapter 7, and Chapters 8 and 9 were Alaric's.

## Where she stands (from the page)

- **When and where:** late morning on Day 2, at the arch of the old aqueduct under the palace, below the old south stair. She can't hear the falls any more.
- **The lamp and the hands:** Nereth holds the lamp in her left hand. Seralune has taken Nereth's right hand with her own left. Her cut is on her right palm, from Chapter 4.
- **What she's wearing:** servant's grey, a shawl and borrowed shoes. She hasn't slept since she woke ("You can hardly stand." / "I'm standing.").
- **The route:** Nereth knows the channels "as far as the first turning. After that, I don't."
- **Cyrandor:** Seralune turned back for Nereth ("Let her go."). Cyrandor then took the guard's wrist, put them both through the iron door, locked it and dropped the key down the drain. He stayed on the other side. Thaer sent someone to bring Cyrandor to him, and nobody knows what happens to him. At the arch, Nereth wouldn't talk about him: "Not yet, Your Highness. Please."
- **Thaer:** he let her go. He won't force her back and won't go with her: "Go. Before Leorin comes down himself."
- **Her goal:** Mydea and Inrandeel, where her mother went "to find a way for you to wake and live outside that thing". The last word of her mother is centuries old.
- **Nereth:**
  - Her reason for coming is "I've wanted to see what's past the falls since I was small."
  - Cyrandor secretly told her to watch over Seralune.
  - Her first slip came at the iron door: "Cyrandor, open it. Open the door!"
  - She hasn't used her Fire on the page yet, and she has no corruption.

## The direction (recommended)

**The chapter's question** (from the outline): *What did her imprisonment cost Natharul, and Nereth?*

**It runs** from the arch (Day 2, late morning) to first light on Day 3. That's the same night as Alaric's vision in Chapter 9, and nothing on the page needs to say so.

**Six movements, rough:**

1. **Into the old channels.** Nereth's knowledge runs out at the first turning. Beyond it the channels are older, and Seralune knows their shape from her own time, the way she knew the south stair. For once her old life is useful instead of only lost. Neither of them mentions Cyrandor, but he's on both their minds.
2. **What's down there.** The old aqueduct isn't empty. A thousand years of her mana leaking from the seal above has changed what grows and lives in the water. It's beautiful at first, then wrong. The page shows it and never explains it: she doesn't know it's hers. *(Your call.)*
3. **The choice.** Something down there is suffering, and she goes to it. Nereth warns her, Seralune decides anyway, and Nereth follows rather than let her go alone. It's the same move as "Let her go." in Chapter 7: her compassion decides a risk that someone else pays for.
4. **The cost.** Nereth has to use her Fire, for the first time on the page.
   - Seralune is holding on to her and is afraid for her.
   - Her mana can't be used, but it acts on its own, and it goes into Nereth's fire.
   - The fire is far beyond anything Nereth can make, and it burns through the old channels.
   - The corruption begins below Nereth's left eye (her established look, handoff §6.6). *(Your call: how it enters.)*
5. **The slip.** Nereth is in pain, with something in her that isn't hers, and she turns on Seralune over Cyrandor: he stayed behind because Seralune turned round. It's Revy at full strength for the first time. Seralune doesn't defend herself.
6. **Out.** The channels are coming down. Seralune gets Nereth out herself. It's the first time she's the one protecting someone, after Thaer, Cyrandor and Nereth protected her. Behind them the palace has felt it, and its bells are ringing. They come out on the mountainside above the coast at first light, and Nereth sees what's past the falls.

**Seralune's belief.** "How could I be dangerous? I never had magic." Where this first appears on the page is still open. This chapter is where she can think it, and then have it broken.

## From the old Chapter 10, and what changes

The old chapter is `Old - Before Re-plan/Volume 1 - Rewrites/Chapter 10 - Beneath Natharul (Rewrite).md`, and its design is `Old - Before Re-plan/Chapter Design/Chapter 10 - Story Design.md`.

**Kept, updated to the decisions:**
- the aqueduct under the palace;
- beauty turning wrong;
- her compassion doing harm;
- Nereth's corruption beginning;
- Nereth's accusation;
- Seralune carrying her out;
- dawn above the coast, with the bells behind them.

**Changed:**
- **Cyrandor's arm.** He doesn't lose it in the new Chapter 7. He stays behind a locked door, and nobody knows what happens to him. Nereth's anger rests on that now.
- **Where the corruption comes from.**
  - In the old chapter, a creature bit Nereth, and then the growth drew Seralune's mana through her.
  - *Recommended:* the corruption comes from Seralune directly, because the rule is that her mana works properly only through Alaric, and through anyone else it corrupts. A creature can still be the reason Nereth needs her Fire.
  - This keeps the cause in Seralune, which is her Volume 1 arc ("her own actions endanger people"). It also means "her feelings make it flare" follows from how it began.
- **The untouched hollow.** In the old chapter, the fire leaves a perfect untouched circle round Seralune, echoing the green circle in Chapter 1. *Recommended:* cut it. It hands the reader the answer (principle 5), and the volume's rule is that the two stories meet through cause and effect, not matching signs.
- **The scale.** In the old chapter, the southern slope tears open into a crater above the coast, and Natharul shakes.
  - *Recommended:* smaller, but public. A stretch of the old channels burns and falls in, and smoke comes out of the south slope. The palace feels it and rings its bells, and nobody above ground dies.
  - A disaster that size in Chapter 10 would compete with the tear, the volume's catastrophe.
  - The public part is what matters: Natharul now has its own reason to call her dangerous.
- **"Seralune".** In the old chapter, Nereth calls her by name for the first time when she thinks they'll die, then goes back to "Your Highness". *(Your call, for the dialogue round.)*
- **Not carried over without your yes:** the corpses with roots through them, the pale salamander-like creature, and the child-like cry used as a lure.

## What could make this chapter 100/100?

- **Cause, not accident.** The weakest version is "a monster bit Nereth". The strongest is Seralune's choice, her mana and her fear. The page never says so, and the reader can add it up.
- **Nereth as a person, not an injury.** Her own wants need to be on the page before she's hurt: Cyrandor, and seeing the world.
  - The dawn above the coast gives her wonder, at a price.
  - This answers the open question in `Decisions.md`: "Should Volume 1 still give her real moments of wonder?"
- **Not a tunnel crawl.** Each movement changes something between them: the silence about Cyrandor, Seralune leading, the choice, the cost, the slip, the carry.
- **Horror from rules and absence** (design bible §19.3). Show what the place does, not a list of monsters.
- **Her thoughts reason** (her rule from Chapter 5). At the biggest moment, she adds up the evidence and flinches.
- **Risks:**
  - The corruption's look and spread have to start small, so the rest of the volume can grow them.
  - The fire mustn't look like Seralune using her mana. It acts on its own.
- **Length:** a day and a night, six movements, and two people pushing on each other. *Estimate:* 4,500–5,500 words.

## Round 1 (2 October)

1. **Seralune, from the arch to first light on Day 3?** *Recommended:* yes.
2. **Has the aqueduct been changed by a thousand years of her leaking mana?** *Recommended:* yes. It answers half the chapter's question (what her imprisonment cost Natharul), and fits the handoff's reading that the world's corrupted mana relates to her.
3. **Does Nereth's corruption begin here, from Seralune's own mana?** *Recommended:* yes, from Seralune's mana going into Nereth's fire while Seralune holds on to her, not from a bite.
4. **Does the turn come when she goes to something suffering, against Nereth's warning?** *Recommended:* yes. What it is, is open: the old creature, or something of yours.
5. **Does Nereth's slip turn on Cyrandor?** *Recommended:* yes. The line comes in the dialogue round.
6. **The scale and the ending.** *Recommended:* smaller but public (bells, smoke, no deaths above ground). They come out above the coast at first light, and it's Nereth's first sight of what's past the falls.
