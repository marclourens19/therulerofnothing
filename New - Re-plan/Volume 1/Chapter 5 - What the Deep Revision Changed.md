## Why it isn't 4,000

The design budgeted about 4,300 words. Written the way you want (plain, no decoration, nothing explained), everything agreed came to just under 2,900. The fourteen calls above bring it to 3,208, and going further would have meant padding, which you've never wanted. The estimate was wrong, not the target. The lesson for Chapter 6 on is that a chapter's length comes from how much happens in it, so the design has to plan enough happening from the start.

**What would bring it to 4,000 with real content.** Each of these needs something only you can give, because it's backstory:

- a. **A memory of Thaer from before.** For example, when her knees give on the stairs and he catches her: something he always did for her. That's the Shoko side of them, and it would make tonight's Thaer hurt more.
- b. **A memory of her mother,** in the night, beside *Mother would never let them do this to me*.
- c. **Leorin young.** One thing she remembers about her cousin, at the moment she knows him.
- d. **More of the changed palace** on the walk up, from your picture.

*Recommended:* a and b. The title is the shape of what's missing, and the people she's missing are her brother as he was and her mother.

**Answered (29 September):** "No, fine as is." No memories are added, and the chapter stays at about 3,200 words. The 4,000–5,000 target applies from Chapter 6.
