# Chapter 3: Design

Started 27 September 2026. **Status:** round 1 of questions is waiting on the author. This is the plan for Chapter 3, agreed one question at a time before any writing, following the `chapter-rewrite` skill. Most of the old material changes, so the plan comes first.

## Where Chapter 2 leaves it

- Gerolt is dead, kneeling in the firelight by the river. His last sound was "Heh", when he saw Silas.
- Silas is dragging Alaric backwards through the trees, away from him. Alaric still has Gerolt's sword, and Wena has followed them.
- More horns are sounding along the river. Two riders are burned and three cut down, but more are coming.
- Liluth escaped maimed and heard Alaric shout, so Natharul knows Gerolt wasn't alone.
- Gerolt's house is burning. The neighbours two fields over saw the fire, and what happens to them is still open.
- Alaric knows his name. Gerolt's last instructions were: "Kelmend. Over the river. Marta, at the inn by the south gate. Show her that. Tell her the old fool sent you." and "Take Wena and run, Alaric."
- Alaric doesn't know who the man is. He saw the man's face change at the sword, and he saw Gerolt laugh once at him.

## Already fixed by decisions

- **Whose eyes.** Stay in Alaric's eyes when the chapter is about him.
- **Silas** is Gerolt's pupil from Avarice, Eminent Fire, the same level as Gerolt.
  - He takes Guts's pragmatism in brutality, and he's very cunning.
  - He's harsher than Gerolt and always wants to win, by any means.
  - He acts instead of thinking. He wants to fix the past, and he has to learn that it can't be fixed.
  - Underneath, he's a loving man who wants to protect his comrades.
  - He lives with twenty years of guilt over the gorge, where his squad and Marta's father died. He and Marta were going to marry.
  - He lives on the river to stay away from people, near Kelmend for news of Marta.
  - He knows his master's sword on sight. He says "boy", and Gerolt said "lad".
  - He will teach Alaric the sword later.
- **Alaric.**
  - When he's frightened, he has Subaru's mouth, running inside his head. It spills out loud only when he's cornered.
  - He thinks a lot and keeps his emotions to himself instead of asking for help.
  - He's kind, smart and caring, and not afraid to act.
  - He believes things are right when they aren't.
  - He wants to learn who he was.
- **Wena** is Gerolt's dying gift, "almost a wish to keep her safe". She stays by Alaric.
- **The world.**
  - Natharul punishes a place for a death ("They don't ask who did it. They ask where.").
  - Mydea is ruled with an iron fist, and people give each other up for food and gold.
  - Natharul scouts are everywhere in Mydea but never seen. That's the agreement.
- **The writing rules from Chapter 2:**
  - add meaning where he'd feel it, small and concrete, never melodramatic;
  - the narration follows his attention;
  - don't tell the reader what they already know;
  - plain, common words, and no "the dark";
  - important lines are designed together (the author gives their version, Claude gives it back in the character's voice);
  - no comparisons he couldn't make.

## Questions

### Round 1 (asked 27 September)

1. **When Seralune comes in (the author's question).**
   - *Recommended:* Chapter 4 is hers, a whole chapter in her eyes, and Chapter 3 stays Alaric's to the end.
   - Her seal broke at the moment Alaric arrived on the Silent Field (old canon), so her first chapter goes back half a day, to midday. It opens at the instant "the dark had been white", which sits beside Alaric's Light in Chapter 1 without anyone explaining it.
   - The old version put her at the end of Chapter 3. That would break "stay in Alaric's eyes", and cut short the chapter where his grief and Silas have to land.
   - *The risk:* readers who have spent three chapters with Alaric may skip her. So her chapter needs its own hook, its own danger and its own deadline, as old Chapter 4 had: the court means to seal her again by dawn. That's for Chapter 4's design.
2. **What Chapter 3 covers, and where it ends.**
   - *Recommended:* the rest of the night, from being dragged away to first light at Silas's home.
   - It ends on a hook: Silas hearing the name "Marta".
   - Kelmend, the gate and the inn come later.
   - *Alternative:* push on to Kelmend's gate by the end, which squeezes old Chapter 6 into this one.
3. **The river and Wena.** They still have to get over the river, with riders along it.
   - *Recommended:* keep the old beat and sharpen it. Wena is swept away, Silas says to leave the dog, and Alaric lets go and goes in after her anyway.
   - It's his first choice without Gerolt, and it goes against the man who just saved him. The reason is Gerolt's last wish ("Take Wena").
4. **How his grief shows.**
   - *Recommended:* it comes out first as anger at Silas ("You left him there"), the way you shaped the last stand ("sadness into anger that he has nothing again").
   - The tears come only when he's alone, because he keeps things to himself.
   - What Silas says back is a line to design together.
5. **What Silas gives away, and what he keeps back.**
   - *Recommended:* he gives his name, a small answer, because Alaric will ask who he is.
   - Alaric can see that he knew Gerolt. Silas says nothing about being Gerolt's pupil, or about Avarice, the gorge or Marta.
   - Why he helps stays unspoken: first his master's sword, then, once Alaric says it, the name "Marta".
6. **Silas's home.** "He lives on the river to stay away from people." What is it? For example, a hut on the bank, a boat, or a hollow in the rock like old Chapter 6's hideout. Your call.
7. **What Alaric works out about the world.** These are small answers.
   - *Recommended:* Silas gives one line about Mydea's guards handing him over by sunrise.
   - Alaric works out the other part himself: why Gerolt looked at the neighbours' window and then rode for the trees.
   - The honest tension is that the reader may already have worked it out. But it's the moment Alaric understands what Gerolt did, which is the kind of meaning you asked for. Keep it, or leave it for the reader?
