# Volume 3 Picture

A draft in progress, started 26 September 2026. It's assembled only from the decisions in `Decisions.md`. Anything marked *Open* isn't decided yet.

## Where it starts

- **Alaric, his friends and Darcy** have crossed the sea to Kozmagar. Mydea is at war, with beastfolk landing on its shores. His group is wanted there for killing soldiers and stealing the kingdom's strategist.
- **Alaric and Freya** have become mutual.
- **Seralune and Nereth** have escaped the church's captivity and are heading for the same continent.

## What's decided

- **The Time bearer** is on the move, searching for Alaric. He feels he needs to find him and wants to understand why his predecessor loved him. "Time is broken": the displacement left a thousand-year seam, and the tear reopened it.
- **Alaric and Freya** become something serious.
- **Seralune and Nereth** arrive on the same continent, and the two threads converge.
- **Alaric and Seralune meet at the end of the volume.**

## Open

- The image of the meeting.

- Darcy in Kozmagar, and what she chooses.
