# Volume 1 Picture

The one-page shape of Volume 1. It was approved as a base on 26 September 2026 and brought up to date on 6 October, after Chapter 10, and on 9 October with the Chapter 11 working design. It's assembled only from `Decisions.md`, and where the two differ, `Decisions.md` wins. Anything marked *Open* isn't decided yet.

## The question

**What is your worth when everyone tells you that you're nothing?**

Volume 1 tests the question; it doesn't answer it.

- **Alaric:** the world tells him he's nothing, Empty.
- **Seralune:** the world tells her she's dangerous, and decides her life "for her own good".
- *Reading to confirm:* by the end, each believes the verdict.

## What the reader should feel

- Something terrible happened a thousand years ago.
- People are suffering, places are destroyed, and people are racist, "magicist" and cruel.
- There is danger.
- "I need to know more. Maybe, just maybe, the next chapter will answer it."
- Small answers come constantly; the big ones are withheld.

## The world underneath

These are the author's decisions. None of them is explained on the page in Volume 1.

- **The world is fading.** God is dead, or may never have existed.
  - The world put much of its remaining energy into an attempt to correct its decline.
  - Alaric and Seralune are the result of that attempt.
- **One soul, two people.**
  - They were born at the same moment and share one soul, but they are not the same person.
  - The shared soul never makes them love each other.
- **The ancient pair.**
  - A thousand years ago they loved each other, and were married or about to be.
  - Their failure was mutual: each believed they could save everyone alone, and decide for others.
  - Their promise doesn't bind the present two.
- **The thousand years** is a random distance, rounded to a thousand. The ancient Time bearer sent Alaric forward to save him. Seralune's seal broke at the instant he arrived, because her mana went searching for him.
- **Eight Rulers,** one living bearer per domain. The Veiled four are Light, Dark, Spirit and Time; the Elemental four are Fire, Water, Wind and Earth. Thaeroval is the Ruler of Dark.
- **Mana and 『Affinity』.** Mana is the fuel, and an Affinity shapes it. "Magic" is the old word, from before the laws. 『Faint』 is the lowest rank. Every child is tested at ten, and the Faint are taken.

## Alaric

- **Starts.** He wakes on a silent battlefield knowing nothing. He knows what things are, but has never experienced any of them.
  - Gerolt Warde, a retired Eminent Fire user who only wanted peace with Wena, takes him in. "Empty" is the word they reach between them.
  - Gerolt gives him his sword, then dies in a last stand of fire.
- **Wants:** to learn who he was.
- **Flaw:** he carries everything alone, and believes things are right when they aren't.
- **What he wins:** companions who want to help him grow. The heart of the volume is the travelling group becoming the place he belongs (the *Final Fantasy XV* road trip). They can doubt his secrets, or the danger following him, without ceasing to care.
- **The double.**
  - The world makes an image of him that accuses him of being evil and responsible for deaths. It drives his hunger for his past, and seeing it can bring severe headaches.
  - It isn't the ancient Alaric returning, and only he sees it.
  - It first appears in a false-awakening dream, his first night at Marta's inn (Chapter 9).
- **Five phases:**
  1. **Displacement.** He wakes knowing nothing; Gerolt dies for him.
  2. **Entanglement.** The road, the world's cruelty, friends, and clues to his past. He believes that if he finds who he was, he'll know what he's worth.
  3. **Reversal.** The past answers him with almost nothing. The ancient cores come from the war against the princess history calls evil, and give him no biography.
  4. **Doubling down.** He decides his identity is his responsibility alone. He investigates, withholds and decides by himself, and comes to believe that wherever he goes, people die.
  5. **Consequence.** He reaches for his past without his companions, and the tear passes through him.
- **What he misses:** the life he's living now, with friends who care about him.
- **Ends:** carrying it alone. He believes both "I am nothing" and "I killed thousands."

## Seralune

- **Starts.** She wakes alone in the black, in a broken crystal seal. Nobody tells her what was done to her, so she puts the pieces together:
  - her cousin Leorin grown old;
  - the royal tree grown huge;
  - an attendant nobody has heard of;
  - "the seal" overheard through a door.

  Later she's told it was for her own good. She doesn't remember deciding anything, so it scares her and she rejects it.
- **Her mana.**
  - She has more of it than anyone has found the end of, and she can't use it at all. It's only usable through Alaric.
  - It acts on its own, and her feelings move it.
  - Magic can't touch her, or anyone she's holding, though ordinary harm can.
  - For a thousand years it leaked from the seal into the channels under the palace, changing what lives there and killing the servants sent down.
  - She knows none of this.
- **Wants, deep down:** a world where no one fears those with nothing, or her. A world where people don't have to choose, because they have everything they need. This is the seed of her antagonist arc.
- **Wants, in Volume 1:** to escape the system that is locking her away, and to get answers from her mother, who opposed the sealing.
- **Believes:** "How could I be dangerous? I never had magic." *That line is saved for a later chapter.*
- **Arc:** people accuse her; then her own actions endanger people; then she comes to believe *she* is the problem. Her danger grows from her compassion, never from madness.
- **Her road:**
  - She escapes the palace (Chapter 7) and the channels beneath it (Chapter 10).
  - She goes into Mydea on her mother's trail. The queen's last word came from Inrandeel, a forest in Mydea.
  - The independent elves she remembers there are gone, and something there points her on to Favale.
- **Ends.** She doesn't remember Alaric. She accepts responsibility and resolves to atone for deaths she didn't cause, and she and Nereth are held by the Holy bearer. The church keeps her alive as leverage over Natharul.

## Around them

- **Silas.**
  - Gerolt's pupil from Avarice, Eminent Fire, brutal and cunning. He answers practical questions and evades personal ones.
  - He's married to Marta, and they've been separated since the gorge, where her father died under his command.
  - He reaches Gerolt's last stand in time to see him die, and drags Alaric out. He will teach him the sword.
  - His mistake in Volume 1: he chooses brutality, and leaves an easy trail.
- **Marta.**
  - Gerolt's niece. She keeps the busiest inn in Kelmend, just inside the South Gate, and is a hub of information that she shares with Avarice.
  - She is blunt, worldly and used to being obeyed (her voice is from Baderon).
  - She takes Alaric in and keeps Silas out.
  - She has long wanted to free Darcy, and never managed it. Darcy's transfer towards the frontier may be her last chance (Chapter 11).
- **Redd.** Low Earth, hates elves, raised his sister on the road. He's warm and funny, and the group's emotional support and connective tissue, but too carefree when it matters.
- **Freya.**
  - Redd's younger sister: untested, secretly Eminent Water, and doesn't know it. She hates fighting.
  - She wants her brother to find peace, and has to learn to be her own person.
  - She falls for Alaric. He doesn't see it, because he's looking backwards.
- **Wena.** Gerolt's farm dog, given to Alaric with the sword as Gerolt dies. She stays by Alaric, and knows Marta from Gerolt's visits.
- **Nereth.**
  - Seralune's maid: Ram on duty, Revy when she slips. Fire.
  - Cyrandor, her mentor, told her to watch over the princess. She chose to go with Seralune, wanting to see what lies past the falls.
  - Her corruption begins under the palace (Chapter 10), from a corrupted creature's bite below her left eye.
  - How a servant's Fire opened the side of a mountain is still to be explained, and later chapters must respect its scale.
- **Cyrandor.** Keeper of the queen's Order, and the first person who answered Seralune's questions. He stayed behind to stop the guards. His fate is unknown.

## Against them

- **Thaeroval.**
  - He loves his sister, and believes he must choose for her: he wants her safe in his "chains". He regrets the ancient sealing and hoped their father would change his mind.
  - In Chapter 7 he lets her go, within limits he sets himself.
  - When he meets Alaric he spares him and feels nothing at all. Much later in the series, he slowly loses his emotions until he becomes flat.
- **Leorin.** Her cousin, now head of the court, and old. He insists on resealing her, because he sees protecting the kingdom as his duty.
- **Natharul.**
  - Its king, her father, flew to Mydea on his warship to see a battlefield with dead elves on it. He believes Mydea killed his people.
  - A second, regional destroyer came over Kelmend searching for the farm. The search is now for the boy and the big dog that went into the forest with Gerolt, to be brought in alive for questioning.
  - Its scouts are always present in Mydea, and never seen.
- **Liluth.** The Natharul scout who escaped Gerolt maimed, and who pursues Alaric into the Great Expanse.
- **Brand.** The Ruler of Fire, about Alaric's age. He has killed some of Silas's comrades in Avarice. He first appears in Kelmend in Chapter 11.

## The road

- **Length:** forty or more chapters is fine, governed by completed movements. *The 50–54-chapter map in `Proposals/Volume 1 Structural Map.md` is discussion, not canon.*
- **Kelmend:** Marta's inn, "Empty", Redd and Freya, Fort Kelmend and Darcy.
- **The Great Expanse:** a vast, shifting land of corrupted mana. Night is especially dangerous there, and people who try to live in it can be warped.
- **Foramen:** a hidden settlement tied to Kurdag, a wolf beastman. Alaric may briefly believe he belongs there. Liluth's pursuit brings violence to it, and some want his party gone.
- **Favale:** the tear.

## The tear

- **Where and when:** Favale, on the Longest Light. The names, characters and lore around it will change when we reach those chapters.
- **Both of them reach at once:** he for his past, she for him. Her power passes through him, and he feels it.
- **The cost:** thousands die, and the world blames Seralune. Nereth survives, close to her. Thaeroval is cut off from the centre.
- **What people know:** no one knows the truth, not even Alaric, but he feels it was his fault. The reader suspects, and is never told.
- **Two internal wars, each with its own shape:**
  - His: "Why me, when I'm empty?"
  - Hers: "Why must I? But I should."

## What can never go back

- Thousands are dead.
- Seralune is hated for something she didn't choose.
- He believes he's nothing and a killer; she believes she's the problem.
- *Open:* what changes in the world because of it (the church, Natharul, Mydea).

## Promise into Volume 2

- Darcy's rescue, with his friends.
- Alaric coming to terms with himself, and learning to accept help.
- Seralune held as the church's leverage over Natharul. Volume 2 becomes her escape, decided together with Nereth.
