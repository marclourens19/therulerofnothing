# Chapter 28 — Story Design

_Draft for Joshua's approval, 23 September 2026. Built from ten rounds of Joshua's answers, under his direction for the final arc: a living world, brutal and realistic, characters at their best, more characters talking, and web/light-novel rhythm. Nothing is drafted as prose until this design is approved._

---

## Working Status

- **Viewpoint:** Alaric, close third throughout.
- **Working title:** open (ten options after the chapter is written).
- **Places:** the river under the aqueduct; **Idony's door** (the Keeper's house in the Library's river wall); the Library's public halls at night; the Vault stacks; **the drowned arch and the reading chamber**; the long conduit under the city; the river below **the Lily Steps**.
- **Time:** the night of Day 7, straight on from the water stair in Ch 26 (the same night Seralune is on the laundry roof), until **dawn on Day 8**, the same dawn she watches the saint in Ch 27. **The Longest Light is four days away.**
- **Length:** as long as it needs [Joshua], likely 8,000–10,000 words, split for posting if needed.
- **Style targets:** 30% or more dialogue; about 16 words a paragraph; almost none over 80.
- **Action:** one realistic ordeal (the long swim), with no fight. **Nobody is hurt, barely** [Joshua].

## Joshua's Decisions (locked, ten rounds)

**Getting in**

1. **It opens that night, at Idony's door.** The group comes straight from the water stair: four fugitives and a dog after curfew, with the four dead in Helmi's house not yet missed.
2. **Idony opens the door and says "You're late."** Thirty years, and she says it as though she'd set a time.
3. **Idony helps completely.** She's **waited thirty years to be asked**, and the debt was never the reason. She's also **devout and afraid**: helping them costs her faith, not only her post.
4. **Helmi doesn't tell her about Darcy yet.** He saves it for afterwards, if there is an afterwards. **He keeps the small cup** in his pocket (for Ch 31).
5. **The way in is a combination.** Idony walks them into the Library through her own door as her copyists. **The last stretch isn't a door: the pool itself is the way in.** You reach the reading chamber by going under the surface of the still water, through a drowned arch.

**The Library (the living world)**

6. **The public halls:** painted ceilings of the Light's victories, pilgrims' names scratched into the benches, and lamps kept burning all night.
7. **The Vault stacks:** rows below the river, dry as bone, catalogued in Helmi's old hand. **His writing is still on the labels.**
8. **Night people:** copyists and priests at vigil who work through the night, and **a cat**. The Library is never empty.
9. **The pool room:** older than the Library and older than Favale, in a stone nobody in Mydea ever carved. **It feels like the hill** (the same wrongness Seralune feels in Ch 27; readers can connect them).

**The reading**

10. **Everyone has to be perfectly still.** The water has to forget them after the swim. Helmi lowers the core into the pool on a cord, and nobody may move or speak, not even Wena. The stillness is the tension.
11. **The water shows a battle:** **the war machines alive** (the Silent Field's machines, whole and moving), banners, and beastfolk among the humans, **then the end: light, and then nothing.** The core's first mana ends there.
12. **Alaric sees nothing but water.** Everyone else sees the battle. He sees only water, and **nobody asks him.** The secret keeps itself.

**The page**

13. **The ending matches.** One chronicle's record of the Great Remaking stops with the same light, then nothing. Only one page shows what came before it.
14. **The page is forbidden.** It's on the list of things no Keeper shows anyone, and **Idony breaks her oath** by opening it.
15. **The record of two figures:** a crowned elven woman with her face defaced, and beside her a figure scraped out of the page. **Who the woman is stays unclear** [Joshua]: no ring, no lily, no hair.
16. **Looking at the scraped place hurts Alaric**, like pressing on a bruise that isn't his.
17. **Helmi and Idony work out together, arguing like old partners, that no tool made either mark.** The core is **a thousand years old**, and both of them can prove it.

**The watch**

18. **The search reaches the Library by chance.** After the four dead are found at the change of watch, the search crosses the river, and the Library is on the list.
19. **Idony faces them.** From below, they hear her swear **"By the Light"** that there's no one in the Vaults. It's the worst thing she's ever done, and her faith breaks on the page.
20. **She's believed, but they're trapped.** The watch post men on every door until a priest comes with a seal at dawn to search the Vaults. The only way out is back through the water, by the long conduit.
21. **Helmi can't follow.** The long swim would kill him. **Redd chooses to stay with him** (he killed a boy for Helmi last night and won't leave him now), and **Wena stays with Redd.** Idony can hide two people and a dog in the Keeper's rooms.
22. **Alaric goes first** into the water to find the way, and **Silas follows him**, for once. This is Alaric's decision that changes the chapter.
23. **Nobody is hurt, barely.** It comes down to one breath under the water.

**Dawn**

24. **They come up in the river below the Lily Steps at dawn.** They climb past a burned ruin, and **Alaric doesn't see the carved lily** (it's too dark, and he's too cold). Only readers know where they are.
25. **There's no voice in this chapter.** The only thing he gets is water.
26. **Silas sees the healing.** The knot came off in the swim, and the cut that was open to the bone last night is a pale line.
27. **Final image: Silas takes Alaric's wrist, turns the palm up to the light, and looks at it. "How long?" He waits for the answer.**

## Current Position (handoff from Ch 26)

- **The water stair, Day 7, after the raid.** Four temple watchmen are dead on Helmi's floor, beside the bowls and bread of the supper. They'll be **missed at the second bell after midnight.** Silas has the sergeant's book.
- **Helmi** carries **the core** in Alaric's satchel, **the small cup** in his pocket, and **Darcy's blue coat** folded inside his own.
- **Alaric's right palm and forearm:** opened to the bone by the frightened boy's blade, and already closed within the hour. He's re-tied **Redd's double knot** over it so no one will know.
- **Redd** killed the crying boy with his father's sword, and his Earth heaved the kitchen flagstones. He's quiet.
- **Silas** has a cut on his cheekbone he hasn't mentioned.
- **The core:** a clear shell as big as two cupped hands and as thick as a thumb, with a black centre hanging in it. It looks like a fisherman's glass float. "The first mana poured into a core leaves an impression, and water will hold its shape, if the water is still enough." The Vault pool "has been still for longer than Favale has had a name."
- **Idony:** Keeper of the Vaults. She was given Helmi's keys the morning after they were taken from him. They "came up together, two junior archivists with ink on our cuffs." She held Darcy the day she was born. The silver-cup debt: "She let me… someone who knows to the drop what she owes."

## Causal Chain

- **What caused this chapter?** Darcy's plan (Ch 7) to bring a core to the Great Library; Helmi's verdict ("It's real… I can't find out here"); the raid, which forces them to go tonight instead of "first light, tomorrow".
- **What becomes possible?** Proof that the core is a thousand years old; the record of two figures; Alaric's secret (he can't see what everyone else sees); Silas knowing about the healing.
- **What becomes impossible?** Helmi going home; Idony keeping her oath; the group staying together.
- **Who behaves differently afterwards?** Idony (faithless and complicit), Silas (knows), Redd (chose Helmi over Al), and Alaric (sure now that something is wrong with him, and still telling no one).

## Scene Plan

### 1. You're late

- Out from under the aqueduct along the river's edge in the dark. Helmi knows the water ways: an eel-catcher's punt under the aqueduct's river pier, and the far bank below the Library's river wall. It's quick, cold and silent. (Proposal: a line of practice lanterns on the far bank shows that the city is still awake.)
- **The Keeper's door:** small, in the river wall, with one lamp. Helmi knocks.
- **Idony** (proposal: small, straight-backed, sixty-five, a sun token worn thin on a cord, ink on her cuffs to this day) opens it, looks at him wet, bloody and hunted after thirty years, and says: **"You're late."**
- **Helmi:** "I was detained." Or something as dry. Their voices are old partners' voices, and the others see who Helmi used to be.
- She sees the blood, the dog and the sword, and doesn't ask. She asks one thing: "What do you need?" **He tells her: the pool. Tonight.** She says yes before he's finished. (Nobody has asked her for anything that mattered in thirty years.)
- **Darcy isn't mentioned.** Readers notice.

### 2. The Library at night

- Idony walks them in as her copyists (cloaks over the blood; Wena "the Keeper's dog"; Silas's sword under a cloak).
- **The public halls:** the painted ceilings of the Light's victories (proposal: a panel of the Great Remaking ending in a painted sun, the church's version); lamps burning all night; pilgrims' names scratched into the benches.
- **Night people:** an old priest at vigil who nods to the Keeper; a copyist asleep over her work; **the cat**, which Wena very badly wants.
- **The Vault stacks:** dry below the river. **Helmi's handwriting is still on the labels.** He stops at one and touches it. Idony: "I never changed them." That's thirty years in a line.
- Redd talks too much from nerves; Silas tells him not to.

### 3. The pool is the way in

- The lowest room ends in water. There's no door: a drowned arch, and dark water under it. Idony: "The only way to the reading pool is through it. The first Keepers made it so."
- **Alaric goes first** with the lamp in oilcloth (proposal). A short, frightening duck under stone, and he comes up in **the pool room.**
- **The pool room:** older than the Library, in stone nobody in Mydea carved, with joins too fine for a blade. It feels like a place he should know, and he doesn't. (Readers link it to the hill in Ch 27.)
- **Everyone has to be perfectly still.** Idony: "It takes an hour for the water to forget you." They sit wet on cold stone and wait, and nobody may move. Wena lies with her chin on Redd's boot. Redd, who can't bear silence, bears it.

### 4. The reading

- Helmi, gloved, lowers the core on a silk cord until it hangs just under the surface. The black centre darkens the water round it.
- **The water shows a battle:** the war machines alive and moving, banners no one living has flown, beastfolk among the humans, all of it silent. **Then light, from one point, and then nothing.** The water is only water again.
- **Alaric sees water.** Only water, the whole time. He watches the others watching something he can't see. **Nobody asks him.**
- Redd: "Al. Al, did you see —" and then he answers himself, because of course Al saw it, and moves on. The secret keeps itself.

### 5. The page

- **The ending matches:** Idony has seen that ending before, in writing. One chronicle of the Great Remaking stops with the same light, then nothing, and the page before it is on the list no Keeper shows.
- **Idony breaks her oath.** She hesitates a long time, then fetches it. (Her faith breaking, in her hands.)
- **The record of two figures:** a crowned elven woman with her face defaced, and beside her a figure scraped out of the page.
- **Alaric looks at the scraped place, and it hurts,** like pressing on a bruise that isn't his. He doesn't say so.
- **Helmi and Idony, together, arguing:** lenses and the silver rod; "Nothing scraped this." / "Then what did?" / "Nothing *made* it. It was never painted." / "It was painted. Look at the edge." Both marks are unnatural; no tool made either.
- **A thousand years:** the chronicle's age and the pool's battle together. Helmi says it aloud. Silas: "So what does that make him?" (He means Alaric's clothes, the field and the core.) Nobody answers.

### 6. By the Light

- A bell above; boots on the Library's stair; the watch at the Keeper's door. **The search has crossed the river by chance.**
- **Idony goes up alone.** Through the Vault's speaking-shaft (proposal) they hear her: **"By the Light the Ruler carries, there is no one below."** It's the words of the hanging's sentence, turned into a lie. Helmi closes his eyes.
- The watch believe her but won't leave. **Men on every door until a priest comes with a seal at dawn** to search the Vaults for form's sake.
- **The split:** Idony comes down white. She can hide two people and a dog in the Keeper's rooms, which even a priest won't search. The only other way out is **the long conduit** from the reading pool to the river, and it's too long for Helmi's lungs. **Redd chooses:** "I'm staying." (He killed a boy for this old man last night.) Wena stays with Redd.
- **Alaric decides to go first,** and Silas follows. Helmi gives Alaric one look and **keeps the core** (it stays with the archive).

### 7. The long water

- The conduit: black, cold, stone close on every side, one rope of air where the roof dips and then none. It's brutal and realistic: lungs, panic, a snagged cloak, Silas's hand on his ankle. **THOOM** (once, under real pressure).
- **Redd's knot tears away** on the stone.
- They come up in the river at dawn, below **the Lily Steps**, half-drowned, and climb out past a burned ruin at the top of the steps. **Alaric doesn't see the lily.** It's too dark, and he's too cold.

### 8. How long

- In the grey on the steps, Silas gets his breath back. He looks at Alaric's hand, where the cloth was.
- **Silas takes Alaric's wrist, turns the palm up to the light, and looks at it.** Last night's cut, open to the bone, is a pale line.
- **"How long?"**
- **He waits for the answer.** (End.)

## Character Movement

- **Alaric:** from carrying the answer to learning that he can't see it. He decides to go first. Ends caught by the only person he couldn't lie to.
- **Helmi:** the archivist at his best (labels, lenses, the argument with Idony). He's left behind by his own body, and still hasn't told Idony about Darcy.
- **Idony:** a woman who waited thirty years to be asked, and gives everything: her oath, her faith, "By the Light".
- **Redd:** chooses Helmi over Al. It's a quiet, grown-up choice.
- **Silas:** follows, for once. Knows.

## Dialogue and Voice Notes

- **Idony and Helmi:** exact, dry old partners who finish each other's objections. Her devotion shows in small things (two fingers to the forehead at the page, a prayer under her breath before she lies).
- **Silas:** economical ("Door.", "Breathe.", "How long?").
- **Redd:** nervous jokes in the stacks, then silence; and the choice, plainly: "I'm staying."
- **Alaric:** few words and careful; the lie is in what he doesn't say.
- **The watch:** tired and procedural at the door above.

## Magic and Lore Guardrails

- **No new mana events.** The pool shows only the core's first mana. There's no Seralune connection in this chapter, and no voice.
- **Alaric sees nothing.** It's unexplained, and nobody on the page theorises about it.
- **The page:** the woman stays unidentifiable. No names, and nothing about the ritual or the erasure is explained. It's evidence, not an answer.
- **The pool room** feels like the hill, but nothing links them on the page.
- **The healing** is seen, not explained.
