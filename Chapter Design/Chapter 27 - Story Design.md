# Chapter 27 — Story Design

_Draft for Joshua's approval, 23 September 2026. Built from ten rounds of Joshua's answers, under his direction for the final arc: a living world (LOTR, FFXIV), brutal and realistic, characters at their best, more characters talking, and web/light-novel rhythm. Nothing is drafted as prose until this design is approved._

- **Working title:** Open (ten options follow the finished chapter)

## Joshua's Decisions (locked, ten rounds)

**Arrival**

1. **Through the Pilgrims' Gate, under Nell.** The watch have stopped pulling hoods now they've hanged their witch. The two women come in with the last pilgrims before curfew and walk the old river road under the western arch.
2. **Nereth sees Nell first** and pulls Seralune away before she can look properly. Seralune learns what happened later, from someone's mouth, somewhere safe, and the delay makes it worse.
3. **The murders, as rumour, the next morning.** The city is saying "the witch's people" killed four of the temple watch at the old archivist's house, with a dog. The watch are out again in force. Only readers know it was Alaric, and Seralune thinks it's somehow her fault too.

**The queen's trail**

4. **Her clue is the closed lily,** her mother's private sign on the signet. Whoever her mother trusted in Favale would have marked their door, stone or grave with it.
5. **It leads to a house of keepers, long ended.** They were **humans**, a human family a Natharul queen trusted, which is unthinkable, and it tells Seralune something about who her mother was.
6. **The house is a ruin.** The lily survives on one stone.
7. **They search together, carefully,** as two pilgrims, keeping to crowds and never speaking Elvish.
8. **How it ends badly: Natharul came for them.** Elves came to Favale with a royal warrant **twenty years ago, during the Mydean Campaign.** The family was taken alive, children too, and the house burned behind them. Nobody in Favale lifted a hand, because Mydea gave them up to keep the peace. The trail ends because Seralune's own family closed it. (Lore: one of the Campaign's aims was the queen's trail. Nobody says so on the page, but readers can work it out, and it quietly answers part of "why Mydea, why then?")
9. **She comes away with nothing at all.** There's only the carved lily and a stranger's story.
10. **She learns it from a priest's warning.** He's **the ruin's own shrine priest,** an old man who keeps the little sun shrine the temple built into what's left of the lily house "to cleanse the ground". He's there every day, so his being there isn't a coincidence. He tells the story kindly, as a lesson about "elf-friends".
11. **She goes numb, and that's the horror.** While she feels nothing, Nereth's marks go quiet. Seralune realises she can protect Nereth by feeling nothing at all, and she chooses it. It's the logic of the seal she escaped, and it's what breaks at the tear.

**The joy and Nereth**

12. **The joy: the lanterns from a rooftop.** The night they arrive, from the laundry's flat roof, they watch the practice lanterns lit across the city. The roads go gold. They eat something warm, and Nereth makes Seralune laugh until she has to stop herself. For one evening, Favale is beautiful.
13. **Nereth reaches first.** After two days of careful distance since the flinch, Nereth takes her hand on purpose on the roof, knowing what it might cost. It isn't fixed, but it's a choice.
14. **Money: Nereth warms water for a laundry.** Her small Fire, the gift the palace hired her for ("I could warm washing water"), earns them a corner and supper. It behaves while Seralune is calm, and part of the joy is Nereth being useful and proud.
15. **Lodging: the drying loft above the laundry,** among lines of wet sheets, with the flat roof above.
16. **Nereth is worse, and the drops are running out.** The marks have spread past her collarbone. Nyana's jar has a few days left in it, and nobody in Favale knows what it is.
17. **Why they stay: to lay Nereth in the saint's shadow** on the Longest Light. It's the only healer left in the world for her, and it's the hope that walks them into the tear. (It pays off Nyana's instruction in Ch 21: "If the marks go hot, the jar won't help you. You find a healer." The marks are hot now.)
18. **The laundress:** hard and fair, a widow running the laundry with her daughters. She has **hidden her youngest son from the stewards** for years (he's never been tested; she knows he'd be Faint). She doesn't ask what Nereth is, because she knows what it is to have someone the law wants.

**The city**

19. **Seralune recognises the hill.** The shape of the land, the hill and the river's bend: there was a place here in her time. The temple sits where something she knew once stood.
20. **Elves in Favale: the envoy's servants.** Natharul palace servants from the old palace run errands with their eyes down. They're elves walking freely in a city that hangs elves, because they belong to Natharul. She recognises the livery, and so does Nereth, who wore it. (Colour: see Small Choice 9. Grey is already taken in Favale.)
21. **The saint at dawn, from the laundry roof:** the small white figure at the top of the western stair, lit from behind, with the sick lying in the temple's shadow. This is the morning after Alaric saw her in Ch 25. "Ruler" catches on her again, and that's where Nereth has to be in four days.
22. **The envoy: street talk only, and who it is stays hidden.** Readers can guess at the reasons, which include the survivor of the field, standing beside the Ruler of Light on the Longest Light, and ordinary Kozmagar campaign business. Seralune never learns who is inside.

**Thaeroval, Alaric, danger**

23. **Two knocks.** At night in the loft, two knocks sound somewhere below. Seralune is on her feet, heart going, with her mother's words in her head, and it's nothing. It foreshadows her brother.
24. **Her mana finds a direction, on the rooftop, during the joy.** It happens the same night as the raid at Helmi's. For a heartbeat her mana pours one way, somewhere across the city and close now, and **Nereth's scars stay quiet under Seralune's hand.** Neither of them understands it. Only readers can match the timing with the blade in Alaric's hand. (Lore: the true connection doesn't corrupt.)
25. **The danger: the laundress's son is taken.** After the murders, the watch search the pilgrims' quarter house by house. They don't find two elves among the sheets. They find the hidden boy, and they take him for testing and service in front of his mother. Seralune watches from behind the sheets, holding herself numb so Nereth won't burn. It's the system, not a fight, and it's brutal.
26. **Mana: only the direction moment.** No new corruptions.
27. **Order: night, dawn, day, night.**
28. **Guardrails: no meeting with Alaric. No reveal of the envoy.**

## Chapter Purpose

1. Bring Seralune into Favale under the body of the woman hanged for her.

**Gained:** a roof, a laundress, one beautiful night, and a hope (the Longest Light).
**Lost:** the queen's trail; Seralune's right to grieve; the laundress's son; the last ease of pretending her mana is manageable.

## Scene Architecture

### Scene 1 — Under the Arch

**Opening image:** the last pilgrims squeezing through the Pilgrims' Gate at dusk, and the watch not pulling hoods any more, bored and looking elsewhere. (It's the image of their safety, bought with Nell's death, which readers know.)

**Required developments:**

- The gate, the crush and the song. Two hooded women pass unexamined.
- The old river road and the western arch. Nereth sees the rope, the grey shift and the ears before Seralune lifts her head. She pulls Seralune into a side street by the arm, hard, without explaining. Seralune: "What —" Nereth: "Walk."
- The evening bell. Shutters come down. They have nowhere to be, and curfew is starting.

**Turn:** they're inside Favale, safe for the worst reason, and Seralune doesn't know it yet.

### Scene 2 — The Laundry

**Required developments (dialogue-led):**

- A laundry in the pilgrims' quarter: coppers, lye, steam, and a widow closing up. She's hard, fair and tired, with two daughters folding.
- Nereth offers what she has: "I can warm water." The laundress's boy usually lights the coppers before dawn, and he's out of sight (planted). Nereth puts her palms to the copper and the water steams. She's proud. Seralune watches Nereth's Fire behave, and stays very calm on purpose.
- Terms: a corner of the drying loft and supper, in exchange for hot water before first light. The laundress doesn't ask what's under Nereth's scarf. (Proposal: the laundress **Agna**, her daughters, and a son **Kit**, about twelve and never tested.)

### Scene 3 — The Lanterns

**Required developments (the joy):**

- The flat roof above the loft, reached by a ladder. The city below: the practice lanterns lit along every banner road, and the old river road going gold. The temple black on its hill against the last light.
- Something warm, perhaps barley and bacon from the laundress's pot, eaten with their fingers.
- **Nereth makes Seralune laugh.** Proposal: stories from below stairs in the palace, like what the kitchens called the young princess, or the time Thaer walked into a laundry line. It's real laughter, and Seralune has to stop herself before it gets too big.
- **Nereth reaches first.** She takes Seralune's hand on purpose, after two days apart. "If it burns, it burns."
- **The direction.** For a heartbeat, Seralune's mana pours one way, out across the city, somewhere close. It's fullness with somewhere to go (the Ch 16 feeling). Nereth's hand is in hers, and **nothing burns.** "What was that?" / "I don't know." (It's the night of the raid. Only readers can match it to the blade in Alaric's hand.)

**Turn:** the one feeling Seralune has that doesn't hurt Nereth, and neither of them understands it.

### Scene 4 — Dawn

**Required developments:**

- Before first light, Nereth heats the coppers. Seralune goes up to the roof alone.
- **The hill.** In the grey light she sees the land properly for the first time: the river's bend and the one hill. She knows it. There was a place here in her time. She can't remember when she stood on it, or why, and the not-remembering is its own chill. (Lore hint: the erasure. Nothing is named.)
- **The saint:** the small white figure at the top of the western stair, lit from behind, with the sick lying in the temple's shadow. "Ruler" catches on her again. She thinks of Nereth lying on that stair.

### Scene 5 — The Coppers

**Required developments (four or five voices):**

- The laundress's daughters gossip over the steam. Seralune learns about Nell here, from their mouths: "They hanged the witch yesterday. An elf out of the temple kitchens, eleven years scrubbing floors. Burned a thatcher from the inside, on the pilgrim road." She understands. It lands in the worst way, delayed and folded into chatter. She counts, and Nereth's scars heat. She holds, just.
- **The murders:** a neighbour at the door with news. "The witch's people" killed four of the temple watch at the old archivist's house last night. They had a dog. The watch are out on every corner. Seralune thinks, *mine too*.
- **The Longest Light:** the laundress, drily: "Four days. Then this whole quarter will stink of pilgrims till harvest." The saint carried down the stair among the sick. Seralune decides: Nereth will be there.
- **The drops:** Nereth's jar. Seralune counts what's left and says nothing.
- **The clue:** one of the daughters mentions "the Lily Steps" in passing (a place, a shrine, an old name nobody thinks about). Seralune goes still. (Proposal: the lily house is found through a street name that has outlived its meaning. That avoids coincidence.)

### Scene 6 — The Search

**Required developments:**

- Together, carefully: two pilgrims in a crowd. They never speak Elvish, and Nereth keeps her scarf close.
- **The living city:** sun basins, the banners, the watch in fours, the pilgrims' quarter hostels, the sick carried to the stair.
- **The envoy's servants:** two elves in Natharul palace livery from the old palace, eyes down, carrying baskets of fruit. They walk freely because they belong to Natharul. Seralune recognises the livery and turns her face away. Nereth's hand tightens: she wore it herself. A human child stares at their ears, and his mother pulls him along. Nobody touches them.
- **Street talk** about the envoy (Joshua: only street talk), with competing theories: the boy from some northern battlefield; standing with the saint on the Longest Light; war business for Kozmagar; "the king bowed first."
- **The Lily Steps:** worn steps down to the river in the old quarter.

### Scene 7 — The Lily House

**Required developments:**

- A ruin: blackened walls, a burned lintel, weeds. Built into the corner is a small sun shrine with a basin and a painted disc, and it's newer than the ruin. **One stone in the wall carries the closed lily,** its petals folded so tightly that the centre almost vanishes. Her mother's sign.
- **The shrine priest:** old, kindly and patient. He sweeps the shrine every day. He sees a pilgrim staring at the stone and tells her the story as a lesson:
  - "Elf-friends. A human family. Kept an elf's secret here for, oh, generations, they say."
  - "In the Campaign, twenty years back, riders came in pale armour with a warrant from their king. The family came out, all of them. The little ones too. Nobody lifted a hand. You don't, against a warrant like that."
  - The pale armour is the escort Seralune saw round the carriage on the pilgrim road (Ch 24). She doesn't need anyone to name her father.
  - "They took them alive. Burned the house behind them. The temple built this after, to clean the ground."
  - "There's a lesson in it. Secrets don't keep in the Light."
- He's kind to her. He blesses her. He has no idea.
- **Nothing is left.** There's no letter, no object and no message, only the stone.
- **She goes numb.** Feeling drains out of her all at once, and it's almost restful. Beside her, Nereth's marks, which were burning, go quiet. Nereth notices first: "They've stopped." Seralune understands what that means. She can keep Nereth safe by feeling nothing, and she chooses it. The design doesn't narrate the irony; the reader feels it.

**Turn:** the trail is dead, killed by her own family, and she has found a way to live with it that is its own kind of prison.

### Scene 8 — The Search House to House

**Required developments (the brutal centre):**

- The watch come through the pilgrims' quarter house by house, after the murders, looking for three men, a dog and the archivist.
- Seralune and Nereth are in the loft among the wet sheets. The watch glance up, see laundry, and don't climb. (This is realistic, and not luck: the search is for men.)
- **They find Kit.** He's hidden under the coppers or behind the lye barrels. A boy of about twelve, untested, when the law says every child is tested at ten. "How old is he? Tested? Where's his paper?" The mother lies, then begs, then says nothing.
- **They take him for testing and service.** It's calm and procedural: "He'll be tested. If he's Faint, he goes where he's placed. That's the law, mother." Cruelty is calm. He's taken in front of her and his sisters.
- **Seralune watches through the sheets, numb.** She doesn't feel it. Beside her, Nereth is crying without a sound, and her marks stay quiet, because Seralune feels nothing.

**Turn:** numbness works, and it's the worst thing she's ever done.

### Scene 9 — Two Knocks

**Required developments:**

- Night in the loft. The laundress still brings supper up the ladder, because the work doesn't stop. She says nothing about the boy. Nereth thanks her. Seralune thanks her too, and hears how flat her own voice is.
- Nereth, quietly: "Are you all right?" / "Yes." Nereth doesn't believe her. (A line or two at most.)
- **Two knocks** sound somewhere below: a shutter, a neighbour, the laundress at a door. Seralune is on her feet before she knows it, heart going, her mother's words in her head: *Two knocks are a warning. They are not permission.* Her brother's sound.
- It's nothing.
- She sits down again, and counts.

**Final image:** a princess counting in a laundry loft in the human capital, four days before the Longest Light. Readers have already met Interlude IV, so they know her brother is on the road.

The shrine priest kindly tells her the lesson: in the Campaign, twenty years ago, white riders came with her king's warrant, took the whole human family, children too, and burned the house, and nobody lifted a hand. That afternoon the watch search the quarter and take the laundress's hidden boy while Seralune watches from behind the sheets, feeling nothing. That night two knocks sound below. She's on her feet with her mother's words in her head. It's nothing. She sits down and counts.**
