# Chapter 25 — Story Design

_Draft for Joshua's approval, 22 September 2026. Built from three rounds of Joshua's answers, under his direction that the coming chapters escalate in intensity, brutality, character writing and questions. Nothing is drafted as prose until this design is approved._

---

## Working Status

- **Viewpoint:** Alaric, close third throughout
- **Working title:** Open (ten options follow the finished chapter)
- **Primary location:** the last miles out of the Expanse, the road south, then Favale: the Pilgrims' Gate, the western market, the western aqueduct and Helmi's street
- **Time:** from the dawn Chapter 23 ended on to the morning of the third day. This is Seralune's Day 5 (the day of the pilgrim-road witch) to her Day 7.
- **Target length:** approximately 5,000–5,500 words
- **Primary register:** relief, then the happiest evening the three of them have had, then a morning that takes it back
- **Combat:** none. A public hanging that nobody stops.

---

## Joshua's Decisions (locked)

1. **Favale is ruled by the church in fact.** The high priests of the sun temple run the city. A king sits in the old palace and signs what he's handed. Atera is the church's greatest asset, which is why she never leaves the temple. Her "protection" in Ch 32 will be a church cage.
2. **The brutal centre: the wrong witch.** The pilgrims' story reached Favale first. The temple watch seizes an elven woman, and the church puts her to death in public as the pilgrim-road witch. Readers know it's the wrong woman, and that it's Seralune's doing.
3. **She's an escaped Natharul slave** who has scrubbed the temple's floors for years. The one elf in the city with most reason to hate Natharul dies for what a Natharul princess did.
4. **She's hanged from the aqueduct.**
5. **Alaric does nothing, and knows it. Redd says "good."**
6. **Alaric keeps the voice to himself.** He tells no one that it said his name.
7. **Meal first, hanging after.** They get their pies and chairs: one real evening of joy, the last easy thing. Next morning, on the way to Helmi's, they walk into the hanging at the western aqueduct, in sight of his street.
8. **Getting in is easy.** Favale is choked with pilgrims, and three filthy men and a dog pass through in the crush. The hunt for "the survivor" hasn't reached the capital. The ease is unsettling.
9. **The sentence is read "by the Light the Ruler carries"**, so the first time Alaric hears of the Ruler of Light it's attached to a killing.
10. **A glimpse of Atera:** a small figure in white, high on the temple steps, far off. Alaric doesn't know who she is.
11. **The ending: Helmi's door, a house kept for two.** He's thin, clean and very quiet. The house is perfectly kept, with a second cup set out and Darcy's coat still on its peg. He opens the door as if he's expecting no one at all.

## Current Position (handoff from Chapter 23)

Chapter 23 ended at dawn on the rock spur. A green line on the southern horizon, a chimney thread above it, Silas: "A day. Maybe less." Then, from the north, the voice: *"——Alaric."* Nobody else turned round.

Carried in:

- **Alaric:** Silas's spare sword; the satchel with the wrapped core, cold and dormant; his knee mended faster than it should have (not remarked on). He hasn't slept properly in two nights. He has never told anyone where his name came from, and now the voice knows it.
- **Redd:** the four claw furrows on his forearm under a sleeve pulled to the knuckles, tied with Silas's double knot; his father's sword. He knows about Marta now. He sat up with Alaric on Night 5, "In case I walk."
- **Silas:** the empty skins; the cord; the lead taken back. He has told them the gorge. He's the only one who has seen Favale.
- **Wena:** thin, sore-footed, attached to Redd.
- **Water:** none. They walk out on nothing.
- **Money:** Silas's. ("I'll have saved it.")
- **Knowledge:** the king of Natharul wants "the survivor of the field" alive. The forty are dead. The voice said his name.
- **Promises:** pies with folded edges ("You have to let them cool first"), and chairs. "You'll have to show me." Alaric told Redd, "I don't know what I like."

**The other thread, for timing (not on the page):** on this same morning, Seralune's mana killed Osric on the pilgrim road. A church rider galloped for Favale with the story of an elf witch with a burned face. When Alaric reaches the gate the next afternoon, the watch are already pulling back women's hoods. Seralune and Nereth are off the road, about two days out. They'll reach the city after the hanging (Ch 27).

## Chapter Purpose

1. Get them out of the Expanse and into Favale, and let the arrival feel like the end of something.
2. Pay off pies and chairs in full. Give Alaric the first thing he knows he likes in this life.
3. Establish Favale on the page: the temple over everything, the king small beneath it, the aqueducts, the pilgrims, the watch in white and gold.
4. Land the brutal centre: an innocent woman hanged for Seralune's act, with Alaric watching.
5. Change a relationship: Redd's "good" and Alaric's silence crack the friendship from both sides.
6. Plant Atera twice: a glimpse at dawn, then her title in a death sentence within the hour.
7. Deliver them to Helmi's door, and show the grief before a word is spoken.

**Gained:** Favale; the meal; something Alaric likes; Helmi found alive.
**Lost:** a woman's life; Alaric's belief that he's someone who steps in; the ease between Alaric and Redd; Alaric's honesty with Redd (the lie at night).

## Causal Chain

**What caused this chapter?**

- **Ch 9:** Darcy's directions: "Ask for Helmi where the western aqueduct meets the old river road." The aqueduct steps and the silver cup (Interlude I).
- **Ch 22:** the pies, the chairs, "a place near the western market".
- **Ch 23:** the edge in sight; the voice's one word; Silas's lesson ("That's who it works on. Someone who doesn't want to hurt you."); Redd's hatred sharpened by what was done to Marta.
- **Ch 24:** the witch story running ahead of Seralune to Favale.
- **Bible:** Natharul burned Redd's village when he was eight and killed his family.

**What becomes possible?**

- **The church has its witch, so it stops looking.** The watch stop pulling back hoods. That's what lets Seralune and Nereth into the city in Ch 27, over a dead woman.
- Seralune can arrive to find the "witch" still hanging on the aqueduct (proposal for Ch 27: the church leaves her there until the pilgrims go home).
- Helmi, and the message.
- Readers know Redd has said "good" to an elf's death for something Seralune did. That's waiting for the day they meet.

**What becomes impossible?**

- Alaric believing that the lesson from Ch 23 makes him someone who stops things. It only works on people who don't want to hurt you, and a crowd does.
- Redd and Alaric going back to exactly how they were in the Expanse.

**Who behaves differently afterwards?**

- **Alaric** carries a death he watched and a lie he told. He looks at crowds differently.
- **Redd** is defensive and hurt that Alaric looked at him like that. He doesn't take it back.
- **Silas** is unchanged, and that's the point: he read the hanging as useful.

## Plain Scene Skeleton

**water at the edge → the road south and the first sight of Favale → the gate, where hoods are pulled back and an elf woman gives Wena water → the place with chairs → pies, and something Alaric likes → the news from the kitchens, which he doesn't follow up → the lie at night → dawn: the saint on the western stair → the aqueduct: the woman from the gate → "by the Light the Ruler carries" → the hanging → "Good." → the walk → Helmi's door**

Compared with the previous five chapters:

- **Ch 20:** stand-off → battle → deliberate kill → dispute → departure
- **Ch 21:** landfall → inspection → farewell → city → road
- **Ch 22:** meal → displacement → shelter → siege and pull → landmark → water
- **Ch 23:** lesson → wrong lead → sleepwalk → confession → the forty → enemy killed → name
- **Ch 24:** fire → talk → riders → mob → death → flight → confession → flinch

Ch 21 was a city arrival too, so this one is built differently: the arrival is quick and easy, and the chapter's weight sits on one evening and one morning. The book has one hanging already (Meren's thief, Ch 21), seen in passing and already dead. This one is watched from the first word of the sentence to the end, by someone who met her the day before.

## Scene Architecture

### Scene 1 — Water

**Opening image:** a bucket of well water tipped over Redd's head by a stranger, and Redd laughing under it. (Not waking and not walking: the chapter opens on relief, and on someone else's kindness.)

**Immediate want:** water, then Favale.

**Pressure:** they walk the last of the sand on empty skins. Alaric keeps not looking north.

**Required developments:**

- Brief: the sand thins, then scrub, then grass. Alaric kneels and puts his hands in it. (A line or two, not a set piece.)
- **The chimney pays off:** a goat farm at the edge, a woman at the well (proposal: a widow, no name given) who draws water for them before they ask and asks nothing back. Wena drinks from the trough until she's sick, then drinks again.
- She tells them what the road's like: pilgrim season, the western road's full, "The saint's been on the steps every dawn, they say." (The first touch of Atera for Alaric, from a stranger, without a name.)
- They sleep in her goat shed. Redd sits up with Alaric again. This beat is only a line or two.
- Alaric listens north in the dark. The voice doesn't come, and its silence is worse.

**Turn:** they're out. Nobody has come after them.

### Scene 2 — The Gate

**Immediate want:** get into the city without being looked at.

**Pressure:** a gate means a watch, and the king of Natharul wants Alaric alive.

**Required developments:**

- **First sight of Favale**, from the western road, late afternoon (Alaric's recognising-nothing register, but he can't look away):
  - The temple on its hill at the centre: gold roofs, with a great stair running down its western face.
  - Aqueducts striding in over the roofs from the hills.
  - The river and its barges.
  - The old palace lower down by the river, smaller than the temple and darker.
  - Silas, asked who lives where: "The king's got the palace." / "And up there?" / "Everything else." (One exchange, and it's the whole of Favale's politics.)
  - Optional: the Great Library in the distance, a grey mass with no gold on it at all (a plant for Ch 28).
- **The Pilgrims' Gate:** the western road joins pilgrim traffic in the last miles. It's a crush: the sick on doors and backs, sun tokens, singing, Faints at the back.
- **The temple watch** (white and gold, the same colours as the church rider in Ch 24) are stopping women, not men. They pull back hoods and scarves and look at ears. A girl cries; her mother holds her still. Three filthy men and a dog go through the crush and nobody looks at them twice. It's easy, and Silas doesn't like it being easy.
- **The woman at the gate (spend warmth before blood):**
  - Inside the gate, temple kitchen servants hand out water to pilgrims from buckets (church charity).
  - One of them has an old burn across one cheek and a scarf tied close. She fills Alaric's cup, then crouches and pours water into her own cupped hands for Wena. Wena drinks and licks her fingers, and she nearly smiles.
  - Her scarf slips. Alaric sees the ear, long and pointed. She sees him see it. She fixes the scarf and looks at him, afraid.
  - He says nothing, and goes on. He's kept her secret, he thinks. That's all.
  - Redd is ahead with Silas, watching the watch, and doesn't see her face.
- **Pilgrims' talk in the crush:** an elf witch on the pilgrim road; she burned a thatcher from the inside; "two of them, one with her face burned black"; she talked like a lady. It means nothing to Alaric. Redd spits.

**Turn:** they're in Favale. Alaric carries one face from the gate that he doesn't think about again that day.

### Scene 3 — Chairs

**Immediate want:** Silas's place, if it's still there.

**Pressure:** none, and that's the danger. This is the warmth the morning spends.

**Required developments:**

- **The place near the western market is still there** (proposal: no sign, a green door, run by a broad woman called **Hild** who looks at Silas and says, "You owe me for a chair.").
- Chairs. Redd sits in one "until somebody complains", and nobody does.
- **Pies with folded edges.** Redd shows Alaric how: bite a hole and let the steam out first. Alaric burns his mouth anyway. Redd is delighted.
- **Alaric likes it.** It's the first thing in this life he knows he likes, as opposed to being grateful for. Pay off "I don't know what I like" from Ch 22 without saying so.
- Silas pays. Silas almost smiles. Wena gets the crusts under the table.
- Redd makes a toast to chairs. It's funny, and the laughter should be real on the page.
- **Optional, for Joshua:** Hild asks after "the girl with the freckles". Silas says, "Kelmend." Redd looks at his plate. It's a small ache inside the joy, and it doesn't stop it.
- **The news, near the end of the meal:** Hild, clearing plates: "Temple watch pulled an elf out of their own kitchens not an hour since. Scrubbed their floors eleven years. Took them two days to remember they had one. They'll have her on the aqueduct by the morning bell." Alaric thinks of a cup of water and a slipping scarf. He doesn't ask which elf, because he doesn't want to know tonight. He wants the evening. (Alaric's wanting, and a choice he'll pay for.)
- Redd reaches for another pie.

**Turn:** the best evening of Alaric's new life, and he protected it by not asking.

### Scene 4 — The Lie

**Short, a few hundred words.**

- A room above Hild's. Two beds, and Redd takes the floor across the door, grinning, in case Alaric walks.
- In the dark, Redd: "Heard her again? Your voice?" Alaric: "No."
- It's the first straight lie he has told Redd. He lies there awake afterwards, listening north. Nothing comes.

**Turn:** Alaric has now chosen a secret over his friend, and the reader knows the voice is Seralune's world calling him.

### Scene 5 — The Saint

**Immediate want:** Helmi's house, early, before the streets fill.

**Required developments:**

- Before dawn, the three of them and Wena cross the western city towards the aqueduct. People are going the other way, uphill, to the temple.
- From a turning, the whole western stair of the temple is in view. The sick lie on it in rows, on doors and blankets. **The sun rises behind the temple, so a long shadow runs down the western stair** as a small figure in white comes out at the top. The square below goes silent.
- **Atera** (glimpse only): very small with the distance, just a white shape. She stands at the top of the stair and looks out over the western city for a moment, the way anyone might look at a morning. Then she goes back in.
- Someone near Alaric whispers "Atera", and someone else hushes them: "The Ruler." It's the first time Alaric hears the word as a title.
- Silas doesn't stop walking.

**Turn:** a girl on a stair, and the whole city holding its breath for her.

### Scene 6 — The Aqueduct

**Immediate want:** get past the crowd to Helmi's street.

**Pressure:** the crowd is between them and the old river road, and it's there for the hanging.

**Required developments:**

- The western aqueduct crosses the old river road on tall arches. A crowd fills the road beneath. The temple watch have cleared a space. A rope hangs from the channel walkway.
- **The woman:** in a grey shift, barefoot, hands tied in front. She has the old burn on her cheek, and whip scars across her shoulders where the shift is torn. Her ears are bare for everyone to see.
- **Alaric knows her.** It's the woman from the gate, the cupped hands. Wena knows her too: she whines and pulls towards the rope, and Redd holds her back without knowing why she's doing it.
- **The sentence** is read by a priest in white with a gold sun on his breast, in a reasonable voice (cruelty is calm): the elf called **Nell** in the temple kitchens, her own name not known; fled her lawful masters in Natharul and sheltered eleven years by the temple's charity; did on the pilgrim road work witchcraft and burn a man of the Four from within. "By the Light the Ruler carries," she is to hang.
- **Alaric does the arithmetic.** She was at the Pilgrims' Gate yesterday afternoon with a bucket, and the witch was two days away on the road the morning before. He knows it isn't her. He knows he could say so. Nobody asks him, and he doesn't.
- **Silas's lesson won't help.** "That's who it works on. Someone who doesn't want to hurt you." Everyone here wants it. There's nothing to turn.
- **The hanging, at the Tobin bar:**
  - They put a blackened sun disc round her neck, the Light withdrawn (proposal).
  - They push her from the walkway. The drop's wrong and it doesn't break her neck. She takes her time dying: her bare feet find the stone of the arch and lose it, and the crowd sings to the Light over it.
  - The camera stays on who watches and who looks away, and on the calm of the priest. There's no catalogue of her body.
  - Alaric doesn't look away, because he thinks that's the least he owes her, and it isn't enough.
- **Redd, quietly: "Good."** He isn't cheering. He means it.

**Turn:** Alaric stood in the crowd like everyone else. Redd, at least, believed in what he was watching.

### Scene 7 — The Door

**Immediate want:** Helmi. Anything but this.

**Required developments:**

- They push through the edge of the crowd. Alaric can't look at Redd.
- **Redd, grounded rather than ignorant:** "Don't look at me like that, Al. You weren't there when they came to ours. I was eight. Nobody hanged anybody for that." Alaric has no answer, because he stood there too. (It should challenge both men, and neither comes out clean.)
- **Silas, cold:** "They've got their witch. They'll stop pulling back hoods." He means it's good for them. Readers hear what it means for Seralune.
- **Helmi's house** stands where the aqueduct meets the old river road, close enough that its front step looks at the arch.
- **Alaric knocks three times and can't make the fourth** (proposal). Four knocks came to Gerolt's door in Ch 1, "slow and deliberate as a funeral bell", and after them the arrows. Three also keeps clear of Seralune's two.
- **The door opens on a house kept for two.** Helmi is about sixty-five, tall, thin, stooped, clean-shaven with care, and very quiet. Behind him the room is perfectly kept, with the table set with a second, smaller cup, and a child's coat on a peg by the door. Darcy was ten when they took her, thirty years ago.
- He looks at three strangers and a dog as if he's expecting no one at all.
- **Last line (proposal):** Helmi: "If it's about the woman on the aqueduct, I didn't see anything."

**Final image:** a whole city that saw nothing, including Alaric, and the only man who can help him saying it first.

## Character Movement

### Alaric

- **Starts:** out of the Expanse, alive, carrying a name the voice shouldn't know.
- **Joy:** finds something he likes, and laughs with his friends.
- **Choice 1:** doesn't ask about the elf from the kitchens, because he wants the evening.
- **Choice 2:** lies to Redd about the voice.
- **Choice 3:** does nothing at the aqueduct, knowing she's innocent.
- **Ends:** at Helmi's door, ashamed and silent, with Redd's "good" in his ears. He's learned that stepping in was never who he was. It was only what he'd done when it was easy.

### Redd

- He's at his best at the meal: funny, generous, teaching Alaric how to eat a pie.
- He's at his hardest at the aqueduct. His hatred is real and earned, and it lands on an innocent.
- He's hurt by how Alaric looks at him, and he doesn't take it back.

### Silas

- He knows Favale and is uneasy that it's easy.
- He gets almost-smiling at Hild's.
- He's cold at the aqueduct and useful after it. The unchanged one.

### Nell

- One kindness, then her death. Readers should feel that she was a person. She's given no speech and no last words (proposal): she looks for someone in the crowd and doesn't find them.

### Helmi

- Seen for one moment. Grief as order: the second cup, the coat.

## Dialogue and Voice Notes

- **Alaric:** few words. His horror is recognising someone: the one face in the whole city he knew was hers.
- **Redd:** at his funniest at the meal, and his jokes drop away completely at the aqueduct. "Good." is one word, said quietly.
- **Silas:** blunt and economical ("Everything else.", "They've got their witch.").
- **Hild:** earthy Mydean speech, warm, and she likes Silas more than she lets on. "By the Four" at most once.
- **The priest:** calm, procedural and reasonable. The sentence uses church language ("lawful masters", "sheltered by charity", "the Light withdrawn"). Nobody narrates his evil.
- **The crowd:** sings. It doesn't jeer.
- **Nell:** no lines.
- **Helmi:** one line, flat and careful.
- Avoid: "not X, but Y"; "for a moment" more than once; narrating Redd's trauma or Alaric's shame after the scene has shown them.

## Final Chapter Shape

**They walk out of the Expanse on empty skins, and a stranger at a well pours water over Redd's head. Next day Favale rises ahead, the temple's gold over everything and the king's palace small beneath it. At the Pilgrims' Gate the watch are pulling back women's hoods, hunting an elf witch, and three filthy men and a dog walk straight in. An elf woman with a burned cheek gives Wena water from her hands, and Alaric sees her ear and keeps her secret. Silas's place is still there, with chairs. Redd teaches Alaric to eat a pie, and Alaric finds the first thing in his new life he knows he likes. When the cook mentions an elf taken from the temple kitchens, he doesn't ask which one. That night he lies to Redd about the voice. At dawn a small figure in white stands on the temple stair while the sick lie in her shadow. An hour later, at the western aqueduct, a priest sentences the woman from the gate "by the Light the Ruler carries", and she is hanged for what happened on the pilgrim road. Alaric knows she's innocent and does nothing. Redd says, "Good." They walk in silence to the house where the aqueduct meets the old river road, and a thin, quiet man opens the door on a table set for two and a child's coat on its peg. "If it's about the woman on the aqueduct, I didn't see anything."**
