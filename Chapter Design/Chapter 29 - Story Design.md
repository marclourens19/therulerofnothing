_Draft for Joshua's approval, 24 September 2026. Built from ten rounds of Joshua's answers. Where Joshua gave his own version of a scene, it is shaped here rather than copied, and every place I've added to or changed his idea is marked **[P]** so he can push back. ## Working Status

- **Viewpoint:** Seralune, close third throughout **[J]**. Thaeroval stays external, as at Meren: we see only what he does, never what he thinks.
- **Working title:** open (ten options after the chapter is written).
- **Places:** Agna's laundry (the loft, the coppers, the roof); the pilgrims' quarter filling for the Longest Light; the streets on the eve, against the vigil crowd; the old quarter; **the lily house** at the top of the Lily Steps; the leaning house beside it.
- **Time:** **Days 9 to 11** **[J]**, ending at **dawn on Day 12, the Longest Light.** The meeting is on the evening of Day 11, the eve.
- **Parts:** **decide after writing [J].** Write it whole, then split only if there's a real turn. The likeliest break is Thaeroval's first line at the ruin.
- **Length:** as long as it needs, likely 9,000–12,000 words.
- **Style targets:** 30% or more dialogue across the chapter, about 16 words a paragraph, almost none over 80. The night in the ruin is silent by design, so Days 9–11 and the confrontation carry the talk. No THOOM (Alaric's). British spelling.
- **Brutality:** one blow, one bystander death, and a woman Seralune loves broken in front of her. It's the darkest thing done to her in the volume so far.

## Workshop Notes [P]

These are the places where I've shaped Joshua's version. Each is a proposal.

1. **He calls her "Seralune", not "Sera."** In Chapters 4 and 5 he only ever uses her full name. "Sera" belongs to Nereth and the Meren harbour book. His line becomes: "The thatcher on the road. The woman on the arch. How many more, Seralune?" Hearing her whole name after days of being Sera is part of what cracks her.
2. **Why he loses control.** Seralune never learns why, and the page doesn't say. But we set it up so readers can feel it: at Meren he thanked Nyana ("You were kind to her"), and he knows exactly how ill Nereth is. What he strikes is the person who took his sister away from him. For one breath the flatness that severance left in him fills with something ugly, then closes again.
3. **The walk towards them.** It's slow and even, like a man crossing his own hall. He's taking hold of himself with every step, and Seralune reads it as victory. That's the moment she screams.
4. **She screams at him.** She has never raised her voice to her brother. "CAN'T YOU SEE SHE'S SUFFERING!?" is the numbness breaking open for a breath. It's also the moment the fragments come hardest, so what she shuts away later is bigger than anything before it.
5. **The bargain's wording.** "Take her to the stair tomorrow. Lay her where the saint's shadow falls. And I'll come with you. I won't run again." She offers him the bands; he doesn't mention them. His "I'll carry her" takes the weight on himself, and it's the nearest he comes to sorry.
6. **The watch, twice.** First they come down the lane with lanterns at the crash. He looks at them, and they back away (the dread from Meren). Later, with the vigil hymn still coming from the hill, they come back for the old woman with a sheet and carry her out without looking at him. The sheet echoes the laundries' sheets for the four dead in Chapter 27.
7. **The basin.** It's the temple's cleansing shrine in the ruin of a house Natharul burned: a prince of Natharul washing a Mydean woman's blood off his hands under a painted sun. He keeps washing after the water runs clear.
8. **Copying him works, and that's the horror.** As Seralune makes her face like his, Nereth's marks cool and go quiet under her hands, just as they did at the lily stone in Chapter 27. The logic of the seal proves itself in her lap.
9. **Dialogue on Days 9–11.** Because the night is silent, the laundry has to talk. Bett's gossip (the saint will walk the whole stair; how many lanterns), Tilde's temper, pilgrims at the door with sheets, and Nereth still able to speak on Days 9 and 10 ("You've gone somewhere again").
10. **The jar is left behind.** They run without Nereth's pack, so the last of Nyana's drops is on a nail in Agna's loft. There's nothing between Nereth and the stair now.

## Scene Plan

### 1. The first flash (Day 9, before dawn)

- Seralune wakes in the loft out of the field: running, blood, crying, someone dying with his hand out. She can't breathe. Nereth is asleep beside her, and her marks prick under Seralune's hand.
- She counts, as he taught her, and the counting doesn't reach it. The pain behind her eyes lasts a breath, then it's gone.

### 2. The city fills (Days 9 and 10)

- The laundry runs day and night: every hostel's sheets before the Longest Light. Agna works without speaking. Tilde snaps. Bett brings the city in with the sheets (the stair scrubbed white by five hundred people on their knees; the saint will walk the whole of it; lanterns going up on every street; the envoy's baskets of fruit; dogs in pens by the river).
- Pilgrims at the door, the sick on doors in the lanes, hymns in patches, the practice lanterns at night. The world is loud and alive all round her.
- **Flashes, each worse:** one at the coppers (horned beastfolk in the line with men, and a man with no face dying on his knees); one in the market (a silver machine walking, as the pool showed it); one at night on the roof (crying, and her own feet running). She never sees whom she's running towards.
- Nereth on Days 9–10, still on her feet and still sharp: "You've gone somewhere again." Seralune: "I'm here." Nereth: "No, you're not."

### 3. The eve (Day 11, evening)

- Agna comes back from the stewards: they'll sit after the Longest Light, when the pilgrims have gone home. She sits with Kit's untested name on a scrap of paper.
- The vigil begins: the city's lamps start up the hill.
- In the laundry, the worst flash yet: the field whole in noise and blood, the dying calling out, and her running. Her feeling breaks through the numbness, and **Nereth's marks flare**. Nereth folds up at the coppers with a sound Seralune has never heard her make.
- **Agna gets down on the wet floor and holds her.**
- Seralune sees what she is to everyone in the room, and runs. Nereth gets up after her. Agna: **"You can't stand!"**

### 4. Against the vigil

- Downhill against the flood of lamps: the sick carried up on doors, children with candles, a hymn she half knows. She's the only thing in the city going the other way.
- Behind her, Nereth fights through the crowd, falling behind.
- The old quarter is dark and empty; everyone has gone up to the hill. The Lily Steps. She doesn't know why she's here. She's running to her mother.

### 5. The lily house

- **Thaeroval stands in the ruin, staring at the house, not moving.** Dark armour, travelling cloak, the sword. The lily stone is beside his shoulder.
- **"I knew you'd come here."**
- **"Did you do this?" / "No. But I knew."** Twenty years ago. The warrant. He didn't ride.
- His case, in few words: **"The thatcher on the road. The woman on the arch. How many more, Seralune?"** He can protect her, and them from her.
- The fragments build under every line (a hand, a cry, running). She won't show him.
- **She's had enough of this life. She'll live it on her own terms.** She runs.
- **"So you've chosen force, then."** Barely a whisper.

### 6. The blow

- Nereth comes up the Lily Steps out of the dark, gasping.
- He is across the ruin before Seralune sees him move. One blow. **Nereth goes through the wall of the leaning house** beside the steps.
- For one breath his face isn't flat. Then it is.
- Seralune goes through the hole after her. Nereth on the floor in the plaster dust: ribs, an arm, blood at her mouth. The beam is down. **Under it, an old woman's hand.** She never learns who she was.
- He comes after them slowly, evenly, sure he's won.

### 7. The price

- **"CAN'T YOU SEE SHE'S SUFFERING!?"** The first time she has ever raised her voice to him.
- She begs. **"Take her to the stair tomorrow. Lay her where the saint's shadow falls. And I'll come with you. I won't run again."**
- **"I'll carry her."**

### 8. The night in the ruin

- **The watch** come with lanterns at the crash. He looks at them, and they back away.
- Later **they come back with a sheet** and carry the old woman out past the ruin, not looking at him. He watches it go by. **Then he goes to the shrine basin and washes his knuckles,** and keeps washing after the water runs clear.
- **Nereth unconscious in Seralune's lap** under the lily stone. His back to them, guarding the lane. He says almost nothing all night.
- Up on the hill, the vigil lamps and the hymn.
- The flashes keep coming. **She watches his face and makes hers the same.** Under her hands, Nereth's marks cool and go quiet. It works. She keeps the someone to herself.

### 9. Dawn, Day 12

- Grey light. The first bells of the Longest Light.
- **He kneels and gathers Nereth up to carry her to the stair.** (End.)

## Character Movement

- **Seralune:** from holding the numbness to losing it, and then to choosing it, by copying the brother she ran from. She ends the chapter promised to him and sure she's the danger.
- **Thaeroval:** first on the page in Favale. He is patient, then cruel, then shaken for one breath, then sure he's won. Every move is his idea of protection.
- **Nereth:** from the one who keeps Seralune alive to the one Seralune has to save, carried in her brother's arms.
- **Agna:** holds a stranger on her floor while her own son waits for a hearing.

## Dialogue and Voice Notes

- **Seralune:** formal with him at first ("Did you do this?"), then contractions and broken sentences, then the scream, then flat.
- **Thaeroval:** sparse, formal, few contractions. No warmth except in what he does. Never explains. Says "Seralune" in full.
- **Nereth (Days 9–10):** dry, watchful, still bossy. Silent after the blow.
- **Agna and the girls:** Agna short and hard; Tilde sharp; Bett a stream of the city's news.
- **The watch:** they say almost nothing either.

## Magic and Lore Guardrails

- **No new mana events.** The flashes are memory, felt as pain behind the eyes. Nereth's marks flare and cool as the existing rule says (Seralune's feelings feed the corruption). Nobody burns.
- **The Dark pressure from Meren is not used.** The blow is only a fist.
- **The seal-bands are never shown.**

- **Thaeroval's thoughts are never narrated.** How he reached Favale isn't explained.

## Knowledge Boundaries

- **Seralune learns:** her brother is in Favale; he knew about the lily house and let it happen; he will strike the people she loves; numbness quiets Nereth's marks.
- **She doesn't learn:** the old woman's name, how he got here, why he lost control, what the flashes are, or that the young man is in Favale.
- **Readers know:** the flashes match the pool, so she was on Alaric's field; Alaric sat under that lily stone the morning before; Thaeroval was told at Meren how ill Nereth is.
