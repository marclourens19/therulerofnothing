# Chapter 30 — Story Design

_Draft for Joshua's approval, 24 September 2026. Built from ten rounds of Joshua's answers. His locked choices are marked **[J]**. Where I've shaped or filled in around them, the change is marked **[P]** so he can push back. Nothing is drafted as prose until this design is approved._

**Title [J]: "The Tear".** Fixed from the start, so there are no title options at the end.

---

## Working Status

- **Parts and viewpoint [J]:** three long parts.
  - **Part 1 (Seralune):** dawn to the stair, up to the moment she breaks.
  - **Part 2 (Seralune):** the memory, the tear, and what's left at the centre.
  - **Part 3 (Alaric):** goes back to dawn by the river and catches up to the instant the seam reaches him. It ends as the dragon comes through.
- **Thaeroval and Atera stay external.** We see what they do, never what they think.
- **Time:** Day 12, **the Longest Light**, from the dawn bells that ended Chapter 29 to mid-morning. Part 3 also looks back briefly at Alaric's Days 8–11.
- **Places:**
  - Part 1: the old quarter, the old river road under Nell's arch, the square at the foot of the western stair, and the stair.
  - Part 2: the stair, the square, the hill and the western city, all ruined.
  - Part 3: the fishers' landing among the aqueduct piers, then the Great Library's public hall **[P]**.
- **Length:** Part 1 about 7,000–8,000 words; Part 2 about 5,000–6,000; Part 3 about 7,000–8,000. That's roughly 20,000 in all, the biggest chapter in the volume.
- **Style targets:**
  - Parts 1 and 3 are talk-heavy, 30% dialogue or more. Part 2 is lower by design, because it's memory and horror.
  - About 16 words a paragraph, with almost none over 80.
  - British spelling. No THOOM, which belongs to Alaric's sword.
- **Brutality:**
  - Thousands dead on the holiest day of the year, including children: never sexualised, never softened.
  - The destruction is **horror, not spectacle**. It's scary because it is real **[J]**.
  - Atera is torn apart and rebuilt on the page.
  - Agna and both girls die.

## Joshua's Decisions (locked, ten rounds)

**The core**

1. **The tear is both of them [J].** Seralune breaks down, and all of her mana is directed towards Alaric. For an instant it connects with his Veiled Four, and that releases an uncontrollable catastrophe. It's pure overload, and **the distance between them is the tear.** It destroys everything and kills thousands.
2. **The shape is a seam from her to him [J].**
   - Her end explodes.
   - A seam tears in a straight line from the stair to Alaric and **stops at him.** Along it the **ground is cut and the sky is torn**, and anyone in the city can see it.
   - It passes through **Nell's arch** and **the Great Library** **[J]**.
   - (Chapter 31: Thaeroval follows the seam to its end and finds the "empty" boy there.)
3. **The look is raw destruction only [J].** There's no violet frost, no glow and no signature. It's **uncanny as in horror and scary**, not magical oddity.
4. **The reach is everything [J]:** the stair, the square, the hill and temple, and out into the western city. At the centre it's torn apart, and the carnage runs all the way to the edge **[J: "mix 1 and 2"]**.

**Part 1: the walk and the stair**

5. **The build is long, with lots of talk [J].**
6. **On the way, the crowds part in silence [J].** The watch shadow him and no one stops him.
7. **Brother and sister talk about their childhood [J]:** one warm, shared memory, the only moment he sounds like her brother.
8. **She holds his face, with one slip [J].** A flash pushes through and she hides it from him. The real crack comes at the stair.
9. **Nereth is laid near the top, by fear [J].** People who held their places all night move their own sick out of his way. A mother carries her child down to give Nereth the step.
10. **The envoy stays hidden [J].** The white carriage waits at the foot of the stair with its curtains drawn. It is **destroyed** in the blast, and the envoy's fate stays unknown **[J]**.
11. **Atera speaks to Thaeroval [J]:** no blade is carried on the stair on the Longest Light, and she asks him to lay the sword down. **He doesn't [J].**
12. **Atera and Seralune's eyes meet, and a few words pass [J].**
13. **The trigger is all of it at once [J].**

**Part 2: the memory and the tear**

14. **The memory comes back whole:** **his face, and his bare left hand closing on her wrist with his thumb on the inside [J].** Then the light takes him. **No name [J].**
15. **Something black runs through the light and eats its own reflection. She doesn't understand it [J].** It's carried into Volume Two.
16. **Atera is rebuilt close and slowly, and it costs her [J].** Seralune watches from a few steps away: bone, then flesh, then skin. Her white is gone and nothing covers her. She feels every part of it.
17. **Thaeroval is simply gone [J].** When the dust clears, the place where he stood is empty.
18. **Nereth lives because Seralune is holding her.** Her corruption is contained in **Chapter 32**, not here **[J]**.
19. **Agna, Tilde and Bett all die [J].** They went up to the stair for Kit, and Seralune finds them.
20. **Faces among the dead [J]:**
    - the mother and her child;
    - Old Hobb;
    - Yseth, by the white carriage;
    - the older watchman and young Wil.
21. **Part 2 ends along the seam [J].** She kneels among the dead holding Nereth, with Atera rebuilt and gasping a few steps away, and looks along the torn line running across the city. She doesn't know what's at the other end.

**Part 3: Alaric**

22. **Days 8–11 [J]:** Alaric and Silas hide **by the river with the fishers**, waiting for any sign of Wena and Redd.
23. **Idony smuggles Helmi, Redd and Wena out [J],** and Alaric and Silas meet them. **All of them are together** when the seam reaches him **[J]**.
24. **"How long?" [J]:** Alaric tells Silas **the truth about the healing only**, not the voice.
25. **The voice [J]** is silent all week. It **returns on the morning of the Longest Light** and grows as the bells ring and the saint comes down. He hides it.
26. **The instant [J]:** agony, and the voice **screams**, the sound of someone being torn apart, inside him. **Nothing visible comes off him.**
27. **The dragon comes through the torn sky [J].** First a sound, then a shape too big to understand. It comes out already mid-battle from the Great Remaking, with old spears and old wounds, and furious. **The last beat is its first roar over Favale.**
28. **Why Thaeroval can't kill it (for Chapters 31–32) [J]:** it isn't wholly here. Part of it is still a thousand years ago, and his cut passes through it like smoke; for the first time, his sword fails. Light exposes and isolates, so only Atera can pin it into the present, where it can die.

## Workshop Notes [P]

These are the places where I've shaped your answers or filled a gap. Each one is a proposal.

1. **"Hold her."** This is why Seralune is holding Nereth at the instant of the tear. Atera kneels and says, "Hold her," before she lays her hand on Nereth. The saint's own words save the one person who lives. Neither of them knows it.
2. **Atera and Seralune's few words.** Atera asks the burned woman's name. Nobody ever asked Nell hers; the saint asks Nereth's. Then:
   > "And yours?"
   >
   > "Sera."

   Seralune says it in front of her brother, the name he won't use. It's the one thing she claims all day, and it happens a breath before she breaks.
3. **The standoff, and why nobody wins it.**
   > "No blade is carried on this stair on the Longest Light, First Blade. Lay it down."
   >
   > "No."

   It's one word, like "Yes. No. Just like that." Atera doesn't argue and doesn't back away. She steps past him to the sick woman, which is what she came down the stair for. The whole city watches a Ruler of Natharul keep his sword on the Light's stair, and Leorin's words from Chapter 17 come true in public. **Atera knows what he is before anyone tells her,** because Rulers can feel the shape of an affinity. How she knows stays unsaid.
4. **The childhood memory: the glass bird.**
   - When Seralune was six she broke something of Mother's, a glass bird. She ran to Thaer, and he said he'd done it.
   - The exchange:
     > "You told Father it was you."
     >
     > "It was easier."
     >
     > "It wasn't. He made you —"
     >
     > "It was easier than you crying."
     >
     > "I cried anyway."
     >
     > "Yes."
   - This is where "when she was six and had broken something" (Ch 29) comes from. He took the blame once, for her.
   - It happens under Nell's arch, while he carries Nereth.
   - Alternative: he once carried her to bed asleep from a palace stair. That echoes Bett's "Da carried me all the way home", but the glass bird is ours alone.
5. **The slip.** In the crowd, a pilgrim weeps with his hands over his face. For a heartbeat he is the man on the field, and her face nearly goes. She looks at the cobbles until it's hers again. Thaeroval doesn't turn, or she doesn't know if he saw.
6. **The laundry on the stair.**
   - Agna, Tilde and Bett have come up after all. They're sitting on **the forty-third step**, Bett's step, where her Da once carried her home from.
   - Agna has Kit's name on its paper, written by Father Clem, to hold up for the saint's shadow.
   - Bett sees Nereth in the elf lord's arms and calls her name. Tilde: "That's him, isn't it."
   - Agna looks at Seralune. **Seralune doesn't answer.** She's wearing his face, and it's the cruellest thing she does all day.
   - After the tear she goes back down the broken stair and finds them. Agna is lying over both girls, with the paper still in her hand.
7. **Yseth sees.** At the foot of the stair, by the white carriage, Yseth sees the First Blade carrying a burned woman. Then she sees Nereth, and then Seralune. She goes to the carriage window and speaks through the curtain. We never hear what she says. Did the envoy learn, in the last minutes? It's a question for Volume Two. Then the carriage is destroyed, and Yseth with it.
8. **The trigger, all at once.** Everything arrives in a few breaths:
   - Every bell in Favale rings for the saint's first steps down.
   - The hymn rises from ten thousand throats: "through the dark, through the dark."
   - Thaeroval's hand rests on his hilt.
   - The white carriage stands below with her father's tree on it.
   - Agna's family sits below, holding up Kit's paper.
   - She has just said "Sera" aloud.
   - Atera's hand is on Nereth, and **the Light goes into Nereth. Nereth's marks flare white under it, and her breath catches and stops.**
   - The white of the Light is the white at the end of the field.

   Her face cracks. **Part 1 ends here: "The field came. All of it."**
9. **The memory's last beats.**
   - She's running, late, and the smoke tears open.
   - He turns: a young man with dark-brown hair, green eyes, blood on his face. It's the face readers know, never named.
   - He reaches, and **his bare left hand closes round her wrist, thumb on the inside, the way it always had.**
   - His mouth shapes a word, and there's no sound at all. The word was cut out of the world.
   - Then the white, and in it **a line of black that ate its own reflection**, and his hand isn't on her wrist, and there's nothing.
   - She was late.
10. **The reaching.**
    - In the present, on the stair, she reaches after him: *not again.*
    - All of her goes out of her at once, in one direction, across the city.
    - **For an instant, something answers,** the way a lock answers a key, once. Then the world tears.
    - Nothing names what answered. Readers who have followed Alaric will know which direction she reached in.
11. **Nereth's marks go quiet during the tear.** It's an existing rule that the true connection doesn't corrupt (Chapter 27). For the instant her mana has its true direction, none of it goes into Nereth. That's part of why Nereth lives, but it's never explained. Readers see only that the lines on her throat are dark and still.
12. **Seralune is unhurt.** She's the one living, whole person at the centre, and her hood is gone, so her ears show. At the far edge, a survivor sees an elf girl kneeling untouched among thousands of dead. That is Chapter 31's danger and Volume Two's.
13. **The horror, told close and in order.**
    - First the sound, then no sound at all.
    - Then what's in front of her: the step, the sick on their doors, the mother and her child, Wil and the old watchman a few steps below.
    - Then further: the square, Old Hobb's jars, the carriage in pieces and Yseth, the temple's gold roofs, the western city.
    - Short paragraphs, plain words, no metaphor where a fact will do. It's scary because it's precise.
14. **Atera's rebuilding** is told as body horror, never beauty and never sexualised.
    - Something moves in the ruin of her a few steps up: bone first, then what goes over bone, then skin.
    - **She screams as soon as she has a throat,** and keeps screaming until she has breath to stop.
    - Seralune covers her with the torn remains of her white, because there's nothing else.
15. **Along the seam (Part 2's last image).**
    - From the ruined stair Seralune can see the whole line: ground cut, sky torn, running straight away across the western city.
    - It goes **through Nell's arch, which has split,** so Nell is finally down. Then across the river, into the Great Library, and on to something she can't see.
    - She doesn't know that she's looking at the distance between them.

**Part 3**

16. **It opens on the same dawn bells** that ended Chapter 29, heard from the river. The rewind is clear at once.
17. **Days 8–11 come in short scenes and talk while they wait,** not as a summary:
    - pulled out at the washing rocks;
    - the eel-catchers among the aqueduct piers, who take coin and ask nothing;
    - watching the Library's river wall day and night;
    - Silas's "How long?" answered by a fire on a punt: *since the field; faster every time.* The voice stays his.
18. **The sign.** On Day 10, **Redd's knot is tied to the ring of the Keeper's river door**. It's the knot Redd lost on the grate, tied again his way, and it means they're alive. A fisher child carries messages back and forth under a basket of eels. Idony's plan comes back:
    - The temple has asked the Library for its old hymnals for the stair.
    - She'll wheel a handcart of them up through the public halls on the Longest Light, while priests and watch are all at the stair.
    - Helmi, Redd and Wena will be under the books.
    - Alaric and Silas will meet the cart in **the Great Hall**, under the painted ceiling of the Remaking. From there they go out to a fishers' punt downriver.
19. **The meeting in the Great Hall.**
    - The hall is empty for the feast, apart from **Aubin**, too old for the stair, at his vigil, and **Tollan** the cat.
    - Wena bursts out from under the hymnals.
    - Redd tries not to look glad.
    - **Helmi gives Alaric back his satchel with the core in it.**
    - Idony means to stay: "Someone has to be Keeper when they come back."
20. **The voice** has grown all morning with the bells, and Alaric has hidden it. In the hall it swells. **Wena howls towards the hill** a few breaths before anything happens.
21. **The seam arrives.** It comes across the city and the river, **cuts the Great Hall along its length (the painted sun at the end of the ceiling splits in two)**, and **stops at Alaric.**
    - The voice screams inside him, and he's on his back on the floor.
    - Everyone sees the line stop at him.
    - **Silas: "It stopped at you."**
22. **The last beat.** Alaric is on his back, looking up through the cut roof at the torn sky. First a sound, from very far off and very long ago. Then a shape too big to understand coming through: iron-dark and scaled like slate, wings torn, **old spears still in it, the red mud of another field on it, a silver machine's leg hooked in its side.** It's still fighting a war a thousand years gone. **Its first roar goes out over Favale.** The chapter ends.

## Scene Plan

### Part 1: The Longest Light (Seralune)

**1. Bells over the old quarter (dawn).**
- The same bells as the end of Chapter 29.
- Thaeroval carries Nereth out of the ruin, and Seralune follows, wearing his face.
- The old quarter is empty: everyone is on the hill.

**2. The old river road.**
- The city is lit. Every lantern burns in daylight, and the banners are out.
- The crowds heading for the stair see him and **go silent and part.**
- The watch appear behind them, stay behind them, and don't come closer: the older watchman with the grey moustache, and Wil.
- People say "elf" behind him and look away when he turns.

**3. Under Nell's arch: the glass bird.**
- Nell still hangs there. Seralune looks up this time.
- Thaeroval doesn't. Or he does, once.
- Here they talk about their childhood (Workshop Note 4), the only warmth all day. Then it's gone.

**4. The slip.** The weeping pilgrim with his hands over his face (Workshop Note 5).

**5. The square.**
- The foot of the western stair is packed.
- **The white carriage** with the royal tree, its curtains drawn, and the envoy's servants in livery round it. **Yseth** among them (Workshop Note 7).
- **Old Hobb** is selling "the saint's own water".
- Priests look at Thaeroval and then at each other. Nobody knows who has the authority to stop him, so nobody does.

**6. Up the stair.**
- He climbs among the sick on their doors and hurdles.
- **The forty-third step: Agna, Tilde and Bett**, with Kit's paper (Workshop Note 6). Seralune doesn't answer them.

**7. The place near the top.**
- **A mother picks up her own sick child and carries her down** so Nereth can have the step: "Here, my lord. Here."
- He lays Nereth down in his cloak.
- Seralune kneels. She watches power do what it always does, and her face doesn't move.

**8. Waiting.**
- The sun climbs. The hymn goes on.
- Some talk on the step: Seralune and the sick around her, and perhaps the mother, a few steps down, still looking up at Nereth's place.
- Thaeroval stands, one step up, with his hand on nothing.

**9. The saint.**
- All the bells. Atera comes out at the top, and this year she **walks** down, barefoot on the scrubbed stone, just as the rumour said.
- She is small and very pale, a girl who hasn't left the temple since she was four **[P]**, with priests round her.
- She stops at the first of the sick and lays her hands on them.

**10. Two Rulers.**
- She reaches their step and stops in front of Thaeroval.
- "No blade is carried on this stair on the Longest Light, First Blade. Lay it down." / "No."
- The air is heavy, and the bells hum strangely under their own ringing **[P, optional]**.
- She steps past him (Workshop Note 3).

**11. "Hold her."**
- Atera kneels at Nereth. "What is her name?" "Nereth." The saint says it back.
- "And yours?" **"Sera."**
- "Hold her." Seralune gathers Nereth up against her (Workshop Notes 1 and 2).

**12. All of it at once.**
- The Light goes into Nereth, and everything lands together (Workshop Note 8).
- **"The field came. All of it."** End of Part 1.

## Dialogue and Voice Notes

- **Seralune:** clipped and flat all morning. She's wearing his face. The glass bird is the one soft exchange, and "Sera" is the one claim. Part 2 is almost wordless.
- **Thaeroval:** very few words, formal, few contractions. "No." "It was easier." He calls her "Seralune" in full.
- **Atera:** plain, calm, church formal with him, and simple with Nereth and Seralune: "What is her name?" "Hold her." She never preaches.
- **Agna, Tilde and Bett:** Bett calls out, Tilde is sharp and knowing, and Agna says only Seralune's name, once.
- **The crowd:** "elf" behind his back; "Here, my lord"; the hymn; Old Hobb's patter.
- **Silas:** dry, and watching. "How long?" gets its answer. "It stopped at you."
- **Helmi, Redd, Idony:** Helmi precise; Redd gruff and glad; Idony devout and steady.

## Magic and Lore Guardrails

- **The tear is never explained.** Nobody names the Veiled Four, the divided soul, or why the line ends at Alaric.
- **No new mana effects beyond what's decided:** the Light going into Nereth, the flare, the tear, the seam, and Atera's rebuilding.
