## Joshua's Decisions (locked, ten rounds)

1. **Ten rounds, full length.** The interlude runs as long as it needs.
2. **He comes quietly and alone,** with no escort, no ship of his own and no announcement.
3. **He's unmistakably himself:** dark armour under a travelling cloak, and the single-edged sword. Anyone who knows what that means, knows.
4. **He's already inside.** When Gurdred comes back to the office, he's sitting at the clerk's desk with the ledger open. **Nobody dared stop him:** the door guard stepped aside, and the harbourmaster found somewhere else to be.
5. **In the room:** Gurdred, and **Neldor and Nyana, held over the passengers.** Waluna reported two women missing after the southern alarm, and Meren has kept Neldor's ship since (a consequence of Ch 21). **The clerk is off shift, so Gurdred has to read the entry aloud herself.**
6. **He's coldly courteous.** He has perfect manners in a human tongue he plainly finds distasteful, never raises his voice, and his replies are flat (the severance cost, never narrated). **He says "Please" once, to Gurdred: "Read it to me. Please."** There are no other human details [Joshua].
7. **He already knows where she's going.** He doesn't ask about roads or Favale. (Lore: he **knew** about the lily house twenty years ago, **but didn't ride** with the warrant. None of this is on the page here. It lands in hindsight once readers reach Ch 27.)
8. **He asks about the attendant:** Nereth's burns, how bad they are, what the medicine is.
9. **Nyana tells him the truth,** the healer warning: severe burns, poisoning suspected, five drops in water no more than twice a day, and "if the marks go hot, the jar won't help you. You find a healer." He doesn't comment. Readers know there's only one healer in Mydea.
10. **The trigger is "looking for family."** It isn't in the written entry. **Gurdred heard Seralune say it on the ship** in Ch 21 and remembers it when he asks what else the woman said. **He repeats "Family."** Then he takes a long breath out, and **everything starts to shake**, as if he's getting angry.
11. **The shaking** is the pressure of Dark gathering before a release that never comes:
    - **Bodies:** teeth ache, ears ring, hands won't keep still, and Gurdred feels her heartbeat in her tongue.
    - **Metal:** Gurdred's sword shivers in its scabbard, and buckles, nails and coins hum.
    - **The harbour:** the water shivers with no wind, the rigging sings, and **the harbour bell sounds once on its own.**
    - **His hands stay flat on the desk, nowhere near the sword.** The shaking comes from him, which is worse.
12. **Then nothing.** It stops. He closes the ledger and sets it square. **He takes nothing.** He stands and says to Neldor and Nyana, **"Thank you. You were kind to her."** Then he goes.
13. **Neldor knows him.** Neldor holds a Natharul military contract and has seen the First Blade before. He goes grey and silent, and **when he's thanked, he says nothing at all.** Gurdred sees only the captain's fear and never learns the name.
14. **They're stun-locked** after he leaves. **The harbour bell is still humming, and only when it stops can anyone move.**
15. **He never says how he found the entry.**
16. **Neldor and Nyana are released at once.** Meren wants nothing more to do with the Waluna entry.
17. **Gurdred's life:** she's **tired and counting days**, with **a year and a half** of her service left and a small plan for after it. She wants to get through the season without trouble.
18. **Her report is understated.** It goes to Favale: "A Natharul officer inspected the Waluna entry and continued inland." She can't find words for the rest, and the understatement is the horror. (It reaches Favale for Ch 29 politics: someone in the capital will read it.)
19. **Ending: crossing off a day.** She crosses a day off her count as she always does, and for the first time she can't remember how many she has left.

## Chapter Purpose

1. Show the brother at last: close enough that he can't be doubted, and far enough that he can't be read.
2. Start the clock, a week's road with five days to the Longest Light.
3. Pay off the Ch 21 ledger ("If anyone ever came to Meren asking… the harbour would be able to tell them exactly who to look for").
4. Deliver pure dread: one breath out from him and a harbour shakes, and then he says thank you.
5. Let the humans feel it: Gurdred's tiredness, Neldor's knowledge, and Nyana's honesty out of love for Nereth.
6. Send word to Favale that the capital will read too lightly.

## Scene Plan

**One continuous scene, then a short coda.**

### 1. The office

- **Rain on the harbour; a slow shift.** Gurdred comes back from the quay, wet, counting. We learn her own life in her count and her feet, and in the grain holds she stamps.
- **Something's wrong before she's through the door:** the door guard standing too straight and staring at nothing; the harbourmaster's cloak gone from its peg; Neldor and Nyana on the bench where she left them, not looking at the desk.
- **He's at the clerk's desk** with the ledger open. Dark armour, a travelling cloak wet at the hem, the sword. He's looking at the page. She tells him he can't be there. He doesn't argue, and doesn't move.
- **"Read it to me. Please."** She reads the entry aloud: black hair, very fair, green eyes, speech of a great household. (Proposal: as she reads "green eyes", she looks up, and his are green. She doesn't know what to make of it. See Small Choices.)

### 2. The questions

- **He asks about the attendant,** courteous and flat: how bad were the burns? Did she walk? What medicine?
- **Nyana answers.** She's frightened, and she tells him the truth anyway, because she thinks Nereth will die without help: the burns, the poison, five drops, and "if the marks go hot, find a healer." She's the only one in the room who speaks at length. (Her voice is warm and bossy; the fear shows in how she keeps talking.)
- **He asks Gurdred what else the woman said.** Gurdred remembers the gangplank: "She said they were looking for family."
- **"Family."**

### 3. The breath

- He breathes out, long and slow. **Everything starts to shake:** teeth, ears, hands; her heartbeat in her tongue; her sword in its scabbard; buckles, nails, the coins in the harbour box; the rain on the water turning to a shiver with no wind; the rigging singing; **the harbour bell ringing once on its own.** His hands are flat on the desk.
- It goes on longer than it should. Gurdred understands, the way you understand weather, that the room is waiting to find out what he decides.
- **Then it stops.**

### 4. Thank you

- He closes the ledger and squares it on the desk. He stands. To Neldor and Nyana: **"Thank you. You were kind to her."**
- Neldor, grey, says nothing. Nyana says nothing.
- He goes out into the rain. **He takes nothing.**

### 5. The bell (coda)

- Nobody moves. **The harbour bell is still humming.** When it finally stops, Nyana's breath comes out of her, and people can move again.
- The harbourmaster reappears. **Neldor and Nyana are released within the hour.** Neldor won't say who it was. Gurdred doesn't ask twice.
- **The report:** Gurdred writes it for the Favale rider. She tries three times and ends with *"A Natharul officer inspected the Waluna entry and continued inland."*
- **Final image:** she crosses off the day on her count, as she does at the end of every shift, and **for the first time she can't remember how many she has left.**

## Voice Notes

- **Gurdred:** plain Mydean soldier; tired; dry; fair-minded; resentful of Natharul's grain holds; not a coward, but not stupid either. "By the Four" at most once.
- **Thaeroval:** sparse, formal, no contractions; "Please" once; "Thank you. You were kind to her." There's no warmth except in what he asks about. Narration never enters him.
- **Nyana:** warm, bossy and intrusive about treatment, frightened here, and honest because she's a medic.
- **Neldor:** level and dry by nature. Here, **grey silence**; his fear is the only clue Gurdred gets.

---

## Guardrails

- No violence, no drawn sword, no cut. The shaking is pressure, not a release.
- No access to Thaeroval's thoughts. Gurdred can read nothing in him.
- No mention of the lily house, the queen or the sealing. He doesn't say "mother".
- His name is never spoken. Neldor knows it; Gurdred doesn't.
- No two knocks.
- No hint of how he'll cover the road to Favale (hidden until Ch 29).

## Small Choices for Joshua to Confirm or Change

1. **Green eyes:** as Gurdred reads "green eyes", she looks up and his are green too, which makes him look like the woman she's describing. This would be new canon for Thaeroval's appearance (only "dark armour" is established).
2. **Where Gurdred was:** she was out on the quay sealing a Natharul grain hold in the rain, which is her whole job.
3. **The report's three drafts:** she crosses out "royal", then "the harbour shook", and sends the plain line.

5. **Neldor afterwards:** before he leaves, he says one thing to Gurdred, "Don't write his name." She doesn't know the name, so she can't. (Optional. Say if you'd rather he stayed silent to the end.)
