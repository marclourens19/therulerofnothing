# Chapter 24 — Story Design

_Draft for Joshua's approval, 22 September 2026. Built from two rounds of Joshua's answers, under his direction that the coming chapters escalate in intensity, brutality, character writing and questions. Nothing is drafted as prose until this design is approved._

---

## Working Status

- **Viewpoint:** Seralune, close third throughout
- **Working title:** Open (ten options follow the finished chapter)
- **Primary location:** the pilgrim road into Favale, where the inland road from Meren joins it, then the fields and hills beside it
- **Time:** the evening of the fourth day out of Meren to the night of the fifth. Favale is about two days further on.
- **Target length:** approximately 4,800–5,500 words
- **Primary register:** warmth among strangers, then the world's hatred, then the discovery that she is the danger
- **Combat:** none. A mob, and a death Seralune doesn't choose.

---

## Joshua's Decisions (locked)

1. **Nereth's rule: Seralune's feelings feed the corruption.** It flares whenever Seralune's mana surges (fear, grief, anger), and eases when she's calm. In this chapter they work it out together, and Nereth admits she has hidden the flares since the ship. Seralune learns she can hurt Nereth just by feeling too much, which is exactly what breaks at the tear. This is the rule for the rest of the series.
2. **Main pressure: found out.** During a flare, Nereth's scarf comes off and pilgrims see an elf's ears. People who've lost villages to Natharul turn on two women who had nothing to do with it.
3. **Cost: Seralune's mana hurts innocents.** In the crisis, her mana surges and reaches into the people touching them, the way it reached into Nereth in Chapter 10. Their own affinities go wrong. Seralune understands that what happened to Nereth came from her.
4. **The man who grabbed Nereth dies.** He has a Fire affinity, and it turns inward: black-purple veins, then he burns from the inside, in front of everyone. Others near him are hurt as their own affinities go wrong. A child sees it all.
5. **The story runs ahead.** Survivors carry word to Favale of an elf witch on the pilgrim road. Along with Meren's ledger, the net tightens before Seralune reaches the city.
6. **The Holy bearer: pilgrims' talk.** The sick are carried to Favale to lie in her shadow. Pilgrims call her the living saint; the priests call her the Ruler of Light. Her name is **Atera**.
7. **Ending: Nereth afraid of her.** At night, away from the road, Nereth tells her about the flares. They work out the rule together, and Nereth, who has never once flinched from her, flinches. The chapter ends on a relationship changed, not a threat.

## Chapter Purpose

1. Show Seralune the Mydea of the pilgrims, human faith and grief at close range, and let her enjoy some of it before it turns.
2. Plant **Atera**, the living saint, the Ruler of Light, through the mouths of people who need her.
3. Make Natharul's cruelty visible on the road, then set the pilgrims' hatred on two elves who had no part in it.
4. Answer a question cleanly: why Nereth's corruption flares. The answer is Seralune.
5. Take the first of the four things that break Seralune (Ending Plan): Nereth's corruption worsens, and Seralune caused it.
6. Change the most important relationship in her thread. Nereth flinches.
7. Tighten the net on Favale: a story about an elf witch reaches the city before they do.

**Gained:** the truth about her mana and Nereth; knowledge of Atera.
**Lost:** a man's life, at her hands; Nereth's worsening body; their cover on the pilgrim road; Nereth's ease with her.

## Causal Chain

**What caused this chapter?**

- **Ch 10:** Seralune's mana poured into Nereth through the cut on her palm, and the corruption took her.
- **Ch 16:** the surge on the night of the memory, and the flare Nereth hid.
- **Ch 21:** Nyana's warning. Meren's hatred of Natharul (Dalen, Harl's Ford, the hanged thief). The ledger. The route to the pilgrim road.
- Natharul's continuing presence inside Mydea.

**What becomes possible?**

- Favale's watch and temple are listening for an elf witch before Seralune arrives (Ch 27).
- Seralune now fears her own feelings, which is the ground the tear grows from.
- Atera is a name readers know before she appears.

**What becomes impossible?**

- Walking the pilgrim road openly.
- Seralune believing her mana is harmless when it has nowhere to go.
- Nereth hiding the flares any more.

**Who behaves differently afterwards?**

- **Seralune** holds herself still to keep Nereth safe. She counts through every feeling (Thaeroval's lesson), which is exactly the kind of containment she escaped.
- **Nereth** is honest about the flares, and afraid of the person she chose to follow.
- **The pilgrims** carry the story to Favale.

## Plain Scene Skeleton

**fire shared → pilgrims' talk (Atera; Harl's Ford) → Natharul riders ride through → an old woman trampled → Seralune's surge → Nereth's flare → scarf off → ears seen → mob → a man's grip on Nereth → Seralune's terror → his Fire turns inward and he burns → others hurt → the crowd breaks → flight → hiding → confession → the rule → the flinch**

Compared with the previous five chapters:

- **Ch 19:** arrival → suspicion → acceptance → the smooth edge → alarm
- **Ch 20:** stand-off → battle → deliberate kill → dispute → departure
- **Ch 21:** landfall → inspection → farewell → city → road
- **Ch 22:** meal → displacement → shelter → siege and pull → landmark → water
- **Ch 23:** lesson → wrong lead → sleepwalk → confession → the forty → enemy killed → name

This is the first mob in the book, and the first killing by Seralune's power. Ch 10's corruption of Nereth is the only near-echo, and this chapter deliberately inverts it: there she didn't understand what her mana did; here she does.

## Scene Architecture

### Scene 1 — The Fire

**Opening image:** a man lights the pilgrims' cookfire with his bare hand. (Not waking, not walking; the first image is the affinity that kills him.)

**Immediate want:** Seralune and Nereth want to pass as two more pilgrims for the last two days to Favale.

**Pressure:** the pilgrim road is a crowd, and crowds look at faces. Nereth's scars draw pity and questions. Seralune's speech still sounds like a great household. And Nereth has walked four days on pain she isn't mentioning.

**Required developments:**

- The pilgrim road: a slow river of people. The sick are carried on doors, litters and backs; people wear sun tokens and sing. Faints walk at the back and drink last at shrine basins (one line of the church's order, no scene).
- **Osric** (name to confirm): a Common Fire man, travelling with his seven-year-old daughter **Wren** (name to confirm) and a wife coughing blood on a handcart. He lights the fire and gives Nereth the warm side without being asked. Wren shares bread with Seralune and asks her if she's a lady ("You talk like one").
- **Atera**, from the pilgrims: the living saint walks the temple steps at dawn, and the sick lie in her shadow. "She's no older than you." The priests call her the Ruler of Light. One human detail: they say she hasn't left the temple since she was four. The word "Ruler" catches on something in Seralune that she can't place.
- **Harl's Ford:** Osric's sister lived there. "The elves' collectors came back with their own soldiers." Seralune saw that name struck through on Meren's board. She says nothing.
- **Warmth:** Seralune laughs at something Wren says, for the first time since Meren. Nereth sleeps sitting up against her.

**Turn:** Seralune likes these people, and they would kill her if they knew what she was.

### Scene 2 — The Riders

**Immediate want:** keep walking and keep quiet.

**Pressure:** in the morning, a Natharul escort comes up the pilgrim road towards Favale: riders in pale armour around a closed carriage. **Proposal to confirm:** the carriage bears the royal tree, her father's house, travelling to the Holy Capital. They don't slow for the pilgrims.

**Required developments:**

- The riders ride through the crowd rather than round it. An old woman on a litter is knocked into the ditch; the litter bearers are struck with the flat of a sword for being slow. No rider looks back. The pilgrims kneel, or spit once the riders have passed.
- Seralune sees the tree on the carriage door, and every fear she has arrives at once: the palace, the locked door, her brother's hand. **Her mana surges.** (Her sensory language: fullness with nowhere to go, rising under the ribs and behind the eyes.) She counts, and it doesn't hold.
- **Nereth flares.** She goes down in the road, rigid. The scars burn and *move*, and Seralune sees it plainly this time.

**Turn:** the escort is gone up the road. Nereth is on the ground in the middle of a crowd that has just watched elves trample an old woman.

### Scene 3 — Found Out

**Immediate want:** get Nereth breathing and get her scarf back on.

**Pressure:** Osric kneels to help. He loosens her scarf to give her air, a kindness, and her ear comes out from under the cloth.

**Required developments:**

- Osric sees it. His face changes. "Elf."
- The crowd turns, fast and ugly, on a morning that already has a trampled old woman in the ditch. There's no speech about Natharul. People shout what was done to their own villages at an eighteen-year-old on her back in the dirt.
- Osric grabs Nereth by the collar and hauls her up. Someone pulls at Seralune's hood. Stones in hands.
- **Seralune's terror.** Her mana goes out of her the way it did in the aqueduct, through the nearest connection: Osric's hands on Nereth, where the corruption lives.
- **Osric dies.** Black-purple veins climb his wrists and arms. His Fire turns inward; it comes out of his mouth and eyes white-hearted, the same white as the aqueduct. He burns from the inside, still holding Nereth, until he isn't holding anything. Wren is three paces away and sees all of it. This is the Tobin bar: plain and heavy, with the camera on who watches.
- **Others hurt:** a woman with a Water affinity chokes on her own water; a man's Earth answers wrongly, and the road splits under his feet and breaks his leg. Nobody else dies.
- The crowd breaks, screaming: "Witch." "The Last Dark take her." A road priest holds up his sun disc against them, and his hands are shaking.

**Turn:** they're free to go, because the people are afraid of them. Seralune has killed a man who was kind to her, and his daughter watched her do it.

### Scene 4 — Flight

**Immediate want:** get away from the road before anyone finds their courage.

**Pressure:** Nereth can barely walk. The scars are hot to the touch and have climbed: new branches up her neck and down her left arm to the wrist.

**Required developments:**

- Seralune half-carries her through orchards and across a stream into the hills. Nobody follows. Behind them, Wren's crying goes on longer than Seralune can bear, and then it's gone.
- The pack and the map are with them. The medicine jar is safe: five drops, counted into the cup twice because her hands are shaking.
- **The story runs ahead (shown lightly):** from a hillside, they watch pilgrims hurrying towards Favale faster than pilgrims walk, and a rider from the road priest's party galloping ahead. Seralune understands the city will hear of an elf witch before she reaches it. **Proposal to confirm:** the pilgrims saw Nereth's ears, not Seralune's, so the story names the scarred one as the witch. Nereth carries the blame for what Seralune did.

**Turn:** they can't use the road. Favale is two days off, and it will be waiting for them.

### Scene 5 — The Rule

**Immediate want:** Seralune wants Nereth to live through the night.

**Pressure:** the scars flare again whenever Seralune's grief spikes, and ease when she forces herself to be still. She begins to see it before Nereth says a word.

**Required developments:**

- **Nereth's confession:** the ship, on the night Seralune remembered the young man; Meren, when the clerk wrote them down; today. "It happens when you're frightened. Or when you grieve." She hid it because Seralune had enough, and because she wanted to be wrong.
- **They test it, horribly simply.** Seralune thinks of Osric, the flare comes, and Nereth gasps. Seralune counts ("Count. One breath first."), and it eases. The rule is confirmed on the page.
- **Seralune understands:** the aqueduct, the white fire, Nereth's face, Osric. It came from her. It always did.
- **The flinch:** Seralune reaches out to touch the new scar on Nereth's neck, and Nereth flinches. It's small, instinctive, and over in a heartbeat. Nereth is ashamed of it at once ("I didn't mean —"), and Seralune takes her hand back and puts it in her lap.
- No speeches about forgiveness or monsters. Nereth doesn't leave. Seralune doesn't offer to let her.

**Ending image:** Seralune sits a little way off from the person she loves most in this age, with her hands in her lap, counting, so that she won't feel anything. She counts all night.

## Character Movement

### Seralune

- **Starts:** hopeful for the first time since Meren; enjoying the pilgrims.
- **Pressure:** Natharul's cruelty, her father's house on a carriage door, a crowd's hatred.
- **Involuntary act:** kills Osric.
- **Discovery:** her feelings feed Nereth's corruption, and her power corrupts whatever it touches.
- **Choice:** chooses to contain herself by counting. It's her brother's lesson, which is the same logic as the seal she escaped, and the reader should feel that irony without being told.
- **Ends:** afraid of what she is, and holding herself still to protect the one person she has.

### Nereth

- Tells the truth she has hidden since the ship.
- Worsens visibly and permanently: branches up her neck and down her left arm, and heat that no longer fully fades. Never fatal.
- Flinches, and is ashamed. She stays.

### Osric and Wren

- Osric is kind before he's cruel, and cruel because of what was done to his sister's village. He dies without ever knowing what Seralune is.
- Wren sees it. She's a Volume Two possibility (optional).

## Dialogue and Voice Notes

- Seralune's voice (Voice Standard): formal in public, and contractions and unfinished sentences when frightened. Her horror is things that are *almost right*: the royal tree on a carriage in a human road, and the word "Ruler".
- Nereth: dry, practical and honest when asked directly. The confession is short and plain.
- Pilgrims: earthy Mydean speech, "By the Four", "Four preserve us", "The Last Dark take her". Osric's grief comes out as bluntness, never a speech.
- The mob doesn't make arguments. It makes accusations: villages, children, names.
- No one says "monster".

---

## Magic and Mana Guardrails

- Seralune can't cast. What happens is her mana escaping through connections (touch, Nereth's corruption) and corrupting the affinities it reaches. Its results are inconsistent and harmful (Joshua's lore), never controlled.
- **Osric's death:** his own Fire, turned inward, carrying the black-purple and white signature of the corruption. Seralune doesn't aim it.
- **The other injuries** use the victims' own affinities (Water, Earth). One death.
- **Nereth:** her corruption flares with Seralune's surges and eases with Seralune's calm (the rule). No new fire from Nereth.
- **The Alaric connection:** no direction to the surge this time, and no Alaric content. The only clean connection stays reserved.
- Counting is Seralune's motif. It works a little, and it costs her.
- No THOOM (Alaric only).

## Final Chapter Shape

**A kind man lights the pilgrims' fire with his hand, and for one evening Seralune belongs among them. She hears of Atera, the living saint and Ruler of Light, and of the village of Harl's Ford, where the man's sister lived. In the morning Natharul riders bearing her father's tree ride through the pilgrims and trample an old woman. Seralune's fear floods out of her, and Nereth falls in the road with her scars moving. The kind man loosens her scarf to help her, sees her ear, and the crowd turns. He drags Nereth up, and Seralune's terror goes into him through his hands: his own Fire burns him from the inside while his daughter watches. They flee the road as the story of an elf witch races ahead to Favale. That night Nereth confesses the flares she has hidden since the ship, and together they find the rule: Seralune's feelings feed the corruption. When Seralune reaches out, Nereth flinches, and Seralune sits apart and counts until morning.**
